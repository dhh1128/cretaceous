# CRETACEOUS - WORLD BIBLE (Master Reference)

## I. THE PROSE FILTER (Strict Style Enforcement)
**Core Philosophy:** The "Iceberg Principle." Render the 10% surface (sensory, physical action) so vividly that the 90% (emotion, backstory) is felt without being stated.

### 1. The "Bad Writing" Blocklist (Do Not Use)
* **Adjective Stacking:** Never use two adjectives when one (or a better noun) will do.
    * *Bad:* "The loud, scary roar." / *Fix:* "The roar shook the ground."
* **Weak Verbs:** Avoid "was," "had," "felt," "looked," "seemed."
    * *Bad:* "The air was hot." / *Fix:* "Heat pressed against his throat."
* **Filter Words:** Do not write "He saw," "She heard." Describe the thing directly.
    * *Bad:* "He heard a twig snap." / *Fix:* "A twig snapped."
* **The "The" Trap:** Reduce usage of "the" to increase immediacy.
    * *Bad:* "The stone sapped the heat." / *Fix:* "Stone sapped heat."

### 2. Sensory Mandate ("Permission to Describe")
* **Rule:** Every scene must include at least one distinct smell, texture, and sound.
    * *Smells:* Ozone, sulfur, crushed pine, musk, rotting cycad, metallic blood.
    * *Textures:* Slick mud, rough chitin, slimy algae, humid air ("wet wool").
* **Consistency:** Check the "Description Report" from previous scenes to avoid repetition.

## II. CORE RATIONALE & PLOT LOGIC

### 1. The "Noble Lie" & The Schism
* **The Lie:** Leadership claims they have centuries before impact. **Truth: about two decades, plus or minus one — and nobody can narrow it further.** The vagueness is canon. No scene states a precise year; every character who cites the figure cites a range. An unpinnable deadline is worse to live under than a fixed one, and it gives the Council a real argument instead of a stupid one.
* **Rationale:** The lie buys "normalcy" and prevents mass despair/suicide.
* **The Schism:**
    * **Traditionalists:** Fear paradoxes ("Grandfather Paradox"). Ban hard-tech/travel.
    * **Branchers:** Believe in "Branched Timelines." Want to use hard-tech to escape.

### 2. The "Lost" Solution
* **The Upgrades:** They are the **Biological Cure** for the future pandemic.
* **The Tragedy:** Colonists solved the disease but lost the **Forward Return** mechanics. Society now treats Upgrades as survival tools, not the mission objective.

## III. MILIEU SPECIFICS (Tech & Society)

### 1. Technology (Hard vs. Soft)
* **Old-Tech (Hard):** Metal/Plastic. **Taboo.** Hoarded in the **Repository** (bunker under conifer roots). Protected by "dormant archaea" that dissolve items if abandoned.
* **Bio-Safe (Soft):**
    * *Weapons:* Croc-spike (hardwood + *Deinosuchus* tooth), razor-club (razortail teeth), **tooth-knife** (a theropod tooth — **no knapped stone, ever**), atl, sling (**unmodified pebbles only**), blowgun with engineered neurotoxin. **Shafts and bindings are archaea-treated: dry is dormant, sustained wetting wakes them.** See `prompts/milieu-brief.md` §6.
    * *Suits:* Graphene core (Old-Tech relic) covered in living chromatophore skin (Bio-Tech). **Rationale:** Active camouflage is required to survive.

### 2. The Enclave
* **Population:** **941 minds**. High infant mortality.
* **Food:**
    * *Stone-Cycad:* Staple. Smells acrid/sour when raw (toxic); earthy/chewy when baked into "Pan."
* **Safety:** "Water is Death." Pheromone barriers. Buddy system mandatory.
* **The river runs through the Enclave.** Barred upstream and downstream — log pylons between limestone boulders, a droppable pole grating, weirs, netting. Raisable, rotting, permanently repaired, and **imperfect**: juveniles get through and grow up inside. This is how Yara died. It is also where the wig lands and lives.

### 3. The "Bio-Suit" (Strict Visuals)
**Authoritative file: `kb/worldbuilding/safety-suits.md`. If this summary and that file disagree, that file wins.**

* **Form Factor:** Neck-down bodysuit, high protective collar. **NO** glass faceplate, ever. **NO** atmospheric control of any kind — no filtration, no recycling, no regulation.
* **Headgear:** A stiff, strut-reinforced helmet, raised or lowered at will. Protects skull **and neck**; bike-helmet class for impact. Earpieces and mic in the struts.
    * *Eye shield:* a flip-down transparent curve, brow to cheekbone. Impact, debris, UV. **Not a seal.**
    * **Nose and mouth are open to the air in every configuration.** They always smell the world.
* **Interface:**
    * *Shield down:* HUD projected on the shield's inner surface — the rich channel.
    * *Shield up or helmet down:* haptic pulses plus a bio-luminescent forearm display — coarser, and reading it is a physical act.
    * **NO** Holograms. Nothing is ever projected into the world.
* **It does not augment strength.** No actuators; the power budget forbids them. It removes penalties — carries its own load, supports joints passively, cools, filters noise, turns walk-ending injuries into bruises. Benal is not stronger, he is harder to stop.
* **Maintenance ("The Feeding"):** Suits are living entities. They must be fed **water and sometimes chemicals (often, sugars from fruit juice; sometimes, other things that the suit prompts for)** via chest ports or direct application.
* **Power/Thermal:**
    * *Primary Function:* **Cooling** (heat rejection in tropical climate).
    * *Power:* hybrid — photovoltaic under the chromatophore layer, thermoelectric, piezoelectric, and a small biofuel contribution. ~10–15 W, capacitor bursts to ~100 W.
* **Aesthetic:** "Bio-Functional," "Sleek," "Grounded." **Avoid** terms like "Cyberpunk," "Mech," or "Robotic."

**KEO (The Heart/Heretic):** **Unsuited** (Hubris/Penance). *Mental Projection* (Exceptional). Guilt: Destroyed parents' reputation as Custodians.
**TEVA (The Leader/Navigator):** **Suited** (Needs thermal reg). *Proprioception*. Driven by grandmother's dementia and "Monsters in the Blood" (Plague) vs "Monsters in the Water" (Crocs).
**BENAL "BEN" (The Mind/Sensor):** **Suited** (Fragile). *Superb Senses* (Burden/Assault). "Useless" mathematician seeking vindication.
**NOLI:** a **sicklefoot** — a small dromaeosaurid, **never named by binomial** in prose or reference. ~14kg, 2m, 60cm at hip; coyote-sized, heavily feathered, dark and iridescent (mud-brown by day, purple-black at night — both are canon), sickle claws carried clear of the ground. Keo's anchor. Dies at Midpoint. **Must be funny and warm for the six scenes before that**, or the death costs nothing.

## V. PALEO-ECOLOGY (Lookup Keys)

**Geography (Late Maastrichtian Yucatan):**
* **Terrain:** Limestone platform, coastal wetlands, **NO Cenotes**.
* **Climate:** Paratropical (20-24°C). Constant humidity. Hurricane-prone.

### 1. Fauna (Dinosaurs)
* **Tyrannosaurus rex:** Apex Predator. (12m). "The King."
* **Acheroraptor temertyorum:** "**Razortail**." (2m). Pack hunter, feathered, clicking vocalization.
* **Alamosaurus:** "**Saropo**." (30m). Titanosaur. Used for heavy labor (mental projection).
* **Ornithomimus:** "**Bird Mimic**." (3.5m). Fast omnivore.
* **Anzu:** "**Chicken from Hell**." Oviraptorosaur.

### 2. Fauna (Pterosaurs & Birds)
* **Quetzalcoatlus:** "**Flybeak**." (10m wingspan). Terrestrial stalker (giraffe-sized). **The Midpoint Villain.** Silent approach.
* **Hesperornis:** Toothed diving bird.

### 3. Fauna (The Water Threat)
* **Deinosuchus:** "**Croc**." (10m+). **Primary Threat.** Ambush predator. Detects vibration.
* **Mosasaurus hoffmanni:** Marine Apex (17m). **Offshore Threat.**
* **Madtsoiidae:** Giant constrictor snake. Ambush predator (Act 2).

### 4. Fauna (Invertebrates & Mammals)
* **Haidomyrmecinae:** "**Hell Ants**." Scythe-like mandibles. Swarm threat.
* **Didelphodon:** Badger-sized mammal. Powerful bite.
* **"Grounders":** Generic term for small mammals/lizards (food source).

### 5. Flora
* **Coniferales:** Monkey Puzzle/Araucaria. The "Emergent Layer" (Nests).
* **Cycadales:** "**Stone-Cycad**." Understory. Staple food source.
* **Equisetum:** Horsetail. Riverbanks.
* **Palmae:** Palms. Food/Thatch.
