# CRETACEOUS - ACT 2 CONTEXT (The Mudwalk)

## 0. PRECEDING MAP (Act 1, low resolution)

* **Beats 1-4: The Failing World & The Theft.** The crisis is established by Alira's death in the Vitarium. The trio forms an alliance built on shared conviction and personal flaws — Keo's **Hubris**, Teva's **Rage**, Benal's **Shame**. The act ends with the **Theft** of two suits and their escape into the perimeter fog, straight into a razortail threat.

* **Chekhov's guns established, and still loaded:** Noli's dependency on Keo · the suits' potential to fail · the "Noble Lie" framed as protection rather than deceit · Benal's math dismissed as useless · the hard-tech taboo, confirmed by Keo choosing to leave the metal weapons on the shelf · Keo's dead sister, and what she made of his parents · his father's eyes holding nothing but fear (`content/superseded/03.1.md:70`).

## I. BEAT-BY-BEAT B-STORY ESCALATION

The primary function of Act 2 is to move the trio from functional **Allies** (united by the theft/mission) to a **Chosen Family** (united by shared vulnerability, compassion, and shame).

### BEAT 5: Immediate Fallout (Tactical Victory)
* **5.1 Scene Outcome:** Tactical success against the *Acheroraptor* pack. Benal's intellectual skills (sensor analysis/strategy) are **validated** and are recognized as the key to survival. Keo's mental projection is the necessary tool for executing the diversion (via the **Grounder**).
* **B-Story Focus:** The relationship shifts from simple **alliance** to **nascent respect/bond**. Keo's **Hubris** and Benal's **Shame** are active but temporarily deferred by the win.

---

### BEAT 6: Fun & Games (The Cost of Hubris & Frailty)
* **Escalation:** The environment reveals the trio's hidden vulnerabilities and flaws.
* **6.1/6.2 (River Crossing):** Teva's **Proprioception/Magnetic Sense** is established as the key navigation skill. Her **thermal vulnerability** (suit struggling with thermal load) is internally noted as a liability she must hide. This contrasts with Keo's unsuited bravado.
* **6.3/6.4 (Deep Jungle):** Benal's **Superb Senses** become an **"unfiltered assault,"** leading to **Sensory Burden/Isolation**. His **paralyzing fear** during the *Madtsoiidae* (constrictor) attack is a pure physical failure, deepening his **Debt/Dependence** on Keo/Teva's physical skills.
* **6.5/6.6 (Sunfever Trek):** **Hubris Climax**. Keo pushes the pace using his **Thermal Regulation** upgrade, driven by his desire to prove himself to Teva. This forces Teva to hide her own **Weakness** (suit failure/dizziness).

---

### BEAT 7: Midpoint (The Shattering Loss)
* **7.2 (Noli's Death):** The *Quetzalcoatlus* attack is **sudden, shocking, and vertical**. Keo's **Mental Projection** is utterly useless, shattering his **Hubris** and bravado.
* **B-Story Focus:** The bond solidifies through **Shared Grief** and compassion (Teva and Ben providing silent, physical support).

---

### BEAT 8: Bad Guys Close In (Consequence & Vulnerability)
* **Escalation:** The direct, physical consequences of the B-Story flaws.
* **8.2 (Keo's Injury):** The **Hubris Crash**. Keo's fall and injury (on **limestone scree**) is the **direct consequence** of his **unsuited state** and unresolved **Grief/Rage**.
* **8.3 (Caretaking):** Teva's **Caretaker Climax**. She tends to Keo's wound, unshelled since her suit saturated in the Day 7 heat and running on nothing, developing the **Romance Subtext** through action. **The rain is warm** — what is failing is her, not the weather.
* **8.4 (Shame/Gratitude):** Keo's feverish state and **vulnerability** force him to rely on Benal's silent watchfulness, generating a wave of **gratitude** that overcomes his arrogance.
* **Temporal Flash (First):** Keo experiences the first **Temporal Flash** (the symbol, the smell of ozone/ocean). He dismisses it as a fever dream.

---

### BEAT 9: All Is Lost (Ultimate Defeat)
* **Escalation:** The environment strips the trio of their final intellectual and physical resources.
* **9.1 (The Coast):** Benal's **Superb Senses** are momentarily a benefit, spotting the **sea turtles' 'frenzied distraction'** to navigate the croc-infested mudflats.
* **9.3 (Mosasaur Attack):** **Intellectual Loss**. Benal is plunged into his ultimate nightmare (chaos/drowning). His fieldpack (containing his **theoretical work/math**) is lost to the sea, symbolizing the loss of his **Vindication** arc.
* **9.4 (Washing Up):** **Ultimate Defeat**. The collective belief that they have failed their mission and sacrificed their families for nothing.

---

### BEAT 10: Dark Night of the Soul
* **B-Story Climax: Chosen Family Forged**. The trio's mutual, honest confessions of **Guilt** (Keo), **Shame** (Benal's pity), and **Despair** (Teva’s grandmother). Teva provides the emotional **Bridge Speech**, transforming them into a **Chosen Family** unit.
* **Temporal Flashes (Overwhelming):** The flashes become overwhelming and specific, revealing the ancestors' **grief, terror, and paralysis**. Benal sees a corrupted fragment of the **"second jump" equation**.
