# Description reports

Cumulative register of what the canon scenes have already described, and how. Two jobs, from `writing-style-v2.md`:

1. **Consistency.** Cycad mash cannot smell both acrid and yeasty. If a thing has been described, describe it the same way or not at all.
2. **Freshness.** Don't redescribe the same striking detail scene after scene, and don't reuse the words.

Covers `content/superseded/01.1.md`–`content/superseded/04.3.md`, the ten canon scenes, 10,579 words. **Update this file whenever a scene is accepted.** The redraft of Act 2 onward must read it first.

## 1. Milieu facts already fixed — match these or stay silent

**The kiva and Vitarium.** Limestone. Cold underfoot — *established, so never say so again; it is the nature of the stone.* Smells of "clean, mineral damp," which is the smell of safety to Teva. The Vitarium is a natural cleft off the main kiva. Failing cultures smell of **autolysis** — "sweet, denatured-protein decay" that clings to the back of the throat. Healthy cultures have a "faint, yeasty perfume." Vessels are lopsided clay, each mimicking a random clump so nothing looks made. Light comes from cultivated mycelial lattices; the Council chamber's reads **blue**. Culture diagnostics: **rust-red** is T-cell activation, **blue** is lysine/digestive. Dying light was rendered as "a guttering yellow. A murky, apoptotic blue" — *that phrasing is spent.*

**The Council chamber** is a shallow recess off the kiva with a raised stone shelf for a dais. **The walls are bare — no marks, no graffiti, no history, forbidden as a paradox precaution.** "A monument to erasure." Acoustics carry a whisper to every corner and swallow shouting. Entrance is draped with a ceratops hide.

**The village.** Nests thirty meters up in monkey-puzzle conifers, reached by notched rope ladders. Fern-fiber sleeping mats, fern-screen dividers, woven vine walls. The border-tangle is a fifteen-meter wall of woven vine, felled logs and living trees.

**The river, and it runs through the Enclave.** *(Unspent in the prose so far — no scene has yet described it, which makes it available.)* Barred at the upstream and downstream boundaries where large limestone boulders narrow the channel: log pylons driven into the alluvial bed, a grating of smaller poles dropped from above into slots between them, weirs of piled unmodified boulders making shallow rapids, then netting. Raisable, because a sealed river silts and clogs. Rotting continuously and under permanent repair. **And imperfect** — a grating that stops a ten-meter croc passes a juvenile, which grows up inside. See `milieu-brief.md` §4.

**The Repository.** Dug between the buttressed roots of an ancient conifer and limestone boulders. Entrance is an unworked limestone slab on air-bladder counterweights with a hidden release grip — it *floats* back, hissing, and does not scrape. Lit yellow-green by ceiling lightmold. Air is "limestone-damp and heavy with the sterile, electric tang of old-tech — ozone, polymer, and dormant circuits." Racks of lashed branches, nearly all empty. Taboo items smell of **soured figs** (the archaea wrappings).

**The pheromone barrier.** Weeps as "viscous amber" from ceramic nodules. Smells of wounded alpha and carrion; sits in the throat as a "sick-sweet chemical cloy." Terrifies Noli outright.

**Food.** Journey-pan is a dense, dark-crusted flatbread. Crawlers are roasted and salted, carried in a greasy pouch. *Raw* cycad mash is acrid — that is unprocessed pulp and it signals desperation.

**Suits.** Look like they should weigh twenty kilos and weigh three. Step-in, sealed with a tab that hisses. Chromatophores wake on sealing and were rendered once as "charcoal, lichen, and fog-white" — *spent.* They filter the insect wall, which is what lets Benal think.

**Bodies and senses.** Proximity sense is "a tingling pressure," "his mind's hand tracing the shape of the space"; sleeping minds are "warm, banked coals." Projection costs pressure behind the eyes, then a "leaden weight." Razortail vocalization is "a bone-dry click. Like a river stone struck against bone."
