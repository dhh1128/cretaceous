*the Watch ~ Teva, Day 19*

<!-- epigraph: EP13 -->
> **[MSG · C.ARROYO → M.ARROYO · NO ROUTE · NOT SENT · T−3 d]**
>
> Mari —
>
> They told me this morning and I am writing it down before I can be sensible about it.
>
> You have been dead for nine days. I have been writing to you for six of them, which I am aware is not a thing a rational person does, and I have decided that I do not care, and that this is the one place I am permitted not to care.
>
> Here is what I want you to know, and there is nobody else I can say it to plainly, because everyone else has an opinion about morale.
>
> We are not burying it. That is the part everyone gets wrong when they talk about this — they think we are leaving something behind for someone to dig up. We are not. **We are carrying it, and we are bringing it back ourselves.** That is the whole of it. Go, do the work, come home, and hand it to whoever is still standing. Everything else is a detail about how.
>
> I do not know whether it will work. I know that it is not a message in a bottle and I am tired of people talking about it as though it is.
>
> I am taking your coat.
>
> — Cece
