# Back-cover blurb

Desperate for time to cure a pandemic, scientists jump into the distant past, to the one place and epoch where they're sure they won't leave a trace: ground zero, shortly before the asteroid that will end the dinosaurs. They mean to return with a cure. But a way back never materializes, two centuries pass, and the mission drifts into legend. When a child dies because the colony has forgotten how to keep itself alive, Keo, Teva and Benal steal forbidden founder tech and set out to walk eighty kilometers <!-- @WF-route.overland: 80km --> through jungle and swamp to the place where their ancestors arrived. The Cretaceous wants them dead. The truth waiting for them is worse than a lie. What did the founders leave behind? And how long before the sky falls?
