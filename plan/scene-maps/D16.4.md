## MOVES

1. **[opens] [relation]** Dusk in the archive *(D16.3 move 11)*. Keo said they could not carry it all, and that was his whole share of the afternoon. He says he will have a look round. Benal, at the next door, hardly hears him. Teva says, without looking up from the plates, not near the water, and he says not near the water. *(`plan/scene-list.md` D16.4: he is here because he has nothing to do.)*
2. **[world]** He drinks at the crown hollows in the last of the light. Past the end of the banked row, over a cut in the rock, the tall fin they all saw from the brow this morning stands against the evening *(`[requires D16.1 — from the brow, past the last banked shape, a tall fin of metal standing up at the far end, which nobody can name]`)*. He goes along the crown to it at the ankle's pace, the hum falling behind him.
3. **[world]** Round the crown's shoulder, the hard: a poured floor on a shelf of the slope, a ramp from its edge into the lagoon. On it a boat with wings, and the fin is its tail. Off the ramp's foot, a second one sits level in the shallows, its back and tail out of the water, crusted white, and the still air brings up the smell of it, salt, weed and wet metal. He keeps back from the ramp's edge and does not go down to it. *(Two hulls, neither flyable: `plan/scene-list.md` D16.4.)*
4. **[world]** The dry hull close to: stubby wings, round empty mounts along the wing roots and the back with nothing seated in them, a cable from the banked box behind it into a port on its flank. A step, a door; inside, four seats behind a windscreen and a dark panel. He sits in the front seat, and it gives under him all the way through.
5. **[turn — KEO]** Out of habit he reaches with his proximity sense, the way he reached for Noli, and there is nothing alive in the hull. He says something aloud to nobody, as he has taken to *(`[requires D16.2 — Keo alone with empty hands in the dry room, saying something aloud to Noli, who is not there, and nothing answering]`)*. The panel lights, and a voice answers him in the founders' plain words, and asks him to identify himself as pilot.
6. **[relation]** He gives his name. It asks for his rating; he does not know the word. It says it will log him as an unqualified operator, maintenance access only, and that a qualified pilot is preferred *(`kb/worldbuilding/the-wig.md` §5)*. He asks what it is: the craft's maintenance and flight-training system. He asks whether it can hear what he is thinking; it says it does not understand the question. It is the first thing in two days that has answered him about itself, and he notices it does not ask anything back.
7. **[spine]** He asks whether it can fly. It reports, as equipment: this craft cannot fly; the main cell bank is removed for storage, and the reserve with it `[-]`; the surface power membrane is not responding; lift rotors, fitted. Its only power is the station feed through the charge lead. He tells it there is nothing in the round mounts. It says it has no record of their removal, and logs a sensor fault. He asks about the one in the water; it reports the second craft as moored. He tells it that one has sunk, and it logs that too. *(Ledger L3; `[plants → D18.2 — the rotor units are not on the dry hull, and the training system's records say they are]`.)*
8. **[spine]** **The need, before any work.** It tells him what flying needs: the cells, stored dry in the store beside the craft; the electrolyte, sealed in its drums; the cells carried to the bay dry, filled there, linked in series, and charged from the station. He asks how long. It gives a figure from its specifications, about a day, then says it is measuring the supply, and revises: the station's generators `[?]` *(`plan/milieu-allocation.md` §5b: the AI says "generators" to Keo)* are at about half their rating, and a full charge will take two to three days *(ledger L3)*. He asks how far a full charge goes. About a hundred and twenty kilometers <!-- @WF-wig.range.claimed: 120km -->, from the cells' rating; it gives the figure as it gave the first charge time, from its specifications, and this one it does not revise *(ledger L3)*. He says that is farther than home, and it does not know where home is.
9. **[world]** The store's end door is sealed like the hatch, with the same covered handle beside it, and this time he pulls it himself without asking anyone *(`[requires D16.1 — the hatch's covered manual release, which Keo found and pulled when Benal told him to]`)*. Lit inside: racks of gray boxes, seventy-odd; the row of drums; long sealed crates along the back wall, which he looks at and leaves, and which the voice says nothing about `[plants → D18.2 — the crates in the store hold the dry hull's rotor units, seen and not opened]`; a locker with goggles and gloves.
10. **[body]** He carries the cells, dry, one at a time against his chest, from the rack across the few paces of hard to the bay, seventy-odd times. Once he calls them jars; the voice says *cells*, and he goes on thinking jars `[?]` *(`plan/milieu-allocation.md` §5b: Keo calls them jars once, the AI corrects him, and he goes on saying jars to the others, D17.3 and D18.2)*. The rib finds every lift and the ankle every step. The moon goes down in the west while he does it; the bay's lights come on when the voice says they will. **It is the first useful thing his hands have done since Day 11** *(`plan/scene-list.md` D16.4)*.
11. **[body]** Filling. The gloves crack the first time he closes his hand, and he works without them; the goggles hold. He tips a drum into a jug and the jug through a funnel into each cell to the line the voice reads him. The electrolyte is slick between finger and thumb, and then it bites. The voice says to wash it off at once with plenty of water. The only water nearer than the crown hollows is the lagoon at the foot of the ramp, in the dark, and the hollows are what the three of them drink; he wipes his hands on his tunic and goes slower. *(Texture, `[-]`: stakes, his body and his culture's rule; `plan/milieu-brief.md` §3: water is death.)*
12. **[relation] [texture]** Through the hours he talks to it. About the cells first, then not: about Noli, how she ate, what she did with her head when she was pleased. It says it does not understand, and asks him to confirm the fill level of the cell in front of him. He tells it anyway. It does not know some of his words, and he learns to say things the long way, the founders' way, so that it will follow `[-]` `[plants → D19.4 — Keo has learned that the voice can be wrong and how to talk so that it follows him, which is what he weighs when it tells him the river channel is the only water that keeps a working wig]`. Once he thanks it, and it does not answer, because there is nothing in it that takes thanks. *(Hazard: its warmth is in his need, not in its design.)*
13. **[spine]** Linking: it reads him each link, the plus of one cell to the minus of the next, a row of nuts in the work light, and has him check each one back to it. It tells him to leave the bay open on charge, because of the gas. Toward the middle of the night the last link is on, and it tells him to close the isolator.
14. **[spine] [turn — KEO]** He closes it. On the panel the charge indicator does not move; the voice says the first hours only form the cells after their storage, and the figure will not climb tonight. It is running. He knows what the Council would call this, and that nobody voted, and he did it alone *(`plan/scene-list.md` D16.4: his first act of open heresy, alone, before anyone can vote)*.
15. **[closes] [relation]** He walks back along the crown in the dark by the sound of the hum to the hatch. The others are asleep in the dry room. Teva wakes as he lies down and asks where he was, and he says looking round, and she is asleep again before he could have said more. His hands burn. It is not anything yet, and he will tell them when it is. *Ending **RESOLVE*** *(ledger L4)*.

He knows a full charge takes two to three days and, by the voice's figure, reaches about 120 km <!-- @WF-wig.range.claimed: 120km -->, farther than home. His hands are burned and his rib and ankle are worse for the night. For the first time on the journey he has kept something from the other two, and it was Teva he said *looking round* to.
