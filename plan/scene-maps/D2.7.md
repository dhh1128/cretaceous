## MOVES

1. **[opens]** **The first person out pushes the hide back, and the room's air comes out ahead of the people** — wet hair, wet skin, breath, too many bodies in a closed room for too long; the hide had been holding it in. — ***L1: Benal's smell, spent here.*** *Under it the noise of a room getting up, every voice at once and none of them sorted. **It is a fact of being him and he thinks of it as one**, the way D2.2 has him think of the treading.* **Two or three sentences.**

2. **[ledger]** **What he got of it.** — *He came down late from the basin, and from out here the session was noise with his father's voice in it, loud and then louder, and then not there. **That is the whole of what he knows**, and it is enough for him to have decided what happened.* **Two sentences.**

3. **[spine]** **People come out past him and do not stop.** — *Quiet, tired, wet. **One or two of them look at him the way people look at Marek's son**, and he reads it as pity, which is his reflex; tonight it is something closer to sympathy for the father, and he cannot tell the difference.* **Two or three sentences.**

4. **[spine]** **Marek and Lunet come out late, after most of the room — people had stopped him on the way — her hand on his arm.** — ***FO at full pressure.*** *What the reader sees through Benal: a man spent, his voice gone, not looking at anyone. **What Benal sees is a man who made a scene and lost.** The narration gives the face and the posture and nothing else.* **Two or three sentences.**

5. **[turn — BENAL]** **And he thinks *do something real*.** — *`D2.2.md` move 10's line, coming back to him, and with it a small, mean satisfaction he is ashamed of before it has finished arriving: **so this is what doing something real gets you.** That is the wrong reading, and it is the one he believes. `[pays ← D2.2 — "Do something real"]`* **One or two sentences.**

6. **[turn — MAREK]** **Marek sees him and stops, and talks — hoarse, kind, and about Benal's body.** — ***L2.*** *Marek's weapon is pity and his register is public even at home *(`minor-characters.md`; `D2.6.md` WANTS)*, so he does not go silent: he says too much of the wrong thing. **What he says is fear for a fragile child, and it comes out as pity**; the reader has just watched him argue for sending a party because of exactly that fear, and Benal hears only that his father thinks he will break.* **Two or three lines.**

7. **[closes]** **Lunet's line, and then they go, and Benal does not follow them.** — ***Ending HELD*** *(`scene-list.md`, `[?]`)*. *`[-]` **Lunet says the true thing plainly** — that his father argued for him in there — **and Benal does not believe her**, because it is what a mother says.
