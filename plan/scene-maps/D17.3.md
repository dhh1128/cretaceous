## MOVES

1. **[opens] [body]** Early afternoon, back in the dry rooms. Nobody sits. Teva has the register against her chest and the moon table in her head.
2. **[spine]** Teva says it: they lied to us. *(`plan/scene-list.md` D17.3: her reaction is *they lied to us*.)*
3. **[relation]** Keo agrees at once, and goes further: the perimeter, the suits on the rack, every time his parents said *not yet*. They kept him in for a door that was never going to open. *(Keo→Teva 7; FN, which the reader holds and he does not.)* `[requires D2.3 — Riel refusing Keo a suit in the same words as always]`
4. **[relation]** Benal tries to say it is not that. The sky has at most seventeen years <!-- @WF-impact.window.max: 17y -->; the cure is in every child at home; it has to go forward and nobody alive can take it. Teva hears the first half and answers the second with the case. *(Two against one; they fail to notice they are talking about different things.)*
5. **[spine] [turn — TEVA]** She marshals it, flat, in order, the longest she has spoken since Night 15: the wrapping, year three, at home, and the Council calls it founder law; the centuries the Council teaches, when the founders knew better and left it on a tablet here; and the Mission home, two hundred years of waiting for a road nobody born here could walk. *(`plan/voice-sheets.md`, Teva: the marshaling register.)*
6. **[turn — TEVA]** And Omya, at the Watch every clear night for sixty years <!-- @WF-omya.watch.years: 60 -->, guarding a protocol for a journey home that was never hers to make — and she stops there. She cannot finish the count. *(`plan/character-arcs.md` §3, D17.3: she gets as far as Omya and stops, because it is unbearable.)*
7. **[relation]** Benal asks her the question she taught him to ask: what would she tell them. *(`plan/voice-sheets.md`: interrogation is how she leads; he has learned it from her.)* She does not answer it yet.
8. Benal says they did not lie; he thinks they did not know. The founders had the old number; the ones who stayed found the new one and took it with them, further back; nobody here ever had it. He says *I think* and *possibly*, and the hedge takes the weight out of it, and neither of them hears it. *(`plan/voice-sheets.md`, Benal: the hedge measures certainty; here it is back.)*
9. Keo says what difference does it make, if they were kept in either way, and Teva agrees with him. Benal stops trying. *(Benal alone with the impossible mission; the accusation stands unrefuted, `plan/scene-list.md` D17.3 hazard.)*
10. **[relation]** Keo tells them they have to go up to the water before the light goes, which is how he says he is on her side *(`plan/voice-sheets.md`, Keo: practical care, telling you to drink)*. Nobody moves yet.
11. **[body]** Teva opens the register at Cecilia's leaf. The face that wrote *what returns is the platform*, and the woman at home who speaks for her every session and has never read a word of her. *(T19; D17.1, D17.2 move 9.)*
12. **[closes]** She decides what she will say to the Council: the case, in order, the wrapping and the centuries and the road that was never there, and she leaves Omya out of it. It is the wrong speech, and she will not give it *(`plan/scene-maps/D19.7.md`, `D19.8.md`: four words instead)*. *Ending **RESOLVE***.

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** Teva has called it a lie and built the case she means to put to the Council, with Omya left out because she cannot say that part. Keo is on her side. Benal has said they did not know, hedged, and nobody heard it; he is alone with what has to happen next. They are going up to the water.

**Worked on and did not change.** The accusation, which stands unrefuted. Benal's mission, which nobody has taken up.

**Left open.** Whether she is right. How anyone gets the cure forward. How they get home at all.

**Refused outright.** An argument that resolves *(`plan/scene-list.md` D17.3 hazard)*: nobody wins, Benal is not proved right in the scene, and nobody says *drift*. Teva finishing the Omya count. Anyone naming the difference between their experiences. Keo talking about Yara *(`plan/voice-sheets.md`, Keo: mostly not thereafter)*. A speech to the reader.
