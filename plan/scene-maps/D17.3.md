## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** Teva has called it a lie and built the case she means to put to the Council, with Omya left out because she cannot say that part. Keo is on her side. Benal has said they did not know, hedged, and nobody heard it; he is alone with what has to happen next. Benal has made fire from the sun with the lab's glass, and they have eaten roasted crab, the first cooked food since Day 10. Keo has told them about the boat with wings and its charge, and neither of them took it seriously; he did not say it twice. They are at the water.

**Worked on and did not change.** The accusation, which stands unrefuted. Benal's mission, which nobody has taken up.

**Left open.** Whether she is right. How anyone gets the cure forward. How they get home at all.

**Refused outright.** An argument that resolves *(`plan/scene-list.md` D17.3 hazard)*: nobody wins, Benal is not proved right in the scene, and nobody says *drift*. Teva finishing the Omya count. Anyone naming the difference between their experiences. Keo talking about Yara *(`plan/voice-sheets.md`, Keo: mostly not thereafter)*. A speech to the reader. Benal naming the craft, which is D18.2's *(`plan/milieu-allocation.md` §5b)*, and anyone going round the crown to look at it *(D18.1 State closure)*. The fire as a set piece: it is lit in a sentence or two and the scene goes on.
