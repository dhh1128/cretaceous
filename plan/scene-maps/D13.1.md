- **`TIME`** — Day 13, from early in the morning march to the lay-up at about 1100. They left at first light, about 0530 `[-]`, after the dawn meal *(D12.2, state closure)*; she comes due about 0600, within the first kilometer. The day makes 10 km in all *(`journey-calendar.md` §1)*: about 5 km this morning, slowly, and 5 km after the lay-up `[-]`.

## MOVES

1. **[opens] [body]** Coming down off the limestone in the mist, Benal can feel the dawn meal sitting in him, and he is well.
2. **[body]** Teva stops, goes down on her knees, and vomits.
3. **[turn]** All three know what it is, and say so. Nobody suggests anything else; they have leached cycad all their lives. *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §4, assumption 4.)* `[requires D12.1.8 — Keo says it needs another day, and how]`
4. Benal says, or thinks and then says, that the same mash is in him and Keo, and that theirs had the night in it. Nobody can say whether that is enough.
5. **[spine]** She gets up and walks, and takes Keo's hand to do it.
6. **[body]** Cramp and purging; she has to stop, and stop again. Benal gives her water and counts the skins.
7. **[spine]** Her arm goes over Keo's shoulder. Benal says he can take her; Keo says he has her. Benal does not press it, and the reasons are his: the chest, the arm he has not told them about.
8. **[texture]** Watching her retch over Keo's shoulder, Benal remembers her sharing her lunch with him when they were small (B23).
9. **[spine]** When she cannot walk at all, Keo gets her onto his back, her head over his shoulder so she can be sick clear of him. Benal takes her pack and the fieldpack off her, and both spikes `[-]`. On Keo's back, Teva tells him he was right. He answers it without taking it up. `[requires D12.2.6 — he does not fight his way back in]`
10. **[world]** The mist burns off, the air warms, and the insect wall comes up. The midges come in columns at Keo's eyes and mouth and he cannot lift a hand to them; Benal clears them off his face as they walk. *(`milieu-allocation.md` §3b.)*
11. **[body] [ledger]** The noise comes into Benal with nothing to stop it, and the reader learns why: the capacitor in his shoulder died on Day 11, and with it the grid, the flash and the dampening. With Keo's hands under her legs he is the only one with free hands to defend them, and he has nothing to defend them with. He has told nobody, and he does not now. *(`body-and-resources.md` §3b.)*
12. **[relation]** Teva, from Keo's back, asks Benal how far. He has it all: kilometers to the lay-up and to the coast, the water, the hours of light, and when it will come for the two of them, which is around nightfall, by the same count as hers. For once somebody wanted the numbers. *(B30; Teva→Benal 7: her questions are where her warmth is, `voice-sheets.md`.)*
13. **[spine]** Benal walks where he can see her face, and says nothing about what he hears.
14. **[closes]** Keo's ankle turns and he goes down on one knee with her on his back. He gets up. *Ending **RESOLVE*** *(`plan/pacing-and-stakes.md` §8)*.
