Forward map per `process/methodology-theory.md` §4. Moves carry events and changes, not wording; how a line is said is the drafter's. The band is a floor for this map's material, never for the prose. **Phase 4 is a hard stop** — the ledger at the foot goes to Daniel before any prose exists.

## IN

- **`PLACE`** — Genesis by daylight, an island ringed by coral, low coral ground around a limestone crown twenty to thirty meters high <!-- @WF-genesis.crown.height: 20-30m --> *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3)*: too small and too porous for any fresh water to collect in the ground. **The water building** on the shore's landward edge, a ruin with nothing in it *(D15.2)*, on whose walkway they spent Night 15 *(D15.3–D15.4)*; the low ground between the shore and the crown, under the surge all of Day 15 and drained now to wet rock and standing pools of the surge's salt water `[-]`; the landing on this, the mainland side, its works gone under the water, and from it **the way up**, a track up the crown's gentle mainland-side slope and over its brow to the living arches on the top, bare alloy frames now, their skins long gone *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3)* `[-]`. On the crown, the highest ground and above any flood, the founders' lab modules, bolted to the rock and banked to the eaves with coral rubble and sand under their solar roofs, on hard rock whose hollows still hold the storm's rain *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3)*. **The crown block** is a run of those modules joined end to end and banked as one, its inner doors the modules' end doors `[-]`; from the way up it shows as a long bank of rubble with a roof on it, and no door. On Day 15 they could not reach the way up, which starts across the flooded ground they would not wade, and from the shore across the flood, in Day 15's gray light, the banked block showed only low pale edges above its rubble, with no door and no light `[-]` *(D15.2 move 1)*. **The hatch** is in the block's far end wall, which stands in a cut in the crown's rock, under the lip of the banking, out of the salt and the weather, which is why its panel and its seal have lasted; at ground level, a step up from the rock: a door through the wall, not up onto a roof and not down `[-]` *(ledger L1)*. Nothing here is underground.
- **`TIME`** — Day 16, from first light to mid-morning `[-]`.
- **`WEATHER`** — clear, still, washed, the first clear morning since the storm *(`journey-calendar.md` §2, Day 16)*. Warm and clean.
- **`LIGHT`** — hard sun off wet stone and the sea.
- **`SOUND`** — the surf on the reef, seabirds, water draining off the low ground; and, once the wind has gone and the water is off the ground, **a hum from the crown block that holds one pitch and never changes**, for as long as he listens: the power converters of the equipment still running inside, which have no moving parts and hum as transformers do; the generators themselves make no sound `[-]` *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3: the power survived, and what had moving pieces has failed; `kb/worldbuilding/the-second-jump.md` §1: a machine that still answers when spoken to)*. Every living sound he knows pulses or breaks; this does not. **It is not the sound of his flash on the coast**, which rose and fell, every rise the same to a hair *(`content/D14.1.md:113`)*; it shares only that evenness, which nothing alive has.
- **`SMELL`** — at the hatch's seal a thread of warm air comes out, the inside warmed by the generators' decay heat and the equipment *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3: the warmth of their own decay in the banked crown modules)*, and it smells of the air after lightning: ozone, which electrical machinery makes, and warm dust `[-]` *(`milieu-allocation.md` §5, Day 16: wet metal, ozone and dormant circuits)*.
- **`SENSES`** — `[-]` **Hearing, the hum:** one note held with no pulse in it, the same at every count he gives it, where everything alive he has ever heard pulses or breaks; it is the power converters *(`SOUND`)*, and to him it is a number that does not change. **Smell, ozone:** the air after lightning, in a thread at the hatch's seal, the smell from his flash on the coast, here and real *(`plan/milieu-allocation.md` §5: ozone, Day 16 — this scene's use of it; R26)*. **Touch, warm air:** the same thread on his wet face and hands, warmer than the morning, after a night of cold off wet skin; air that is warm with no sun and no fire in it. **Taste, the crown hollows:** rain that tastes of stone and nothing else, cleaner than the shore's hollows last night, which tasted of shell. **First encounters, owned here for Benal:** the hum; warm air breathing out of a seal; a lit panel with words on it. *(Keo's dry room, steady light and the hum are D16.2's; Benal's first quiet room is D16.3's.)*

The best of it is a run of hollows in the crown's rock, perhaps three days' worth for three people as the sun takes it `[-]`. And they came for the protocols, which are inside.

## SPINE

**The way in**, from the broken block to the hatch giving.

## MOVES

1. **[opens] [world]** First light, and the first clear morning since the storm. They come down the stair from the walkway and out at the water building's doorway. The wind has gone, and the surge has gone off the low ground, leaving wet rock and pools.
2. **[body]** The rain on the shore is gone from its hollows. Benal says what he said on the shore: this ground keeps nothing, and only the hard rock up on the crown might. They cross the drained low ground to the landing, on the rock, stepping round the pools, which are the sea's and no use to drink; nobody puts a foot in one, shallow and clear to the bottom as they are. At the landing they keep to the high side, back from the lagoon's edge, and go up the crown's gentle slope, bare to its brow, at Keo's pace on the ankle `[-]`. *(D15.1 move 9; `plan/milieu-brief.md` §3.)*
3. **[world]** Over the brow, in the clear light, Genesis is more than Day 15 could show them: on the crown's top the living arches stand as bare alloy frames, their skins long gone, a great many of them `[-]`, and beyond them long banked shapes with roofs on them, in a row. People lived here, a lot of them, for years. Keo asks whether they see it; Teva looks at it a long time; Benal starts counting the arches aloud, exactly, and keeps going `[-]`. Beyond the row, down past the crown's far shoulder, a tall fin of metal stands up out of the lagoon's shallows, crusted white; Keo asks what that is, and nobody knows *(`[plants → D16.4 — the tail of the wet hull, a wreck off the ramp's foot, which Keo walks to at dusk and past which he finds the hard, the store and the hangar]`; Q-CTEM)*. It lifts all three before anything is found. *(Q-PMHV, Daniel: the emotional uplift the next day when they see more of Genesis; D15.4 move 13.)*
4. **[body]** Hollows in the crown's rock still hold the storm's rain. They drink, and Benal looks along the run of them and says about three days, if the sun is kind. The smallest is already a finger below the dark line its water left on the rock overnight `[-]` *(texture: the clock, visible)*.
5. Teva looks at the long bank beside them with a roof on it: close to, it is buried, no door on any side she can see. She says the protocols will be wherever it is still dry, if anywhere is, and that the founders would not bury their own doors: the way in is somewhere along the row, and they start along it, Keo on her shoulder now (Daniel: everything is up, not down).
6. **[world]** Benal stops. Under the surf and the birds there is a sound that holds one pitch and does not change, and a smell like the air after lightning. The smell is the one from the coast, the thing that was not there; the sound is not the coast's, which rose and fell, but it is as even as that was, too even for anything alive, and for a moment he thinks it is happening again. But his skin does not prickle and the surf and the birds go on, and by that he knows the smell is really there: the real ozone comes without the flash's signature *(Q-A6KA)*. *(R26; `[requires D14.1 — Benal's first flash: a sound that repeats exactly, and the air after lightning]`)*
7. **[spine]** It does not stop, and it has a direction: the bank beside them. He tells them he can hear something in there and smell it, and Teva goes where he points.
8. Along the bank the sound grows toward its far end. Round it the rock drops into a cut, and in the cut, under the lip of the banking, the end wall stands clear, and in the wall a hatch; at its seal a thread of warm air comes out and smells of lightning. He says so, and only that. Keo says that is old-tech, and it is running.
9. **[spine]** Beside the hatch, a panel, lit. Teva says lit means power. Power means something has been running here for two hundred and six years <!-- @WF-transit.elapsed: 206y --> *(scene-list D16.1)*.
10. Teva presses the panel. Words come up, and she reads them out: it wants something of them that they do not have, and it does nothing.
11. Keo finds the covered handle beside it, and asks before he pulls it. Benal says pull it.
12. **[closes]** The seal breaks with a breath of air outward, and the air that comes out of the hatch is dry. *Ending **RESOLVE*** *(`plan/pacing-and-stakes.md` §8)*.

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** The hatch is open and the dry section is real. Benal's senses have got them in, for the third time, and what he took for a flash was really there. They have water for about three days, in the crown's hollows.

**Worked on and did not change.** Benal's capacitor, untold. Their hunger.

**Left open.** What is inside. Whether the water holds.

**Refused outright.** Benal solving it cleverly: he says what he hears and smells, and the others name it. The hatch as a puzzle. Anyone remarking that old-tech has not dissolved: that is D16.2's, where they talk about it (Q-QQDM).

## Event trace

- **Moves 2–3, the way up and the view.** They see only what the brow hid on Day 15, from where they stand on it in clear light; the fin is the wet hull's tail standing out of the shallows past the far shoulder, a salt-crusted wreck, which is what two centuries in the open leave; the dry hull is sealed in its banked hangar and nobody can see it. Nobody names the fin because nobody has seen a wig *(Q-CTEM)*. Stepping round the salt pools is this culture's habit with standing water, not a choice made here *(Q-PMHV; D15.4 move 13)*.
- **Move 4, the water.** Three days is Benal's estimate from the hollows' size and the sun, said as an estimate; the finger-width drop overnight is evaporation and seepage he can see. It sets the clock D18.1 reads at under a day *(`body-and-resources.md` §3)*.
- **Moves 6–8, the hum and the smell.** Benal's hearing and smell are upgraded and the wind has dropped, so a steady hum through the banked block and a thread of warm air at the end-wall seal reach him before they reach the others; both grow toward the cut, which is how they get a direction. He tells the real smell from his flash by the absence of the flash's prickle *(Q-A6KA; `plan/tech-rules.md`)*.
- **Moves 9–10, the panel.** All three read; "old-tech" and "lightcell" are their words, so *lit means power* is in reach, and 206 years is the colony's own count *(`kb/worldbuilding/lingo.md`)*.
- **Move 12, the seal.** Air inside warmed by the generators and equipment is warmer than the morning, so it breathes outward when the seal breaks, and it is dry because the block is sealed and banked *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3)*.
