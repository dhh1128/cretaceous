# D16.1 — the hatch — BENAL (close third)  ·  MEDIUM, ~1,200 words

Forward map per `process/methodology-theory.md` §4. Moves carry events and changes, not wording; how a line is said is the drafter's. The band is a floor for this map's material, never for the prose. **Phase 4 is a hard stop** — the ledger at the foot goes to Daniel before any prose exists.

Purpose **DISCOVER**, ending **RESOLVE**. Ladders EM7 P6 S6 SP7. Moderate stakes: thirst, hunger and a station they cannot get into; nothing attacks. Drafted script-first: moves 3, 6, 7 and 9 are carried by talk; the rest by the morning, the ground and Benal's senses.

## IN

- **`PLACE`** — Genesis by daylight, a spit of emergent land at about sea level ringed by coral *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3)*: too small and too low for any fresh water to collect in the ground. The broken block where they spent Night 15 *(D15.4)*; the dead seaward block whose doors would not open *(D15.2)*; the low ground between the blocks, under the surge all of Day 15 and drained now to wet rock and standing pools `[-]`; and on the spit's crown, the highest ground and above the flood line, **a long low block with its walls whole** `[-]`. On Day 15 they saw it only across the flooded ground, which they would not wade, and its face toward them has no door `[-]`. Nothing here is underground.
- **`TIME`** — Day 16, from first light to mid-morning `[-]`.
- **`WEATHER`** — clear, still, washed, the first clear morning since the storm *(`journey-calendar.md` §2, Day 16)*. Hot by mid-morning.
- **`LIGHT`** — hard sun off wet stone and the sea.
- **`SOUND`** — the surf on the reef, seabirds, water draining off the low ground; and, once the wind has gone and the water is off the ground, **a hum from the crown block that holds one pitch and never changes**, for as long as he listens: the fans and power units of the equipment still running inside *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3: the power survived; `kb/worldbuilding/the-second-jump.md` §1: a machine that still answers when spoken to)*. Every living sound he knows pulses or breaks; this does not.
- **`SMELL`** — a grille high in the crown block's wall breathes out warm air, and it smells of the air after lightning: ozone, which electrical machinery makes, and hot dust off the filters `[-]` *(`milieu-allocation.md` §5, Day 16: wet metal, ozone and dormant circuits)*.
- **`CLOCK`** — water. There is none in the ground; what they drink is what the storm left in the rock *(`body-and-resources.md` §3, Days 16–18)*. The best of it is a run of hollows in the crown's rock, perhaps three days' worth for three people as the sun takes it `[-]`. And they came for the protocols, which are inside.
- **`LOAD`** — their knives, their suits, Keo's clothes *(D14.2 OUT)*.
- **`PRESENT`** — the three of them; seabirds; crabs on the wet rock.
- **`BODY`** — all three cut and stiff from the bank and the raft, unfed since the eggs on Day 14, a night of cold off wet skin behind them *(`body-and-resources.md` §3, Day 15)*. **Teva:** healed, the headache *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §4)*. **Keo:** rib, ankle, cuts on all his unsuited skin; the thigh closed. **Benal:** chest raw from the sea; the arm weak; capacitor dead and untold; his suit's film starving and dying in patches.
- **`WANTS`** — `character-arcs.md` §2. **Benal, from Day 15:** believes *he has lost everything that made him valuable*. Here: to be of use with what is left, his ears and nose. **Teva:** to get in to the protocols. **Keo, Days 14–17:** *to be useful again*.
- **`RELATIONS`** — Keo↔Benal 9 *(D15.4)*; Teva→Benal 7. They follow Benal's senses without asking *(`character-arcs.md` §3, D14.1: the turn is made)*.
- **`REGARD`** — `character-arcs.md` §4, Days 16–19: Benal, the reader wanting him heard.
- **`TEXTURE`** — none spent.
- **`CAPABILITIES`** — **Benal's hearing and smell**, unfiltered: the third and last time his senses are the reason they live *(`plan/scene-list.md` D16.1; `plan/tech-rules.md`)*. **The power**: passive, self-cleaning photovoltaic skin, two centuries old and working *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3)*. **The crown block** is a sealed lab block: its air is kept dry by a machine that takes the water out as warm, damp exhaust, so it makes no water anyone could drink, and its fans keep the inside a little above the outside's pressure, so damp air stays out `[-]`. **The hatch**: a sealed door with a lit status panel that wants something they do not have, and beside it a covered manual release, the kind any sealed lab carries so nobody is shut in or out `[-]`. The suits do nothing here that matters.
- **`READER`** — computed; **SU2** begins: the dry powered section is real *(`plan/knowledge-ledger.md`)*. **R26**: the sound and smell are the ones Benal had in his flash on the coast *(D14.1)*, and this time they are really there.
- **`PREMISE`** — the mash is settled; Teva is healed. This scene breaks none.

## SPINE

**The way in**, from the broken block to the hatch giving.

## MOVES

1. **[opens] [world]** First light. They come down out of the broken block by the break they climbed in through. The surge has gone off the low ground, and the wind has gone.
2. **[body]** Hollows in the crown's rock still hold the storm's rain. They drink, and Benal looks at how much there is and says about three days, if the sun is kind.
3. Teva says the protocols will be wherever it is still dry, if anywhere is. The seaward block is dead.
4. **[world]** Benal stops. Under the surf and the birds there is a sound that holds one pitch and does not change, and a smell like the air after lightning. It is what he heard and smelled on the coast, the thing that was not there, and for a moment he thinks it is happening again. *(R26; `[requires D14.1 — Benal's first flash, the sound that repeats exactly]`)*
5. **[spine]** It does not stop, and it has a direction: the long block on the crown. He tells them he can hear something from there and smell it, and they cross the drained ground to it on the rock.
6. Along the wall, the sound is loudest under a grille high up, and the air coming out of it is warm and smells of lightning. He says so, and only that. Keo says that is a machine. Nobody at home has heard one run.
7. **[spine]** Round the end of the block, a hatch in the wall with a panel beside it, lit. Teva says lit means power. Power means something has been running here for two hundred and six years <!-- @WF-transit.elapsed: 206y --> *(scene-list D16.1)*.
8. Teva presses the panel. It shows them something they cannot read and does nothing.
9. Keo finds the covered handle beside it, and asks before he pulls it. Benal says pull it.
10. **[closes]** The seal breaks with a breath of air outward, and the air that comes out of the hatch is dry. *Ending **RESOLVE*** *(`plan/pacing-and-stakes.md` §8)*.

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** The hatch is open and the dry section is real. Benal's senses have got them in, for the third time, and the sound he took for a flash was real. They have water for about three days.

**Worked on and did not change.** Benal's capacitor, untold. Their hunger.

**Left open.** What is inside. Whether the water holds.

**Refused outright.** Benal solving it cleverly: he says what he hears and smells, and the others name it. The hatch as a puzzle. Anyone remarking that old-tech has not dissolved (D16.2's, and nobody has the energy).

## State closure

**Against D15.4:** Night 15 in the broken block above the flooded hall; the water in the hall going down with the surge; no food, no fire, no way into the station.

**Against D16.2:** inside the crown block by mid-morning.
