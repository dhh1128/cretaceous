Forward map per `process/methodology-theory.md` §4. Moves carry events and changes, not wording; how a line is said is the drafter's. The band is a floor for this map's material, never for the prose. **Phase 4 is a hard stop** — the ledger at the foot goes to Daniel before any prose exists.

## IN

- **`PLACE`** — Genesis by daylight, an island ringed by coral, low coral ground around a limestone crown twenty to thirty meters high <!-- @WF-genesis.crown.height: 20-30m --> *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3)*: too small and too porous for any fresh water to collect in the ground. **The seaward block**, dead, whose doors would not open *(D15.2)* and in whose stairwell they spent Night 15 *(D15.3–D15.4)*; the low ground beyond it, under the surge all of Day 15 and drained now to wet rock and standing pools of the surge's salt water `[-]`; and on the spit's crown, the highest ground and above the flood line, **a long low block with its walls whole** `[-]`, on hard rock whose hollows still hold the storm's rain *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3)*. On Day 15 they saw the crown block only across the flooded ground, which they would not wade, and its face toward them has no door `[-]`. **The hatch** is in the block's far end wall, at ground level, a step up from the rock: a door through the wall, not up onto a roof and not down `[-]` *(ledger L1)*. Nothing here is underground.
- **`TIME`** — Day 16, from first light to mid-morning `[-]`.
- **`WEATHER`** — clear, still, washed, the first clear morning since the storm *(`journey-calendar.md` §2, Day 16)*. Hot by mid-morning.
- **`LIGHT`** — hard sun off wet stone and the sea.
- **`SOUND`** — the surf on the reef, seabirds, water draining off the low ground; and, once the wind has gone and the water is off the ground, **a hum from the crown block that holds one pitch and never changes**, for as long as he listens: the fans and power units of the equipment still running inside *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3: the power survived; `kb/worldbuilding/the-second-jump.md` §1: a machine that still answers when spoken to)*. Every living sound he knows pulses or breaks; this does not. **It is not the sound of his flash on the coast**, which rose and fell, every rise the same to a hair *(`content/D14.1.md:113`)*; it shares only that evenness, which nothing alive has.
- **`SMELL`** — a grille high in the crown block's wall breathes out warm air, and it smells of the air after lightning: ozone, which electrical machinery makes, and hot dust off the filters `[-]` *(`milieu-allocation.md` §5, Day 16: wet metal, ozone and dormant circuits)*.
- **`CLOCK`** — water. There is none in the ground; what they drink is what the storm left in the rock *(`body-and-resources.md` §3, Days 16–18)*. The best of it is a run of hollows in the crown's rock, perhaps three days' worth for three people as the sun takes it `[-]`. And they came for the protocols, which are inside.
- **`LOAD`** — their knives, their suits, Keo's clothes *(D14.2 OUT)*.
- **`PRESENT`** — the three of them; seabirds; crabs on the wet rock.
- **`BODY`** — all three cut and stiff from the bank and the raft, unfed since the eggs on Day 14, a night of cold off wet skin behind them *(`body-and-resources.md` §3, Day 15)*. **Teva:** healed, the headache *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §4)*. **Keo:** rib, ankle, cuts on all his unsuited skin; the thigh closed. **Benal:** chest raw from the sea; the arm weak; capacitor dead and untold. **Both suits** are a dead gray skin now, worn because taking them off is worse *(`body-and-resources.md` §3b, Day 16)*. Thirsty: the hollow on the landing is dry by morning `[-]`.
- **`WANTS`** — `character-arcs.md` §2. **Benal, from Day 15:** believes *he has lost everything that made him valuable*. Here: to be of use with what is left, his ears and nose. **Teva:** to get in to the protocols. **Keo, Days 14–17:** *to be useful again*.
- **`RELATIONS`** — Keo↔Benal 9 *(D15.4)*; Teva→Benal 7. They follow Benal's senses without asking *(`character-arcs.md` §3, D14.1: the turn is made)*.
- **`REGARD`** — `character-arcs.md` §4, Days 16–19: Benal, the reader wanting him heard.
- **`TEXTURE`** — none spent.
- **`CAPABILITIES`** — **Benal's hearing and smell**, unfiltered: the third and last time his senses are the reason they get where they need to be — here, inside *(`plan/scene-list.md` D16.1; `plan/tech-rules.md`)*. **The power**: passive, self-cleaning photovoltaic skin, two centuries old and working *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3)*. **The crown block** is a sealed lab block: its air is kept dry by a machine that takes the water out as warm, damp exhaust, so it makes no water anyone could drink, and its fans keep the inside a little above the outside's pressure, so damp air stays out `[-]`. **The hatch**: a sealed door with a lit status panel whose words ask for something they do not have, a name or a key `[-]`, and beside it a covered manual release, founder alloy under its cover, stiff and sound, the kind any sealed lab carries so nobody is shut in or out `[-]`. **The words are the founders' plain words, and all three can read them** *(`kb/worldbuilding/lingo.md`: no illiteracy, and the language has not gone far)*. The suits do nothing here that matters.
- **`READER`** — computed; **SU2** begins: the dry powered section is real *(`plan/knowledge-ledger.md`)*. **R26**: the smell is the one Benal had in his flash on the coast *(D14.1)*, and the sound has that flash's evenness without being its sound; this time both are really there.
- **`PREMISE`** — the mash is settled; Teva is healed. This scene breaks none.

## SPINE

**The way in**, from the broken block to the hatch giving.

## MOVES

1. **[opens] [world]** First light. They come down out of the seaward block by the break they climbed in through. The wind has gone, and the surge has gone off the low ground, leaving wet rock and pools.
2. **[body]** The hollow on the landing is dry. Benal says what he said on the bank: this ground keeps nothing, and only the hard rock up on the crown might. They cross the drained ground to it on the rock, stepping round the pools, which are the sea's and no use to drink; nobody puts a foot in one, shallow and clear to the bottom as they are. *(D15.1 move 9; `plan/milieu-brief.md` §3.)*
3. **[body]** Hollows in the crown's rock still hold the storm's rain. They drink, and Benal looks along the run of them and says about three days, if the sun is kind.
4. Teva looks at the long block beside them: no door on this face, like the dead one. She says the protocols will be wherever it is still dry, if anywhere is, and that they should go back and try the dead block's far side now the water is off it.
5. **[world]** Benal stops. Under the surf and the birds there is a sound that holds one pitch and does not change, and a smell like the air after lightning. The smell is the one from the coast, the thing that was not there; the sound is not the coast's, which rose and fell, but it is as even as that was, too even for anything alive, and for a moment he thinks it is happening again. *(R26; `[requires D14.1 — Benal's first flash: a sound that repeats exactly, and the air after lightning]`)*
6. **[spine]** It does not stop, and it has a direction: the block beside them. He tells them he can hear something in there and smell it, and Teva does not go back to the dead block.
7. Along the wall, the sound is loudest under a grille high up, and the air coming out of it is warm and smells of lightning. He says so, and only that. Keo says that is old-tech, and it is running.
8. **[spine]** Round the far end of the block, the hatch, with a panel beside it, lit. Teva says lit means power. Power means something has been running here for two hundred and six years <!-- @WF-transit.elapsed: 206y --> *(scene-list D16.1)*.
9. Teva presses the panel. Words come up, and she reads them out: it wants something of them that they do not have, and it does nothing.
10. Keo finds the covered handle beside it, and asks before he pulls it. Benal says pull it.
11. **[closes]** The seal breaks with a breath of air outward, and the air that comes out of the hatch is dry. *Ending **RESOLVE*** *(`plan/pacing-and-stakes.md` §8)*.

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** The hatch is open and the dry section is real. Benal's senses have got them in, for the third time, and what he took for a flash was really there. They have water for about three days, in the crown's hollows.

**Worked on and did not change.** Benal's capacitor, untold. Their hunger.

**Left open.** What is inside. Whether the water holds.

**Refused outright.** Benal solving it cleverly: he says what he hears and smells, and the others name it. The hatch as a puzzle. Anyone remarking that old-tech has not dissolved (D16.2's, and nobody has the energy).

## State closure

**Against D15.4:** Night 15 in the broken block above the flooded hall; the water in the hall going down with the surge; no food, no fire, no way into the station.

**Against D16.2:** inside the crown block by mid-morning.
