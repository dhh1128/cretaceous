# D16.1 — the hatch — BENAL (close third)  ·  MEDIUM, ~1,200 words

Forward map per `process/methodology-theory.md` §4. Moves carry events and changes, not wording; how a line is said is the drafter's. The band is a floor for this map's material, never for the prose. **Phase 4 is a hard stop** — the ledger at the foot goes to Daniel before any prose exists.

Purpose **DISCOVER**, ending **RESOLVE**. Ladders EM7 P6 S6 SP7. Moderate stakes: thirst, hunger and a station they cannot get into; nothing attacks. Drafted script-first: moves 2, 3, 5, 7, 8, 10 and 11 are carried by talk; the rest by the morning, the ground and Benal's senses.

## IN

- **`PLACE`** — Genesis by daylight, an island ringed by coral, low coral ground around a limestone crown twenty to thirty meters high <!-- @WF-genesis.crown.height: 20-30m --> *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3)*: too small and too porous for any fresh water to collect in the ground. **The plant house** on the bank's landward edge, a ruin with nothing in it *(D15.2)*, on whose gallery they spent Night 15 *(D15.3–D15.4)*; the low ground between the bank and the crown, under the surge all of Day 15 and drained now to wet rock and standing pools of the surge's salt water `[-]`; the landing on this, the mainland side, its works gone under the water, and from it **the way up**, a track up the crown's gentle mainland-side slope past the living arches, bare alloy frames now, their skins long gone *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3)* `[-]`. On the crown, the highest ground and above any flood, the founders' lab modules, bolted to the rock and banked to the eaves with coral rubble and sand under their solar roofs, on hard rock whose hollows still hold the storm's rain *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3)*. **The crown block** is a run of those modules joined end to end and banked as one, its inner doors the modules' end doors `[-]`; from the way up it shows as a long bank of rubble with a roof on it, and no door. On Day 15 they could not reach the way up, which starts across the flooded ground they would not wade, and from the bank across the flood, in Day 15's gray light, the banked block showed only low pale edges above its rubble, with no door and no light `[-]` *(D15.2 move 1)*. **The hatch** is in the block's far end wall, which stands in a cut in the crown's rock, under the lip of the banking, out of the salt and the weather, which is why its panel and its seal have lasted; at ground level, a step up from the rock: a door through the wall, not up onto a roof and not down `[-]` *(ledger L1)*. Nothing here is underground.
- **`TIME`** — Day 16, from first light to mid-morning `[-]`.
- **`WEATHER`** — clear, still, washed, the first clear morning since the storm *(`journey-calendar.md` §2, Day 16)*. Warm and clean.
- **`LIGHT`** — hard sun off wet stone and the sea.
- **`SOUND`** — the surf on the reef, seabirds, water draining off the low ground; and, once the wind has gone and the water is off the ground, **a hum from the crown block that holds one pitch and never changes**, for as long as he listens: the power converters of the equipment still running inside, which have no moving parts and hum as transformers do; the generators themselves make no sound `[-]` *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3: the power survived, and what had moving pieces has failed; `kb/worldbuilding/the-second-jump.md` §1: a machine that still answers when spoken to)*. Every living sound he knows pulses or breaks; this does not. **It is not the sound of his flash on the coast**, which rose and fell, every rise the same to a hair *(`content/D14.1.md:113`)*; it shares only that evenness, which nothing alive has.
- **`SMELL`** — at the hatch's seal a thread of warm air comes out, the inside warmed by the equipment and by the morning sun on the roof, and it smells of the air after lightning: ozone, which electrical machinery makes, and warm dust `[-]` *(`milieu-allocation.md` §5, Day 16: wet metal, ozone and dormant circuits)*.
- **`CLOCK`** — water. There is none in the ground; what they drink is what the storm left in the rock *(`body-and-resources.md` §3, Days 16–18)*. The best of it is a run of hollows in the crown's rock, perhaps three days' worth for three people as the sun takes it `[-]`. And they came for the protocols, which are inside.
- **`LOAD`** — their knives, their suits, Keo's clothes *(D14.2 OUT)*.
- **`PRESENT`** — the three of them; seabirds; crabs on the wet rock.
- **`BODY`** — all three cut and stiff from the bank and the boat, unfed since the eggs on Day 14, a night of cold off wet skin behind them *(`body-and-resources.md` §3, Day 15)*. **Teva:** healed, the headache *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §4)*. **Keo:** rib, ankle, cuts on all his unsuited skin; the thigh closed. **Benal:** chest raw from the sea; the arm weak; capacitor dead and untold. **Both suits** are a dead gray skin now, worn because taking them off is worse *(`body-and-resources.md` §3b, Day 16)*. Thirsty with the morning: their last drink was at the bank's hollows at dusk, nothing on the gallery, and the rain on the bank has drained away by morning `[-]`.
- **`WANTS`** — `character-arcs.md` §2. **Benal, from Day 15:** believes *he has lost everything that made him valuable*. Here: to be of use with what is left, his ears and nose. **Teva:** to get in to the protocols. **Keo, Days 14–17:** *to be useful again*.
- **`RELATIONS`** — Keo↔Benal 9 *(D15.4)*; Teva→Benal 7. They agreed in the night that this cannot be all of Genesis and that they would go and look *(D15.4 move 13)*. They follow Benal's senses without asking *(`character-arcs.md` §3, D14.1: the turn is made)*.
- **`REGARD`** — `character-arcs.md` §4, Days 16–19: Benal, the reader wanting him heard.
- **`TEXTURE`** — none spent.
- **`CAPABILITIES`** — **Benal's hearing and smell**, unfiltered: the third and last time his senses are the reason they get where they need to be — here, inside *(`plan/scene-list.md` D16.1; `plan/tech-rules.md`)*. **The power**: americium-241 radioisotope generators at about half their old output; the solar arrays are dead *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3)*. **The crown block** is sealed lab modules, bolted to the rock and banked to the eaves: it is dry because it is sealed and banked, and warm inside from the equipment that runs in it; nothing in it moves, and nothing in it makes water `[-]` *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3: the banked modules on the crown stand dry and lit; what had moving pieces has failed)*. **The hatch**: a sealed door with a lit status panel whose words ask for something they do not have, a name or a key `[-]`, and beside it a covered manual release, founder alloy under its cover, stiff and sound, the kind any sealed lab carries so nobody is shut in or out `[-]`. **The words are the founders' plain words, and all three can read them** *(`kb/worldbuilding/lingo.md`: no illiteracy, and the language has not gone far)*. The suits do nothing here that matters.
- **`READER`** — computed; **SU2** begins: the dry powered section is real *(`plan/knowledge-ledger.md`)*. **R26**: the smell is the one Benal had in his flash on the coast *(D14.1)*, and the sound has that flash's evenness without being its sound; this time both are really there.
- **`PREMISE`** — the mash is settled; Teva is healed. This scene breaks none.

## SPINE

**The way in**, from the broken block to the hatch giving.

## MOVES

1. **[opens] [world]** First light, and the first clear morning since the storm. They come down the stair from the gallery and out at the plant house's doorway. The wind has gone, and the surge has gone off the low ground, leaving wet rock and pools.
2. **[world]** In the clear light Genesis is more than Day 15 showed them. Across the drained ground the crown stands up sharp against the sky; the living arches on its slope are bare alloy frames, their skins long gone, and there are a great many of them `[-]`; on top, long banked shapes with roofs on them, in a row. People lived here, a lot of them, for years. Keo asks whether they see it; Teva looks at it a long time; Benal starts counting the arches aloud, exactly, and keeps going `[-]`. It lifts all three before anything is found. *(Q-PMHV, Daniel: the emotional uplift the next day when they see more of Genesis; D15.4 move 13.)*
3. **[body]** The rain on the bank is gone from its hollows. Benal says what he said on the bank: this ground keeps nothing, and only the hard rock up on the crown might. They cross the drained low ground to the landing, on the rock, stepping round the pools, which are the sea's and no use to drink; nobody puts a foot in one, shallow and clear to the bottom as they are. At the landing they keep to the high side, back from the lagoon's edge, and go up the crown's gentle slope by the way past the bare arch frames, at Keo's pace on the ankle `[-]`. *(D15.1 move 9; `plan/milieu-brief.md` §3.)*
4. **[body]** Hollows in the crown's rock still hold the storm's rain. They drink, and Benal looks along the run of them and says about three days, if the sun is kind.
5. Teva looks at the long bank beside them with a roof on it: close to, it is buried, no door on any side she can see. She says the protocols will be wherever it is still dry, if anywhere is, and that they should go back down and look along the low ground past the landing now the water is off it.
6. **[world]** Benal stops. Under the surf and the birds there is a sound that holds one pitch and does not change, and a smell like the air after lightning. The smell is the one from the coast, the thing that was not there; the sound is not the coast's, which rose and fell, but it is as even as that was, too even for anything alive, and for a moment he thinks it is happening again. *(R26; `[requires D14.1 — Benal's first flash: a sound that repeats exactly, and the air after lightning]`)*
7. **[spine]** It does not stop, and it has a direction: the bank beside them. He tells them he can hear something in there and smell it, and Teva does not go back down.
8. Along the bank the sound grows toward its far end. Round it the rock drops into a cut, and in the cut, under the lip of the banking, the end wall stands clear, and in the wall a hatch; at its seal a thread of warm air comes out and smells of lightning. He says so, and only that. Keo says that is old-tech, and it is running.
9. **[spine]** Beside the hatch, a panel, lit. Teva says lit means power. Power means something has been running here for two hundred and six years <!-- @WF-transit.elapsed: 206y --> *(scene-list D16.1)*.
10. Teva presses the panel. Words come up, and she reads them out: it wants something of them that they do not have, and it does nothing.
11. Keo finds the covered handle beside it, and asks before he pulls it. Benal says pull it.
12. **[closes]** The seal breaks with a breath of air outward, and the air that comes out of the hatch is dry. *Ending **RESOLVE*** *(`plan/pacing-and-stakes.md` §8)*.

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** The hatch is open and the dry section is real. Benal's senses have got them in, for the third time, and what he took for a flash was really there. They have water for about three days, in the crown's hollows.

**Worked on and did not change.** Benal's capacitor, untold. Their hunger.

**Left open.** What is inside. Whether the water holds.

**Refused outright.** Benal solving it cleverly: he says what he hears and smells, and the others name it. The hatch as a puzzle. Anyone remarking that old-tech has not dissolved (D16.2's, and nobody has the energy).

## State closure

**Against D15.4:** Night 15 on the plant house gallery above the flooded floor; the water below going down with the surge; no food, no fire, no way into the station; the agreement to go and look in the morning.

**Against D16.2:** inside the crown block by mid-morning.
