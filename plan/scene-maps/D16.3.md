# D16.3 — the tablets — BENAL (close third)  ·  MEDIUM, ~1,400 words

Forward map per `process/methodology-theory.md` §4. Moves carry events and changes, not wording; how a line is said is the drafter's. The band is a floor for this map's material, never for the prose. **Phase 4 is a hard stop** — the ledger at the foot goes to Daniel before any prose exists.

Purpose **DISCOVER**, ending **RESOLVE**. Ladders EM8 P4 S7 SP8. **SU2**: the journey was not futile, landing on all-is-lost. Drafted script-first: moves 2, 4, 7, 8 and 10 are carried by talk; the rest by the room and the plates.

## IN

- **`PLACE`** — the archive, through the inner door of the crown block: dry, lit, the hum louder `[-]`. Racks of thin metal plates; shelves of paper, dry and whole because the room is dry *(D16.1)*; panels that may hold databases *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3)*. Nothing in it is wrapped.
- **`TIME`** — Day 16, afternoon to dusk.
- **`WEATHER`** — outside, clear and hot; inside, nothing.
- **`LIGHT`** — the panels.
- **`SOUND`** — the hum; their own voices.
- **`CLOCK`** — the light outside, and the water in the crown hollows; they are still on an island with no boat *(scene-list D16.3 hazard)*.
- **`LOAD`** — nothing but what is here.
- **`PRESENT`** — the three of them.
- **`BODY`** — as D16.2's exit: dry, fed, everything still hurting.
- **`WANTS`** — `character-arcs.md` §2. **Benal:** to be of use; believes *he has lost everything that made him valuable* (until Day 17). **Teva:** the protocols, for Omya and for every child like Alira. **Keo:** to be useful again.
- **`RELATIONS`** — `character-arcs.md` §3, D16.3: **the protocols pass through Teva's hands. She cannot read all of it, but she recognizes the first twenty positions Omya drilled into her (T8), etched in metal: her grandmother's words outlasting her grandmother's mind.** Teva→Benal 7.
- **`REGARD`** — `character-arcs.md` §4, Days 16–19: Teva, the reader loving her for stopping at Omya; Benal, wanting him heard.
- **`TEXTURE`** — `plan/backstory.md` T8 (the positions clean to the twentieth, then in pieces).
- **`CAPABILITIES`** — **Benal reads fast and reads everything**; the founders' labels are plain words, and a record room is ordered by what its labels say `[-]`. **Teva** holds the protocol positions to the twentieth *(T8)*. **The inner door** answers its panel from inside, where the hatch's did not from outside `[-]`. **R26 inside Genesis**, Benal's own *(`plan/foreshadow-and-motif.md` R26)*.
- **`READER`** — computed. **SU2 lands** *(`plan/knowledge-ledger.md`)*. **EP11 is found here**, and the epigraph channel becomes diegetic *(`plan/knowledge-ledger.md`)*. EP11 sends the charter and the paradox constraints to the permanent archive, in durable substrate, in triplicate, and leaves operational instruction in the working files. So the charter on metal is what EP11 promised, and **the protocol on metal is more than EP11 asked for: someone at Genesis put it there. Who, and when, is ledger item L1: the side that lost the leave-Genesis argument, in the third year.** Benal sees that nothing here was ever wrapped, which he will need at D17.2 *(R7, R7b)*.
- **`PREMISE`** — none in play.

## SPINE

**The archive**, from the inner door opening to the plates counted.

## MOVES

1. **[opens] [spine]** Benal works out the inner door's panel from its words, from the inside, and it opens on the archive.
2. **[world]** Racks of thin metal plates; shelves of paper, dry and whole. Benal sees that nothing in the room was ever wrapped, nothing at all, and has not the energy to think about it. *(R7, R7b: the observation D17.2 needs.)*
3. **[spine]** He goes along the racks reading labels. The first racks are what they say: the charter, the constraints, in three copies each.
4. At the head of the charter racks, under a clear cover, the ruling that put them there *(EP11)*. He reads it out: charter and constraints to the permanent archive, on plate; operational instruction left in the working files. Teva says the working files are the panels, then, and the panels are two hundred years old.
5. **[spine]** At the end of the last rack the plates change. They are headed with the vector work, and they were cut later and in a different hand of lettering `[-]`: somebody put the operational instruction on plate after all.
6. He hands the first protocol plate to Teva. She reads down it, slowly, and at the first position she stops reading and starts saying it, clean, to the twentieth, and the plate goes on past where she has ever been. *(T8.)*
7. **[relation]** She says it is Omya's, and Benal says yes, it is, and she lets him see her face.
8. **[spine]** The first protocol plate carries a list of the plates that follow, by number. Benal counts the rack against it, and they are all there *(scene-list D16.3: the complete protocols)*.
9. **[body] [ledger]** Counting, he loses his place, and finds himself three plates further on than he has counted, with his hand on a plate he does not remember reaching for. He starts again and says nothing. *(R26: Benal's, in Genesis.)*
10. Keo lifts one plate, feels the weight of it, and looks along the rack, and says what nobody has: they cannot carry all of that. *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §11, item 11: what is portable.)*
11. **[closes]** Outside the light is going. They have what they came for, on an island with no boat. Benal is already looking at the next door, which is dark, and which will wait until the morning. *Ending **RESOLVE***.

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** They have the complete synthesis protocols, on metal, counted against their own list. Teva has recited the first twenty from memory against the plate, and let Benal see what it did to her. EP11 is in their hands, and so is the fact that somebody went past it. Benal has seen that nothing at Genesis was wrapped. Keo has named the weight.

**Worked on and did not change.** How they get home: no boat, no water past the crown hollows.

**Left open.** Who cut the protocol plates, against the ruling. How much they can carry. What is behind the next door.

**Refused outright.** Triumph: they are on a coral island with no boat, and the scene knows it before they do. Anyone making a speech. Anyone connecting the unwrapped room to the Enclave's archaea before D17.2.

## State closure

**Against D16.2:** early afternoon, fed; Benal back at the inner door.

**Against D17.1** (Day 17, morning, Teva, the archive): the night in the dry block; the morning's water from the crown hollows; the plates where they found them; the next door still to open.
