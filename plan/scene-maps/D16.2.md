## SPINE

**The dry**, from standing in it dripping to the question of what else is here.

## MOVES

1. **[opens] [body]** Keo stands in the first room with the water running off him onto a dry floor, under a steady light, and his skin, under no shell since Day 3, begins to dry, cuts and all. It undoes him more than food would.
2. **[spine]** The other two start working at once. Benal goes to the inner door's panel; it is lit and wants something, the same as the hatch's. Teva goes along the labels on the walls, reading them out. Once she stops in the middle of one and answers somebody, and there is nobody, and she goes on reading as if she had not `[-]` *(R26: Teva's, seen by Keo)*.
3. Keo tries a label, slowly, losing his place and turning a letter round the way he did in class, and stops. Benal sees him stop and says nothing *(K15, B22)*.
4. **[world]** Old-tech in every room, none of it wrapped and none of it dissolved: faded and scuffed with age, and whole. Keo notices that it should have, and has not the energy to say so. Nobody does.
5. **[spine]** Teva says the water is low now and will not stay low, and they have had nothing since the eggs. The door can wait an hour; the low water cannot.
6. **[world]** Keo and Teva go down the way and out over the reef flat together, the way you go near water at home, and keep to the pools under the rocks. The dark shape lies far out past the reef edge; Keo names it and they do not go nearer.
7. **[spine]** Keo knows this work. He taps each clam and throws back the ones that gape, takes two fish the falling tide has just caught in a pool, and folds it all in his tunic. He looks at a crab a long time, a crayfish that walks sideways, and leaves it: at home a crayfish is always cooked, and there is no fire (Q-BNBP, Q-DREY). *(K17; `body-and-resources.md` §3, Days 16–18.)* It is the one hour of the day there is anything for him to do.
8. **[body]** Back in the dry room they eat it raw, all three, on a dry floor, the clams opened with knives. It is the first meal that is enough since Day 6. Keo puts the bigger fish in front of Benal without a word about his arm `[-]`. *(No fire: the kindler went with the fieldpack.)*
9. **[world]** Genesis groans on its own weight, a long creak through the walls with the sun full on the block, and dust sifts down from a seam overhead in the outer room `[-]`. They move in, away from it.
10. **[relation]** They say what they will do first at home, lightly, each in their own register: Benal that he will write the equation out again for Marek to look at, the board being at the bottom of the sea; Keo that he will tell his father something; Teva that she will do Omya's hair *(`character-arcs.md` §3, Day 19)*.
11. **[body] [ledger]** The other two go back to the door and the labels. Keo sits with his hands empty, glad they can do it. He says something to Noli under his breath, as if she were at his knee, and nothing answers `[plants → D16.4 — he says something aloud to nobody, and at Genesis something answers]`. For a moment the light in the room seems to come from somewhere else, an hour that is not this one, and then it does not. He says nothing. *(R26: Keo's, in Genesis.)* The scene lets the reader notice his empty hands before he does.
12. **[closes]** Teva asks what else is in here. It is not Keo who asks. *Ending **QUESTION***.

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** They are dry, lit and fed. The three home hopes are said. Keo has watched the others be necessary and had his one hour of use outside, and said nothing about either; tomorrow's low water is the next. Benal has seen Keo stop reading. They have moved inward, away from the seam.

**Worked on and did not change.** The water, still only what the storm left. The inner door.

**Left open.** What else is in here.

**Refused outright.** The sanctuary scene: the dryness is not safety, and the reader is uneasy where the characters are not. Keo sulking. Anyone naming the dyslexia. The croc as an event.

## State closure

**Against D16.1:** inside the crown block by mid-morning, the hatch open behind them.

**Against D16.3:** early afternoon, fed; Benal back at the inner door.
