She reads it out to Keo, who has sat down beside her because there is nothing else for him to do. *(`plan/outline.md`: the logs of the perfect arrival; `content/epigraphs.md` EP3: two hundred and seventy-five personnel, sent to work.)*

Here it is said plainly by the people who were given it, as a duty with a reason, and the reason is real. *(`plan/knowledge-ledger.md`: the duty is a genuine founder instruction and the Traditionalists are right about it.)* `[requires D2.6 — the Council's Mission doctrine, said as founder law]`

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** Teva has read the arrival and the founders' own statement of the duty, and knows it was real. She knows her flash in the plant house showed a real day and real people. She has found Cecilia Arroyo, Sila's ancestor, among the people leaving, and seen Sila's name inside hers. She has not found her own people. Benal has the lab open. Keo and Benal are out at the low water.

**Worked on and did not change.** Her want to find her own line.

**Left open.** What she will do with the face. What the lab holds.

**Refused outright.** An infodump with a POV attached: she reads to find her people, not to inform the reader *(`plan/scene-list.md` D17.1 hazard)*. Pointing at the leaving roll at the back or at any one face Benal will need later *(scene-list D17.1)*. Teva telling anyone yet. Teva concluding anything about Sila.
