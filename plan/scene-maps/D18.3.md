# D18.3 — the man at the cleft — BENAL (close third)  ·  SHORT, ~800 words

Forward map per `process/methodology-theory.md` §4. Moves carry events and changes, not wording; how a line is said is the drafter's. The band is a floor for this map's material, never for the prose. **Phase 4 is a hard stop** — the ledger at the foot goes to Daniel before any prose exists.

Purpose **DISCOVER**, ending **HELD**. Ladders EM8 P5 S8 SP9. Low physical stakes: he sits high on dry rock, back from the edge, high enough that nothing in the water could reach him *(`content/D8.1.md:19`: the same allowance Keo took on the cut bank)*, inside a reef nothing hunts within *(`plan/scene-maps/D15.1.md` `PRESENT`, approved)*; the stakes are what he now knows and will not yet say. **The hazard is the flash as a vision sequence** *(`plan/scene-list.md` D18.3)*: it is a real moment at that place, perceived, and it is written as flat and physical as watching a man work. Drafted script-first in the small way a solitary scene allows: moves 6 and 9 are carried by talk, the first to himself in fragments *(`kb/worldbuilding/benals-equation.md`, rule 1: Benal talks in fragments, to himself)*, the second to the others; the rest by the rock, the crack and the flash.

## MOVES

1. **[opens] [world]** The sun is down in the west and the moon is high over the mainland, laying a road of broken light across the lagoon to the reef. Benal is on the rocks above the flat, on the top of the one over the shelf, well back from the edge. He came because the hard is full of the voice, the cells' fizz and the argument, and this was the quietest place he had found. *(The time and the place are carried by what he sees, not by D18.2's last line, Q-NP03.)*
2. **[world]** Below him the tide is up at the shelf's edge and in the crack, breathing, a suck and a slap. Yesterday at the low water he lay face-down on this same rock with his face over its edge, watching Keo work the pools, and saw on the shelf below, at the crack's lip, the round hole drilled in the rock and the rust run down from it, which marked it out from every other hole on that shore, because nothing else there bleeds rust `[?]`. He can make out the dark of it now in the moonlight. `[requires D17.1 — Benal out on the rocks above the flat at Day 17's low water, watching Keo work the pools]`
3. **[body]** He does the numbers he came to do: full about the middle of tomorrow; short of home at first light; the weight the archive may have. They come out the same each time. *(D18.2 moves 13, 15, 16.)*
4. **[world] [turn — BENAL]** The smell of lightning, and the prickle over his arms, the hairs lifting, and the surf gone. It is day. Below him a man is on his knees on the shelf beside the crack, lifting stones out of the hollow and setting them aside. He lowers something into it with both hands, packs rubble round it and over it, and sets the stones back on top. His clothes are patched and faded `[-]`. He works slowly, his back stiff when he straightens. The hole at the crack's lip is not there. *(R26 signature, glancing; `kb/worldbuilding/the-second-jump.md` §7, §8.)*
5. **[spine]** When he straightens and turns side-on to look along the bank, Benal sees his face, gray-haired and thin, older than the leaf it is on by more years than Benal can count `[-]`, and knows it from the register, among the leaves he looked at longest, the people whose work was the temporal lab `[-]`. `[requires D17.1 — the personnel register, a printed face, a name and a work on each leaf, read by Benal in the night of Day 16]`
   - *Two hundred walked and about seventy-five stayed, and the leaving roll at the back of the register lists who walked (Q-P0BV).* Benal names him, and knows from the roll that his name is among those who stayed.
6. **[spine]** Benal looks along the bank to where the plant house has stood every day he has been on the island, and there is bare rock. There is no Genesis. Then the surf is back, and the moon, and the hole at the crack's lip, and the plant house a dark shape along the bank. **The inference, in four steps, to himself, in fragments, the stammer going out of it as it becomes his own subject** *(`plan/voice-sheets.md`, Benal: the stammer vanishes the moment he is on his own subject)*: he is old, so this is long after they came; there is no plant house, and the plant house went up in the first months and is standing there now, so this is before they came; he can only have grown old after they came, because the leaf is from before; so he went backward, alive, and worked here. *(`plan/scene-list.md` D18.3: the inference chain.)*
7. **[body]** His hands are not steady on the warm rock. He looks down at the hollow and the water breathing in the crack beside it. The shelf is at the water's edge with the tide up and the dark coming, and nobody goes to the water's edge alone in the dark *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3: water is death)*; the stones are packed hard, and he has no knife of his own and no bar *(D15.1 `LOAD`)*. He is glad of the reasons, and knows he is glad. *(`plan/scene-list.md` D18.3: he does not dig tonight.)*
8. **[world]** As he climbs down off the rock the air has turned and is coming off the land, and it brings him the mainland faint over the water: wet leaves, mud, rot, the forest, the flood.
9. **[closes] [relation]** In the dry room the other two are awake. Teva asks where he was, and he says the rocks, where it is quiet; Keo asks him the numbers, and he gives them, full about the middle of tomorrow, short of home at first light, and Teva says she will count the water at first light. About the man at the crack he says nothing. *Ending **HELD***.

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** Benal has seen a man from the register, years older, bury something beside the crack at a time when there was no Genesis, and has reasoned it out: the stayers went backward and lived and worked here. He knows the place: the hollow beside the crack, by the drilled hole. He has given the others the numbers and kept the rest.

**Worked on and did not change.** The charge, the water, the argument about leaving early.

**Left open.** What is in the hollow. When he will dig. Whether he will tell them.

**Refused outright.** The flash as a vision: no shimmer, no glow, no meaning announced, the man only working *(`plan/scene-list.md` D18.3 hazard; R26: no shimmer and no crackle)*. **The man looking up at Benal, or anything that implies two-way contact** *(`kb/worldbuilding/the-second-jump.md` §10)*. Saying how far back the man went *(`kb/worldbuilding/the-second-jump.md` §10)*. Benal digging, or going down to the shelf in the dark. Any link between the man and the hand that cut the plates, which waits on a ruling about whose that hand was *(`plan/scene-maps/D17.2.md`: the equation plate, the fix and the protocol plates in one later hand)*. EP8 *(`content/epigraphs.md`: nobody may connect them in book 1)*. Benal not talking: he talks to himself through the chain and to the others about the numbers, and withholds the one thing.
