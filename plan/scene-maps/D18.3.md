# D18.3 — the man at the cleft — BENAL (close third)  ·  SHORT, ~800 words

Forward map per `process/methodology-theory.md` §4. Moves carry events and changes, not wording; how a line is said is the drafter's. The band is a floor for this map's material, never for the prose. **Phase 4 is a hard stop** — the ledger at the foot goes to Daniel before any prose exists.

Purpose **DISCOVER**, ending **HELD**. Ladders EM8 P5 S8 SP9. Low physical stakes: he sits on the crown's top, back from the edge of its seaward brow, twenty to thirty meters <!-- @WF-genesis.crown.height: 20-30m --> above the sea, where nothing in the water could reach him *(`content/D8.1.md:19`: the same allowance Keo took on the cut bank; Q-HPSB)*; the stakes are what he now knows and will not yet say. **The hazard is the flash as a vision sequence** *(`plan/scene-list.md` D18.3)*: it is a real moment at that place, perceived, and it is written as flat and physical as watching a man work. ## Decided overnight, to review

- The anchor cleft is a crack in the crown's top at its **seaward brow**, where the steep face drops to the sea, twenty to thirty meters <!-- @WF-genesis.crown.height: 20-30m --> above any surge, which is why the packing survived *(Q-HPSB; `plan/milieu-brief.md` §3: the crown's seaward face is steep)*. Drilled into the rock at the crack's lip `[-]` is a round hole with a rust stain run down from it, the anchor of a dead array frame whose steel is long gone; **beside the crack** is a hollow in the rock packed with stones, which is where the man buries the thing *(Q-HPSB; `kb/worldbuilding/the-second-jump.md` §7: the cleft where the anchor shaft of a Genesis solar array will one day be drilled; `plan/milieu-brief.md` §3: steel is rust)*.
- `[-]` Benal knows the place because the brow is where he goes for quiet *(Q-HPSB)*: he has sat there before, back from the edge, and the first time he noticed the hole at the crack's lip, because it was round and clean-sided and bled rust down the rock, and nothing else on that bare rock did. This is D18.3's own memory, told here, and needs nothing from an earlier map.
- `[-]` Why he is here: the hard is full of the voice, the cells' fizz and the argument, his ears have had all day of it, and the brow, across the top from the hard, is where he goes for quiet *(Q-HPSB)*; he came to do the numbers alone.
- `[-]` Why he does not dig: it is dark, and a half moon is light enough to see the hole by and not to dig by, a few paces from a drop to the sea; the stones are packed hard and he has no knife of his own and no bar *(D15.1 `LOAD`)*; and he does not trust what he saw, and wants to be sure before anyone can tell him it was fever *(`WANTS`)*; and he is glad of the reasons. By day, with a tool and a reason to slip away, it is D19.2's *(`plan/scene-list.md` D19.2: late morning)*.
- What shows him there is no Genesis is the crown's own top: from the brow he looks along it at the banked block and the arches' bare frames, which the founders put up in their years here and which stand there every day *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3; D16.1 move 3)*, and in the flash there are no modules and no arches, only bare rock *(Q-HPSB)*.
- `[-]` How he knows the man's face: from the register's leaves he studied hardest, the temporal workers' `[-]`, read in the night of Day 16 *(D17.1 move 1)*; the naming itself is in move 5, by the notch in the brow (Q-NWQR) and the name Daniel chose (Q-MMZ3).
- `[-]` What the man buries is something he carries in both hands, lowered into the hollow beside the crack and packed over with rubble and then stones; its form is left unstated, because D19.2 and book 2 own it *(`plan/sequels.md`: the capsule is small, findable, damaged)*.
- `[-]` The flash in daylight, and in it the drilled hole is not there.
- The prose calls the anchor cleft *the crack*, because `plan/milieu-brief.md` §8 reserves *the cleft* for the kiva's entrance; *anchor cleft* stays the planning label in `plan/scene-list.md` D18.3 and D19.2. Settled by canon *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §8, approved: in this corpus *the cleft* means only the kiva's walk-in entrance)*.
- `[-]` He tells the others the charge numbers and nothing about the man, because at D17.3 he said a true thing and nobody heard it, and he wants this one in his hands before he says it.

## MOVES

1. **[opens] [world]** The sun is down in the west and the half moon is high over the mainland at his back. Benal is on the crown's top at its seaward brow, a few paces back from where the face drops to the sea, the crack and the hollow a few paces along from him. He came because the hard is full of the voice, the cells' fizz and the argument, and the brow is where he goes for quiet *(Q-HPSB)*. *(The time and the place are carried by what he sees, not by D18.2's last line, Q-NP03.)*
2. **[world]** Below the face the surf breaks white along the reef. He has sat here before, and the first time he noticed, at the crack's lip, the round hole drilled in the rock and the rust run down from it, because nothing else on that bare rock bleeds rust `[-]`. He can make out the dark of it now in the moonlight.
3. **[body]** He does the numbers he came to do: full about the middle of tomorrow; a few kilometers over at first light, on the voice's figure; the weight the archive may have. They come out the same each time. *(D18.2 moves 13, 15, 16.)*
4. **[world] [turn — BENAL]** The smell of lightning, and the prickle over his arms, the hairs lifting, and the surf gone. It is day. A few paces along the brow a man is on his knees beside the crack, lifting stones out of the hollow and setting them aside. He lowers something into it with both hands, packs rubble round it and over it, and sets the stones back on top. His clothes are patched and faded `[-]`. He works slowly, his back stiff when he straightens. The hole at the crack's lip is not there. *(R26 signature, glancing; `kb/worldbuilding/the-second-jump.md` §7, §8.)*
5. **[spine]** When he straightens and turns his face along the crown, Benal sees it, gray-haired and thin, and through the white of the left eyebrow a bare notch that breaks it in two *(Q-NWQR; D17.1 move 7)*; decades older than the face on the leaf `[-]`, and knows it from the register, among the leaves he looked at longest, the people whose work was the temporal lab `[-]`: **Elias Sandoval** *(Q-MMZ3)*. `[requires D17.1 — the personnel register, a printed face, a name and a work on each leaf, read by Benal in the night of Day 16]`
   - *Two hundred walked and about seventy-five stayed, and the leaving roll at the back of the register lists who walked (Q-P0BV).* Benal names him, and knows from the roll that his name is among those who stayed.
6. **[spine]** Benal looks along the crown to where the block and the arches' frames have stood every day he has been on the island, and there are no modules and no arches, only bare rock. There is no Genesis. Then the surf is back, and the moon, and the hole at the crack's lip, and the block and the frames dark against the sky *(Q-HPSB)*. **The inference, in four steps, to himself, in fragments, the stammer going out of it as it becomes his own subject** *(`plan/voice-sheets.md`, Benal: the stammer vanishes the moment he is on his own subject)*: he is old, so this is long after they came; there is nothing on the crown, and the block and the arches went up in the founders' years and stand there now, so this is before they came; he can only have grown old after they came, because the leaf is from before; so he went backward, alive, and worked here. *(`plan/scene-list.md` D18.3: the inference chain.)*
7. **[body]** His hands are not steady on the warm rock. He looks along at the hollow and the crack beside it. It is dark now, the moon light enough to see the hole by and not to dig by, a few paces from the drop; the stones are packed hard, and he has no knife of his own and no bar *(D15.1 `LOAD`)*; and he does not trust what he saw `[-]`. He is glad of the reasons, and knows he is glad. *(`plan/scene-list.md` D18.3: he does not dig tonight.)*
8. **[world]** As he walks back across the top `[-]` the air has turned and is coming off the land, and it brings him the mainland faint over the water: wet leaves, mud, rot, the forest, the flood.
9. **[closes] [relation]** In the dry room the other two are awake. Teva asks where he was, and he says the brow, where it is quiet; Keo asks him the numbers, and he gives them, full about the middle of tomorrow, a few kilometers over at first light, on the voice's figure, and Teva says she will count the water at first light. About the man at the crack he says nothing. *Ending **HELD***.

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** Benal has seen a man from the register, years older, bury something beside the crack at a time when there was no Genesis, and has reasoned it out: the stayers went backward and lived and worked here. He knows the place: the hollow beside the crack, by the drilled hole. He has given the others the numbers and kept the rest.

**Worked on and did not change.** The charge, the water, the argument about leaving early.

**Left open.** What is in the hollow. When he will dig. Whether he will tell them.

**Refused outright.** The flash as a vision: no shimmer, no glow, no meaning announced, the man only working *(`plan/scene-list.md` D18.3 hazard; R26: no shimmer and no crackle)*. **The man looking up at Benal, or anything that implies two-way contact** *(`kb/worldbuilding/the-second-jump.md` §10)*. Saying how far back the man went *(`kb/worldbuilding/the-second-jump.md` §10)*. Benal digging, or going to the brow's edge in the dark. Any link between the man and the plates, which the stayers cut in year three (Q-P0BV; plates dated, not handwritten), stated outright *(`plan/scene-maps/D17.2.md`: the equation plate, the fix and the protocol plates, all dated year three)*. EP8 *(`content/epigraphs.md`: nobody may connect them in book 1)*. Benal not talking: he talks to himself through the chain and to the others about the numbers, and withholds the one thing.
