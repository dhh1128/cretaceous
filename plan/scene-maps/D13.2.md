## IN

- **`PLACE`** — the coastal plain: *nypa*-type palms, *weichselia* ferns, salt-tolerant scrub, slow water in the low places; no mangrove <!-- @WF-mangroves.exist: false --> *(`milieu-allocation.md` §2, §4)*. Open enough to see a long way.
- **`TIME`** — Day 13, from the end of the lay-up, about 1500, to camp before the light goes, about 1800 `[-]`. The afternoon's 5 km `[-]` brings the day to its 10 and them to 78 of the 80 <!-- @WF-route.overland: 80km --> *(`journey-calendar.md` §1)*.
- **`WEATHER`** — clear and warming after three days of rain *(`journey-calendar.md` §2, Day 13)*. **To each body:** Keo unsuited in sun, wet boots and the rot in his foot, sweat in the cut; Teva on the frame in a suit that cools patchily, and she sheds heat badly *(`milieu-brief.md` §9)*, so fronds are laid over her for shade `[-]`; Benal in his suit, with the insect wall coming in unfiltered *(D13.1)*. Toward the end a breeze off the sea, and with it the sandflies *(`milieu-allocation.md` §3b: coastal, breezy, sandflies at dusk)*.
- **`LIGHT`** — clear afternoon light, the first long view in days.
- **`SOUND`** — the frame's poles dragging through scrub and soft ground; the insect wall, which Benal can no longer filter *(D13.1; `plan/tech-rules.md`)*; flies at the carcass.
- **`SMELL`** — a hadrosaur carcass three days dead *(`milieu-allocation.md` §5, Day 13)*; then salt.
- **`CLOCK`** — the light: they stop and build the night's fence before it goes *(`body-and-resources.md` §2b)*, and the boys' sickness is coming around nightfall *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §4)*. **They cannot leave her**: anything that hunts would take a body lying still on a frame, so nobody goes off to forage alone and one of them is always at her `[-]`.
- **`LOAD`** — **the frame**: two saplings for poles, crosspieces, and fronds laid across them for her to lie on, lashed with the coil from the fieldpack and, when the coil runs out, with the binding off Teva's spike `[-]`. **Keo** drags it by the two pole ends, one in each hand, the far ends on the ground `[-]`. **Benal** his pack, Teva's, the fieldpack, Keo's spike, his razor-club, and **the bare shaft** of Teva's spike; the croc tooth that tipped it goes in the fieldpack, and with no cord left it cannot be rehafted `[-]`. Three skins of water *(D13.1)*.
- **`PRESENT`** — the three of them; **one distant *Tyrannosaurus rex***, once *(`milieu-allocation.md` §3)*; **one *palaeosaniwa***, three meters of monitor lizard, at the carcass *(`milieu-allocation.md` §3)*; necrophagous beetles; frogs and fish at the slow water *(`milieu-allocation.md` §3b)*.
- **`BODY`** — **Keo:** the healing running hot, no infection, eating reserves he does not have: heat with no source, strength going, a mind that loses the thread; the thigh closed and tender; cracked rib; the ankle that put him on one knee *(D13.1; `body-and-resources.md` §1, row 13)*; the mash in him, not yet due. **Teva:** down; vomiting and purging, keeping a little water and nothing else, lying on the frame looking back along the way they came *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §4)*. **Benal:** the mash in him, not yet due; no grid; the weak arm hidden.
- **`WANTS`** — `character-arcs.md` §2. **Keo, Days 12–13:** wants *to stop being the burden*; believes *he is a liability*; fears *that he has done to Teva and Benal what he did to Yara*; hides *how much pain he is in* and *that he knew the mash needed another day and did not fight for it*. Here: to get her to the coast. **Benal, Days 12–15:** wants *to keep watch*. Here: to keep the frame safe, without the sense that was his use. **Teva, Days 12–14:** wants *to get them there*. Here: to keep water down, and to be some use from the frame.
- **`RELATIONS`** — `character-arcs.md` §3, D13.2: the distant *T. rex* pays Liaso's story from D2.9; Benal says his cousin's name, the only thing any of them has the energy to say, and it brings home and the night of the theft back to Keo, and he can see it reach the other two. Dyads Days 12–14: Keo→Teva 5; Teva→Benal 7; Keo↔Benal 8. **The trio:** the bond is real and nobody has the energy for it *(`body-and-resources.md` §4, rule 5)*.
- **`REGARD`** — `character-arcs.md` §4, Days 12–15: **Keo**, pity and admiration, earned by carrying her and by dragging her without a word of blame; **Teva**, the reader fears for her.

## SPINE

**The frame**, from the last lashing to the smell of salt. It is in Keo's hands the whole scene.

## MOVES

1. **[opens] [spine]** Keo lashes the last crosspiece and the coil runs out a crosspiece short.
2. **[spine]** He takes Teva's spike, unwinds the binding off the croc tooth, and finishes the frame with it. The tooth goes in the fieldpack and Benal takes the bare shaft. Nobody discusses it. *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §6: the binding is the hafting.)*
3. **[spine] [body]** He takes up the pole ends and pulls, and the frame moves. What the healing is doing to him, from inside: heat with no source, strength going, the thread of a thought lost and found. *(`body-and-resources.md` §1.)*
4. **[world]** Frogs and a fish at the slow water, and a stonefruit the animals missed. Benal gets them, with Keo at the frame; Teva cannot keep a mouthful down; the boys eat theirs, with the mash already in them. *(`milieu-allocation.md` §3b.)*
5. **[world] [ledger]** Keo sees it first: across the plain, a long way off and not on their line, a *T. rex* standing and looking at them. It does not come. They keep walking. *(`foreshadow-and-motif.md` R13.)* `[requires D2.9 — Liaso's rex, which looked at him for an hour and did not come]`
6. **[relation]** Benal says *Liaso*, and nothing else, and it is the theft night for all three of them: the watch they got past on Liaso's song. *(`character-arcs.md` §3, D13.2.)* `[requires D3.1 — Liaso on watch, and Benal knowing his rhythm]`
7. **[body] [ledger]** A flicker, ozone, a slip in time, and he is still walking when he comes back to it. He puts it down to the healing. *(`foreshadow-and-motif.md` R26.)* `[requires D11.6 — the first flash, filed under fever]`
8. **[world]** The carcass, on their line: a hadrosaur three days dead, beetles working it. They stop by it to take beetles. *(`milieu-allocation.md` §3b, §5.)*
9. **[world]** Teva, lying on the frame facing back, sees it before anyone: the *palaeosaniwa* coming round the carcass at the people at its food, and then at the frame and the body lying still on it. She says so. Benal's suit pulses a moment later.
10. **[spine]** They drive it off badly: Keo drops the poles and takes his spike from Benal, Benal holds the bare shaft at arm's length, both of them on empty. It backs off to the carcass and stays there.
11. **[body]** From here Benal walks beside the frame with the shaft, and they keep away from anything dead.
12. **[body]** How much of himself is left, measured in steps. No blame of her in it, and no room for any. He is dragging the consequence of a thing he was right about, and he does not think it in those words.
13. **[closes]** A breeze off the sea, the sandflies with it, and salt on the air, which means the coast is close. *Camp, the fence and the boys' sickness are after the scene.* *Ending **REVEAL*** *(`plan/pacing-and-stakes.md` §8)*.

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** They have made the day's distance and smell the sea. Teva's spike is a bare shaft, its tooth in the fieldpack. The frame exists and Keo has dragged it 5 km. The rex has been seen once and will not be again. Keo has had a second flash and filed it under the healing. The lizard is behind them at the carcass.

**Worked on and did not change.** Keo's secret. Teva, who has kept nothing down.

**Left open.** Whether the boys' sickness is as light as they hope.

**Refused outright.** The rex as a set piece. Keo resenting her, even for a moment. Heroism: he does the only available thing, badly. The spike's archaea failure, which stays unspent *(`body-and-resources.md` §3c)*.
