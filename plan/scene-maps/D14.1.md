## IN

- **`PLACE`** — the coast at km 80 <!-- @WF-route.overland: 80km -->. The palms and salt scrub of the plain stop at the edge of a back-barrier mudflat, about a kilometer <!-- @WF-mudflat.width: 1km --> of gray silt that reads as ground and is not, cut by tidal creeks, crossable on foot only around low water; the firmer line runs along low shell ridges, and one creek has to be waded `[-]`. Beyond it a **barrier beach**, long and narrow: lagoon and mud on its landward side, the sea on the other, and between them one strip of sand above the waterline. They come out on it at one end; **the raft lies at the far end**, up in the dune scrub above the storm line `[-]`. The whole strip between is the turtle beach. *(`milieu-allocation.md` §2; `journey-calendar.md` §1.)*
- **`TIME`** — Day 14, from first light, about 0530, when they reach the edge of the flat after the last stretch in the dark, to the launch at about 1030 `[-]`. Low water is at about 0800 `[-]`: the flat is crossable from first light until about 0930, and after that the creeks join and it is lagoon `[-]`. The crossing takes them about two hours; the beach and the raft the rest `[-]`. *(D13.2 state closure; `body-and-resources.md` §2b: one of the two days the doctrine is broken.)*
- **`WEATHER`** — hot, glassy, no wind at all; by the end, a wall of black on the horizon *(`journey-calendar.md` §2, Day 14)*.
- **`LIGHT`** — flat and brilliant off the flat and the sea.
- **`SOUND`** — no wind, so every sound carries. Benal's hearing has no dampening since Day 11 *(`plan/tech-rules.md`)*: the turtle beach comes into him whole, which hurts and is also how he reads it.
- **`SMELL`** — the mudflat; then the beach, turtles and churned sand and blood where the crocs are working.
- **`CLOCK`** — two at once. The tide: the flat closes at about 0930, and after that it is lagoon with crocs in it `[-]`. The sky: the calm is a cyclone coming, and they have to be off the beach before it. *(`journey-calendar.md` §2; scene-list D14.1.)*
- **`LOAD`** — Teva her own pack again `[-]`; Benal his pack and the fieldpack, with his board in it, the croc tooth, the kindler; Keo his spike `[-]`. Teva the bare shaft of her spike, Benal his razor-club *(D13.2)*. Three skins of the Day 13 rainwater, the last fresh water they will have *(D13.1)*; Keo's tooth-knife and Teva's knife on their belts `[-]`. The frame was left at the km 78 camp *(D13.2)*.
- **`PRESENT`** — the three of them. Sea turtles hauling out by day to lay in numbers, as an arribada does `[-]`; crocs working the turtles at the water's edge; *hesperornis* diving in the shallows and staying under a long time; *muzquizopteryx* soaring, small, harmless *(`milieu-allocation.md` §3)*.
- **`BODY`** — **Teva:** on her feet, the poisoning healed and paid for over Night 13; she keeps a splitting headache *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §4, assumption 6; `character-arcs.md` §1)*; her suit's cooling patchy in a glassy heat. **Keo:** the thigh closed and tender, rib, the ankle bad since the burrow; the mash's night behind him; thin and slow. **Benal:** the mash's night behind him; capacitor dead and untold; the arm weak; hearing unfiltered. All three hungry: the dawn mash on Day 13, then the afternoon's frogs, fish, stonefruit and beetles, which Teva could not keep down, and nothing since *(`body-and-resources.md` §3, Days 13–14; D13.2)*.
- **`WANTS`** — `character-arcs.md` §2. **Benal, Days 12–15:** wants *to keep watch*; fears *being right too late*. Here: to get them through, and he is the only one who can. **Teva, Days 12–14:** wants *to get them there*. **Keo, Days 12–13:** to stop being the burden.

## SPINE

**The way to the raft**: across the flat, then through the turtle beach to where it lies, then into the water.

## MOVES

1. **[opens] [world]** First light at the edge of the plain, the dark stretch behind them. The air is wrong — hot, glassy, no wind — and Benal notices it before anyone looks at the sky.
2. **[world]** The mudflat, gray and flat to the barrier beach, the tide going out. It looks like ground.
3. **[spine]** They cross it by the shell ridges. Between them it takes them to the shin and then the knee, and they cannot stop; Benal has the tide in his head and says how long they have. At the creek Keo holds a hand back to Teva and she takes it, and neither says anything *(Keo→Teva 5, changed and quieter)*. *(Hazard: the flat as scenery. It is crossed now because the tide is going and the storm is coming.)*
4. Benal says, looking back at the plain from the beach, that they could not walk home now, not on what they have left. Keo says they are not walking anywhere; the raft is the only way left. Nobody argues. *(`body-and-resources.md` §1: someone says it on Day 14.)*
5. **[world]** The barrier beach, and the length of it: turtles hauling out by the hundred along the whole strip, digging and laying, and crocs at the water's edge taking them as they come and go. The lagoon behind has its own. The raft is at the far end. The only way to it is along the strip, through the middle of it.
6. Keo and Teva look at it and see no way through. Benal says wait, and listens.
7. **[spine]** Benal finds the corridor by ear and nose — which crocs are in the water and which are on the sand, which have just fed and lie heavy, where the turtles are coming up and where they are going back down, so where the crocs will be next — and they follow him without asking, along the upper beach, stopping when he stops. He could not explain it if they asked.
8. **[world]** Ovos: where a turtle is laying on their line, they take the eggs as they drop, by hand, and eat them there, raw, and put more in the fieldpack `[-]`. The last real meal. *(`body-and-resources.md` §3, Day 14; `milieu-allocation.md` §3b: the crocs are the cost.)*
9. **[relation]** At the far end, clear of it, Teva asks him how he did it. He tries to say and cannot, quite. *(`character-arcs.md` §3, D14.1; Teva→Benal 7.)*
10. **[world]** A shadow over the sand and Benal flinches; it is a *muzquizopteryx*, small and soaring, and harmless. Out past the surf line a *hesperornis* goes under and stays under a long time.
11. **[body] [ledger]** Benal's first flash: a sound that repeats exactly, too even and too hard-edged for anything alive, and the air after lightning with something under it he has no name for. Then it is gone. He tells nobody. *(`foreshadow-and-motif.md` R26: only Benal's own words.)* `[requires D10.3 — the records say the founders saw lights and smelled things that were not there]`
12. **[spine]** The raft, up in the scrub where the First Walk story says the founders left it: founder polymer floats on an alloy frame, half buried in sand, decking split, one float cracked *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3)* <!-- @WF-raft.origin: firstwalk -->. They dig it out and drag it down the beach to the water, through the low glassy surf, and get on with the packs between them. They paddle with two blades of its split rail, broken off, and pole with the spike shafts while they can still touch bottom `[-]`. Nobody remarks that it is old-tech and still there. *(`plan/tech-rules.md`, archaea failsafe: founder-era, never wrapped; nobody remarks on it.)*
13. Teva gives them the heading for Genesis. Nobody can see it: the heat haze on the glassy sea takes everything past a few kilometers `[-]`.
14. **[closes]** The raft on the water, and on the horizon the black, nearer than it was. *Ending **THREAT*** *(`plan/pacing-and-stakes.md` §8)*.

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** They are on the water, on Teva's heading, with the eggs in them and a few in the fieldpack, the last food they have. They have followed Benal without asking, and Teva has asked him how. He has had a flash and kept it. Someone has said the walk home is impossible.

**Worked on and did not change.** Benal's capacitor, still untold. Teva's headache.

**Left open.** Whether the raft holds. Whether they reach Genesis ahead of the storm.

**Refused outright.** The flat as scenery. Benal's solution as cleverness: he hears and smells his way and cannot explain it. Anyone remarking on old-tech that has not dissolved. The spike's archaea failure *(`body-and-resources.md` §3c)*: not spent here.

## State closure

**Against D13.2:** camp at km 78, the boys sick from nightfall into the small hours, Teva healed over Night 13, the last stretch walked in the dark before dawn, the frame left at the camp — all in the gap.

**Against D14.2:** the afternoon of paddling, about eight hours, on her heading, in glassy heat, with the black growing, is in the gap; by dusk they have made about two thirds of the way, perhaps 4 or 5 km out, and still cannot see Genesis `[-]`. D14.2 opens at dusk.
