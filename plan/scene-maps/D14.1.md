## IN

- **`PLACE`** — the coast at km 80 <!-- @WF-route.overland: 80km -->. The palms and salt scrub of the plain stop at a back-barrier mudflat, about a kilometer <!-- @WF-mudflat.width: 1km --> of gray silt, soft between low shell ridges that are firm underfoot, crossable only around low water `[-]`. Beyond it a long, narrow **barrier beach**, lagoon behind and sea in front. At its far end **the rock**: a ridge of hardened dune rock ten to fifteen meters high <!-- @WF-firstwalk.rock.height: 10-15m -->, the one high thing on the coast, with the First Walk's boats on a ledge under an overhang on its landward face, four or five meters above the beach <!-- @WF-firstwalk.ledge.height: 4-5m --> at the top of a sand slope from the rock's end, weighted with stones *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3)*. Heat haze hides it until they are close `[-]`.
- **`TIME`** — Day 14, from first light, about 0530, when they reach the edge of the flat after the last stretch in the dark, to the launch at about 1030 `[-]`. Low water is at about 0800 `[-]`: the flat is crossable from first light until about 0930, and after that the creeks join and it is lagoon `[-]`. The crossing takes them about two hours; the beach and the boat the rest `[-]`. *(D13.2 state closure; `body-and-resources.md` §2b: one of the two days the doctrine is broken.)*
- **`WEATHER`** — hot, glassy, no wind at all; by the end, a wall of black on the horizon *(`journey-calendar.md` §2, Day 14)*.
- **`LIGHT`** — flat and brilliant off the flat and the sea.
- **`SOUND`** — no wind, so every sound carries: the surf's slow boom, the turtles' long sighs, the tick and pop of the draining flat `[-]`. Benal's hearing has no dampening since Day 11 *(`plan/tech-rules.md`)*: the turtle beach comes into him whole, which hurts and is also how he reads it.
- **`SMELL`** — the mudflat, a rotten-egg stink wherever a foot breaks through the silt to the black mud under it `[-]`; then the beach: brine, turtles and churned sand, and blood where the crocs are working *(`plan/milieu-allocation.md` §5: brine, Day 14)*.
- **`SENSES`** — `[-]` **Hearing, the insect wall gone:** on the coast at first light there is none, for the first time since the forest closed over them on Day 3 *(`plan/milieu-allocation.md` §5: the insect wall, thereafter only when it stops)*, and what was under it comes to him one sound at a time, each in its own place; he counts them. **Hearing and touch, the surf:** a boom under a long hiss, and with each boom a shiver in the sand that reaches him through his boots before the sound does; the booms come slow, with long gaps between, on a morning with no wind, and he counts the gaps without knowing what they mean (a cyclone's swell runs out ahead of it, `plan/journey-calendar.md` §2). **Smell:** the flat's black mud stinks like the springs of Day 12, which he places at once; the sea smells of salt and of almost nothing alive, which is strange to him and no shock *(`plan/scene-list.md` D14.2: smell is no shock in a sea that is all brine)*, and it is what the eggs taste of. **Touch, dry sand:** above the tide line every step slides back half its length; the sand he knows is the river's bars, wet and firm underfoot.

## SPINE

**The way to the boat**: across the flat, down the beach to the rock, and into the water.

## MOVES

1. **[opens] [world]** First light at the edge of the plain, the dark stretch behind them. The air is wrong — hot, glassy, no wind — and Benal notices it before anyone looks at the sky. There is no insect wall here, and under where it would be he can hear each thing in its place `[-]`.
2. **[world]** The mudflat, silt shining wet to the barrier beach, the tide going out. It looks like ground. The creeks all run one way, out, and that is the tide, a word from the records, happening `[-]`.
3. **[spine]** They cross on the shell ridges. In one gap between ridges Keo's bad leg goes into the mud to the knee and sticks, and the other two pull him out. Benal watches the creeks draining and says how long they have. Keo holds a hand back to Teva at the last gap and she takes it, and neither says anything *(Keo→Teva 5)*.
4. On the beach, looking back at the plain, Benal says they could not walk home now on what they have left. Keo says they are not walking anywhere; the boat is the only way. Nobody argues. *(`body-and-resources.md` §1.)*
5. **[world]** The beach runs on into haze both ways. Turtles are hauling out along it to lay; crocs lie at the waterline. They keep high and give them room, in the dry sand above the tide line, where every step slides back `[-]`. In front of them the sea, which nobody alive has seen *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §5)*: the swell standing up and breaking with a boom he feels in the sand before he hears it, and the hiss going back; the air off it tastes of salt `[-]`.
6. **[spine]** Nobody can see the rock. Benal stops and listens, and says it is that way: the swell is breaking on stone, not sand, far down the beach. He walks, and they follow him without asking.
7. **[world]** Ovos: a turtle laying high on the beach on their line; they take the eggs as they drop, eat them raw, and put more in the fieldpack `[-]`. The last real meal. *(`body-and-resources.md` §3, Day 14.)*
8. **[world]** A shadow over the sand and Benal flinches; it is a *muzquizopteryx*, small and harmless. Out past the surf a *hesperornis* goes under and stays under a long time.
9. **[world]** The rock comes up out of the haze where he said it would be.
10. **[relation]** Teva asks him how he knew, and he tells her: stone sounds different. For once somebody wanted to hear it. *(`character-arcs.md` §3, D14.1; Teva→Benal 7.)*
11. **[body] [ledger]** Benal's first flash: a sound that repeats exactly, too even and too hard-edged for anything alive, and the air after lightning. Then it is gone. He tells nobody. *(`foreshadow-and-motif.md` R26.)* `[requires D10.3 — the records say the founders saw lights and smelled things that were not there]`
12. **[spine]** Up the sand slope at the rock's end to the ledge under the overhang on the landward face, and the boat on it under stones, its floats half buried in drift sand, where the First Walk story says: about 3 m by 2 <!-- @WF-boat.size: 3x2m -->, two hollow polymer floats under an alloy frame and deck, about 100–120 kg <!-- @WF-boat.mass: 100-120kg -->, decking split, one float cracked, and the outboard at the stern hanging loose where the bolts of its mount have rusted away, so it is no use to them *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3)* <!-- @WF-boat.origin: firstwalk -->. They lift the stones off, each one a person can handle, scoop the sand from around the floats with their hands, slide it down the slope on its floats, and drag it a few tens of meters round the rock's end to the water; Keo's knee and ankle cost him on the slope and the drag `[-]`. They break two blades off its rail for paddles and pole with the spike shafts while they can touch bottom `[-]`. Nobody remarks that it is still there.
13. From the top of the rock, above the shimmer on the sand, they see Genesis for the first time: an island with a pale crown standing up out of the glassy water and the station on it, about 5 km. Teva gives the heading anyway. *(Q-SJBY.)*
14. **[closes]** The boat on the water, and on the horizon the black, nearer than it was. *Ending **THREAT*** *(`plan/pacing-and-stakes.md` §8)*.

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** They are on the water, on Teva's heading, with the eggs in them and a few in the fieldpack, the last food they have. They have followed Benal's ears without asking, and Teva has asked him how he knew. He has had a flash and kept it. Someone has said the walk home is impossible.

**Worked on and did not change.** Benal's capacitor, still untold. Teva's headache.

**Left open.** Whether the boat holds. Whether they reach Genesis ahead of the storm.

**Refused outright.** The flat as scenery. The crocs as a set piece: they are background. Anyone remarking on old-tech that has not dissolved. The spike's archaea failure *(`body-and-resources.md` §3c)*: not spent here.

## State closure

**Against D13.2:** camp at km 78, the boys sick from nightfall into the small hours, Teva healed over Night 13, the last stretch walked in the dark before dawn, the frame left at the camp — all in the gap.

**Against D14.2:** the crossing, a few hours of a heavy boat on two broken blades in glassy heat, with Genesis ahead and the black growing behind, is in the gap; they reach the reef ring in the middle of the afternoon `[-]`. D14.2 opens there.
