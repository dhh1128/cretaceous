## IN

- **`PLACE`** — the coast at km 80 <!-- @WF-route.overland: 80km -->. The palms and salt scrub of the plain stop at a back-barrier mudflat, about a kilometer <!-- @WF-mudflat.width: 1km --> of gray silt, soft between low shell ridges that are firm underfoot, crossable only around low water `[-]`. Beyond it a long, narrow **barrier beach**, lagoon behind and sea in front. At its far end **the rock**: a ridge of pale dune rock a few meters high, the one high thing on the coast, with the First Walk's rafts wedged in a cleft in its seaward face *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3)*. Heat haze hides it until they are close `[-]`.
- **`TIME`** — Day 14, from first light, about 0530, when they reach the edge of the flat after the last stretch in the dark, to the launch at about 1030 `[-]`. Low water is at about 0800 `[-]`: the flat is crossable from first light until about 0930, and after that the creeks join and it is lagoon `[-]`. The crossing takes them about two hours; the beach and the raft the rest `[-]`. *(D13.2 state closure; `body-and-resources.md` §2b: one of the two days the doctrine is broken.)*
- **`WEATHER`** — hot, glassy, no wind at all; by the end, a wall of black on the horizon *(`journey-calendar.md` §2, Day 14)*.
- **`LIGHT`** — flat and brilliant off the flat and the sea.
- **`SOUND`** — no wind, so every sound carries. Benal's hearing has no dampening since Day 11 *(`plan/tech-rules.md`)*: the turtle beach comes into him whole, which hurts and is also how he reads it.
- **`SMELL`** — the mudflat; then the beach, turtles and churned sand and blood where the crocs are working.
- **`CLOCK`** — the tide: the flat closes at about 0930 `[-]`. The sky: the calm is a cyclone coming, and they have to be on the water before it *(`journey-calendar.md` §2)*.
- **`LOAD`** — Teva her own pack again `[-]`; Benal his pack and the fieldpack, with his board in it, the croc tooth, the kindler; Keo his spike `[-]`. Teva the bare shaft of her spike, Benal his razor-club *(D13.2)*. Three skins of the Day 13 rainwater, the last fresh water they will have *(D13.1)*; Keo's tooth-knife and Teva's knife on their belts `[-]`. The frame was left at the km 78 camp *(D13.2)*.
- **`PRESENT`** — the three of them. Sea turtles hauling out by day to lay, as some do in numbers `[-]`; crocs lying at the waterline, given room *(`milieu-allocation.md` §3)*; *hesperornis* diving in the shallows; *muzquizopteryx* soaring, small and harmless.

All three hungry: the dawn mash on Day 13, then the afternoon's frogs, fish, stonefruit and beetles, which Teva could not keep down, and nothing since *(`body-and-resources.md` §3, Days 13–14; D13.2)*.

## SPINE

**The way to the raft**: across the flat, down the beach to the rock, and into the water.

## MOVES

1. **[opens] [world]** First light at the edge of the plain, the dark stretch behind them. The air is wrong — hot, glassy, no wind — and Benal notices it before anyone looks at the sky.
2. **[world]** The mudflat, gray to the barrier beach, the tide going out. It looks like ground.
3. **[spine]** They cross on the shell ridges. In one gap between ridges Keo's bad leg goes into the mud to the knee and sticks, and the other two pull him out. Benal watches the creeks draining and says how long they have. Keo holds a hand back to Teva at the last gap and she takes it, and neither says anything *(Keo→Teva 5)*.
4. On the beach, looking back at the plain, Benal says they could not walk home now on what they have left. Keo says they are not walking anywhere; the raft is the only way. Nobody argues. *(`body-and-resources.md` §1.)*
5. **[world]** The beach runs on into haze both ways. Turtles are hauling out along it to lay; crocs lie at the waterline. They keep high and give them room.
6. **[spine]** Nobody can see the rock. Benal stops and listens, and says it is that way: the swell is breaking on stone, not sand, far down the beach. He walks, and they follow him without asking.
7. **[world]** Ovos: a turtle laying high on the beach on their line; they take the eggs as they drop, eat them raw, and put more in the fieldpack `[-]`. The last real meal. *(`body-and-resources.md` §3, Day 14.)*
8. **[world]** A shadow over the sand and Benal flinches; it is a *muzquizopteryx*, small and harmless. Out past the surf a *hesperornis* goes under and stays under a long time.
9. **[world]** The rock comes up out of the haze where he said it would be.
10. **[relation]** Teva asks him how he knew, and he tells her: stone sounds different. For once somebody wanted to hear it. *(`character-arcs.md` §3, D14.1; Teva→Benal 7.)*
11. **[body] [ledger]** Benal's first flash: a sound that repeats exactly, too even and too hard-edged for anything alive, and the air after lightning. Then it is gone. He tells nobody. *(`foreshadow-and-motif.md` R26.)* `[requires D10.3 — the records say the founders saw lights and smelled things that were not there]`
12. **[spine]** The cleft in the seaward face, half full of sand and wrack, and the raft wedged in it under stones, where the First Walk story says: polymer floats on an alloy frame, decking split, one float cracked *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3)* <!-- @WF-raft.origin: firstwalk -->. They dig it out, lever it free and drag it to the water. They break two blades off its rail for paddles and pole with the spike shafts while they can touch bottom `[-]`. Nobody remarks that it is still there.
13. Teva gives them the heading for Genesis. Nobody can see it through the haze `[-]`.
14. **[closes]** The raft on the water, and on the horizon the black, nearer than it was. *Ending **THREAT*** *(`plan/pacing-and-stakes.md` §8)*.

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** They are on the water, on Teva's heading, with the eggs in them and a few in the fieldpack, the last food they have. They have followed Benal's ears without asking, and Teva has asked him how he knew. He has had a flash and kept it. Someone has said the walk home is impossible.

**Worked on and did not change.** Benal's capacitor, still untold. Teva's headache.

**Left open.** Whether the raft holds. Whether they reach Genesis ahead of the storm.

**Refused outright.** The flat as scenery. The crocs as a set piece: they are background. Anyone remarking on old-tech that has not dissolved. The spike's archaea failure *(`body-and-resources.md` §3c)*: not spent here.

## State closure

**Against D13.2:** camp at km 78, the boys sick from nightfall into the small hours, Teva healed over Night 13, the last stretch walked in the dark before dawn, the frame left at the camp — all in the gap.

**Against D14.2:** the afternoon of paddling, about eight hours, on her heading, in glassy heat, with the black growing, is in the gap; by dusk they have made about two thirds of the way, perhaps 4 or 5 km out, and still cannot see Genesis `[-]`. D14.2 opens at dusk.
