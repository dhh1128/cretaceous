# Invention ledger — scene 1.1

2026-09-10. Per `process/scene-build-runbook.md` phase 4.

**Two changes to how this pass runs**, from Daniel's correction of the first attempt (2026-09-10):

1. **Questions go to him one at a time, in dependency order** — the one whose answer implies the most about the others first. A list of twenty questions delivered at once makes him answer ones that later answers would have settled.
2. **Declining to specify something is not a question.** It needs his approval only when leaving it unspecified has a consequence somebody could get wrong. Everything else is a decision, recorded in §3 below so the phase-7 invention audit has something to check against, and not put to him.

The plant, at beat 5: Omya gives back a long stretch of protocol word-perfect — the part drilled into her decades ago — and Rano lets it past without pausing because it isn't the part he needs. Nobody remarks. Then 2.4 detonates it: she recites the whole sky perfectly and cannot hold a fold, and the reader thinks *she did that in the Vitarium too.* Clinically correct (remote rehearsed memory outlasts recent complex memory, `milieu-brief.md:142`) and it needs no invention. |
| Q4 | The close: Teva goes to the tray rather than to her grandmother and puts the fallen keys back in order. | **Answered — yes**, and it follows the dark vessel. Image first, then implement. |
| Q5 | Does a culture visibly go dark inside the scene, so the count at the open is larger than the count at the close? | **Answered — yes**, at beat 19, unremarked, immediately before the close. |

## 3. Decided without asking

Recorded for the phase-7 invention audit, which checks every concrete fact in the draft against canon or this ledger. Each of these is a decision **not to specify** something, and none of them has a consequence that another scene could get wrong. If any turns out to matter, it becomes a queue item.

| decision | why it needs no approval |
|---|---|
| The elders are unnumbered and unnamed | Sila and Hesh are introduced at 3.1; naming anyone here would spend them early. Nothing downstream depends on a count. |
| No figure is ever given for the vessels | Teva holds a count and never says it. A specific number would have to be consistent across the novel forever, for no gain. |
| Teva's age is not stated | `milieu-brief.md:307` gives 16–18 as a range and no scene needs it pinned. |
| Daven is not mentioned | He is `[provisional]` in `minor-characters.md:33` and every scene that needs him is later. |
| Teva touches nobody | `character-arcs.md:112` allows one functional touch per act; this is not the place to spend it. |
| She does not cry, and the narration does not say she doesn't | Stating an absence is the redundant-labeling tell, `ai-tells-blacklist.md` §9. |
| Omya never says Teva's name | Recognition crosses her face and nothing lands; 2.4 is where she speaks to Teva. |
| The scene never states what Teva has decided | `style-canon.md:539` — a summarizing sentence would destroy the ending. |
| The wound is the risen line up the arm, once, at distance | `writing-style.md:144` — no graphic detail for a YA audience. |
| Elana's anaphora (*"She did not die from a croc…"*) is held for 3.1 | `voice-sheets.md:63` attaches the device to no scene; 3.1 already owns *"just words, just air."* Using it twice in one act spends her. |
| *"Precision, Teva"* is quoted as a fragment, not whole | 2.4 delivers it live and should be the first complete hearing. |
| No new colony vocabulary; the vector is *the rust-red* casually and *the vector* precisely | `lingo.md:81` — the list is closed and coining is a defect. |
| Rano does not sweep the gels off the tray | That gesture exists only in `content/superseded/01.1.md`, which is set aside, and it is theatrical for a man defined as worn through. |
| The gel keys do not deform on the floor | G4 makes them stable inside this chamber. They are out of order, not dissolving. |
| Epigraph 1 stays in `content/epigraphs.md` and out of the scene file | Filing, not fiction. *(Separately: `scene-list.md:18` still points epigraph 1 at the superseded text, which `content/epigraphs.md:46` replaced. Reported to canon-coord.)* |
