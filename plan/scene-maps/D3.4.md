1. **[opens] [spine]** **Building the fence**, the three of them, hands busy. — *`body-and-resources.md` §2b: spined cycad fronds points out, green stakes cut with the tooth-knives, the rock on one side. **Keo instructs**, which is his register *(`voice-sheets.md`)*, and they do it well enough.* **~12%.**

2. **[spine] [humor] [ledger]** **Noli inside the half-built fence, and she has decided on Benal.** — ***L3.*** *She lies down against his legs while he works and will not move; he stands frozen with a stake in each hand. **Keo pushes *good-pack-good-food* at her** and she gives him nothing back but contentment. **Her opinion arrives through her body**, and it is that Benal is hers *(`humor-plan.md`: grievances and wrong opinions, confidently held)*.* **~15%.**

3. **[spine]** **Teva gels the stakes, and Noli leaves.** — *`body-and-resources.md` §0: on the fence the gel means Noli will not come near the camp after dark. **Her grievance**: she rises, looks at what they have done to the camp, and goes over the fence into the dark, offended *(read off her body)*. **Keo does not argue this time**; he loses that argument at D3.1 and here he only watches her go.* **~8%.**

4. **[spine]** **The fire, and the frogs on it, and the rations shared out.** — *Small fire on scraped ground *(§2b)*. **Keo hands the food round, and nobody holds back.** **He starts to count what is left and does not finish the sum** — not because he is afraid of it but because the night is good and the sum can wait *(`scene-list.md`: the arithmetic nobody has done yet)*. *(Most of the rations are eaten on Day 3, `body-and-resources.md` §3; the scene does not make that anyone's fault.)** **~15%.**
