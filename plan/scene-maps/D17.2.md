Forward map per `process/methodology-theory.md` §4. Moves carry events and changes, not wording; how a line is said is the drafter's. The band is a floor for this map's material, never for the prose. **Phase 4 is a hard stop** — the ledger at the foot goes to Daniel before any prose exists.

Purpose **DISCOVER**, ending **REVEAL**. Ladders EM9 P4 S8 SP10. **SU3, and the species summit, the only 10 in the column** *(`plan/pacing-and-stakes.md` §6)*. Physical stakes are nil; the dread is intellectual and climbs with each thing put on the bench. ## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** All three know: the flashes are real and are this place; another group went further back from here; the impact is at most seventeen years <!-- @WF-impact.window.max: 17y --> off; the upgrades are the cure; what returns is the platform; and only the people who came could have gone home. Keo has told them about the mark. Teva knows what the Watch was for. Benal has seen that the wrapping is the Enclave's own. His mathematics was the only knowledge that mattered.

**Worked on and did not change.** Nobody has said *lie*, yet, aloud. Teva's face at the moon table.

**Left open.** What the others will make of it. How far the stayers went. Why they went back instead of home.

**Refused outright.** Benal explaining: no lecture, fragments only, and the reader half a step behind *(`kb/worldbuilding/benals-equation.md`, rule 1)*. A document that explains the physics *(`kb/worldbuilding/the-second-jump.md` §10: he deduces it first)*. Stating how far back. Any implication that anything was sent or received across time <!-- @WF-timecrossing.possible: false -->. Pointing at what is missing from the apparatus. Anyone remarking on the wigs line.
