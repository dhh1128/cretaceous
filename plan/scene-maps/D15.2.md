## SPINE

**The doors**, one after another, along the store row, until the light goes.

## MOVES

1. **[opens]** They walk along the bank under the crown's face, slowly, Keo between them, the wind still on their wet backs. The face goes up over them, too steep for anyone, and the station they saw from the rock is on top of it, out of sight.
2. **[world]** The store row close to, where the bank meets the low ground: walls standing, salt-white, two high, the upper tier's windows gone, the low ground beyond under water. It is the place. Teva brought them here.
3. **[spine]** The first door: a heavy slab that slides in a track, with a plate beside it the size of a hand. Teva presses the plate; nothing. There is nothing on this side of it to take hold of `[-]`. Keo puts his shoulder to it. It does not move. They talk it through as they go, each in their register `[-]`: Keo telling them to try the next one, Benal saying what the door is not doing, Teva asking what it would want.
4. **[spine]** The second, the same kind: it has slid a hand's width open, long ago, and its track is packed solid with salt and grit `[-]`. They get their fingers in the gap and pull, all three, until Keo's rib stops him and Benal's arm stops him. It gives nothing. *(The failure is physical and stupid, not a puzzle: scene-list D15.2.)*
5. **[body]** Her hands are bleeding from the edge. The headache. She thinks of the reef in the dark and the gap she could not feel, and that the reason she could not feel it was a bag of mash she would not wait a night for.
6. **[relation] [turn — TEVA]** Keo says they will find another way in. Teva says, flat, *we'll be fine*, the old line, and it breaks in the middle and she cannot finish it. Nobody says anything to her. *(`character-arcs.md` §3, D15.2.)* `[requires D11.5 — "We'll be—" / "Don't", the old line refused]`
7. **[world]** Round the row's end the ground drops into flood, and the flood runs on round the crown. Teva says the founders will have come ashore where the water is quiet and gone up from there, round the far side `[-]`; the way there is water. They do not go down to it.
8. **[world]** The light is going. At the row's landward end the storms have shoved the last top module askew on the one below, and its end door is gone: a dark opening above a bank of storm rubble heaped against the stack `[-]`.
9. Benal says they need anywhere with a roof before dark, and somewhere off the ground, and points at the opening.
10. **[closes] [world]** The light going, and the wind cold on their wet skin, and nothing to make a fire with: the kindler went with the fieldpack *(D14.2)*. *Ending **THREAT***.

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** They have reached Genesis and could not get in. They know the station is up on the crown and the way round to it is flood. They have seen the opening. Teva has said the old line and it broke. Her hands are cut. Night is coming.

**Worked on and did not change.** The doors. The headache. Her decision on Day 12, still hers.

**Left open.** Where they will shelter.

**Refused outright.** A puzzle: they fail at doors, they do not solve one. Anyone blaming her. Comfort that works. Wading: the flooded ground is left.

## State closure

**Against D15.1:** the morning after the storm, the three together, the water drunk.

**Against D15.3:** evening; they need shelter before dark, and the only open way in is the askew top module at the row's end, above the rubble and the flooded low ground.
