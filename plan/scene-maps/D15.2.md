Flooded low ground lies between it and the rest of the station, so the far side, and the powered block D16.1 gets into, cannot be reached today without wading `[-]` *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3: built to be worked in, much of it standing; what has gone is the parts with moving pieces; what has flooded is the low ground)*. **Its doors were power-driven, and this block's power is dead**: its arrays are gone from its roof `[-]`; the power that survived is elsewhere on the spit.

## SPINE

**The doors**, one after another, along the seaward face, until the wind comes back.

## MOVES

1. **[opens]** On the way up the spit Benal tells them what the calm is: the middle of the storm, and the other half is coming. They have until it gets here to be inside.
2. **[world]** The station close to: walls standing, salt-white, the glass of a high row of windows gone, the low ground round it under water. It is the place. Teva brought them here.
3. **[spine]** The first door: a heavy slab that slides in a track, with a plate beside it the size of a hand. Teva presses the plate; nothing. There is no handle, because it was never meant to be opened by hand. Keo puts his shoulder to it. It does not move.
4. **[spine]** The second, the same kind: it has slid a hand's width open, long ago, and its track is packed solid with salt and grit `[-]`. They get their fingers in the gap and pull, all three, until Keo's rib stops him and Benal's arm stops him. It gives nothing. *(The failure is physical and stupid, not a puzzle: scene-list D15.2.)*
5. **[body]** Her hands are bleeding from the edge. The headache. She thinks of the reef in the dark and the gap she could not feel, and that the reason she could not feel it was a bag of mash she would not wait a night for.
6. **[relation] [turn — TEVA]** Keo says they will find another way in. Teva says, flat, *we'll be fine*, the old line, and it breaks in the middle and she cannot finish it. Nobody says anything to her. *(`character-arcs.md` §3, D15.2.)* `[requires D11.5 — "We'll be—" / "Don't", the old line refused]`
7. **[world]** Round the block's end the ground drops into flood: the rest of the station is beyond it, and the way there is water. They do not go down to it.
8. **[world]** The light changes. The birds are gone. The wall of cloud has come over the sun. In the block's landward end, above a slope of fallen wall, there is a break high up, dark inside.
9. Benal says they need anywhere with a roof, now, and points at the break.
10. **[closes] [world]** The first of the wind, from the other side, and the light going. *Ending **THREAT***.

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** They have reached Genesis and could not get in. They know the rest of the station is beyond flood. They have seen the break. Teva has said the old line and it broke. Her hands are cut. The eye is over.

**Worked on and did not change.** The doors. The headache. Her decision on Day 12, still hers.

**Left open.** Where they will shelter.

**Refused outright.** A puzzle: they fail at doors, they do not solve one. Anyone blaming her. Comfort that works. Wading: the flooded ground is left.

## State closure

**Against D15.1:** the eye, the three together, the water drunk.

**Against D15.3:** the back wall arrives; they need shelter at once, and the only open way in is high in a broken wall above the flooded low ground.
