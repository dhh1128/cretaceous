## IN

- **`PLACE`** — the mainland side of Genesis, where the bank of D15.1 lies *(D14.2 move 3)*. Between the bank and **the crown** lies the low ground, and the crown stands up beyond it: pale dune limestone twenty to thirty meters high <!-- @WF-genesis.crown.height: 20-30m -->, its steep face turned to the open sea on the far side, and on this side a gentle slope, the side the founders landed on and left from `[-]`, with the station's banked modules on top *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3)*. **The plant house**, the one thing standing they can reach from the bank: the founders' desalination plant house, coral concrete with glass-fiber rebar, poured in the founders' first months over the intake and the machinery, under a vaulted roof *(Q-PMHV)*. After two hundred and six years <!-- @WF-transit.elapsed: 206y --> one end of the vault has fallen; the steel doors and the plant machinery are lumps of rust, so the doorway stands open; the floor is under the surge; and the raised walkway where the membranes sat runs along the wall above the water, roofed by the part of the vault that still stands. Seabirds roost on the walkway; they roost and do not nest, so there are no eggs `[-]` *(Q-D9H0: steel is rust; concrete lasts)*. It sits on the bank's landward edge, where the bank meets the low ground, its seaward end over the intake `[-]`; its floor is sunk below the bank, so the doorway opens onto a concrete sill above the water, and a concrete stair goes up the wall from the sill to the walkway, its steel rail rusted away `[-]`. Between the house and the crown the low ground, where the landing was and the way up to the crown starts *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3)*, is all under water today; nobody wades it, so the way up, and the powered module D16.1 gets into, cannot be reached without wading; the sea, still heaving on the reef after the storm, keeps driving water in over the low ground and holding it under, and as the swell drops it drains off through the porous ground overnight `[-]` *(`journey-calendar.md` §2, Day 15: the sea still heaving on the reef)* *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3: built to be worked in, much of it standing; what has gone is the parts with moving pieces; what has flooded is the low ground)*.

## SPINE

**The plant house**, from walking in to the light going: nothing in it, and the crown out of reach.

## MOVES

1. **[opens]** They walk along the bank, slowly, Keo between them, the wind still on their wet backs. Across the flooded low ground the crown stands up, an easy slope on this side, bare to its brow; from down here the brow hides whatever is on top, and nobody thinks yet of what they saw from the rock `[-]`. Outside the reef the sea is still heaving, brown-green, white along the ring: the first time it is hers to look at, and it is what took the boat `[-]`.
2. **[world]** The plant house close to, where the bank meets the low ground: a long vault of pale coral concrete, salt-white, one end of it fallen in, rust run down the wall from the doorway. It is the place. Teva brought them here.
3. **[spine]** They walk straight in. There is no door: the doors are a crust of rust on the sill `[-]`. Under the vault the floor is gone under the surge, brown water slapping round lumps of rust standing up out of it that were machines; along the wall, a walkway above the water, and a stair up to it; daylight at the fallen end. Birds go up off the walkway. Every sound comes back off the vault, theirs too `[-]`. They talk as they look, each in their register `[-]`: Keo asking where everything is; Benal saying what the lumps were, a plant for making water from the sea, from the records, and nothing more `[-]`; Teva asking where the founders kept what they came for.
4. **[spine]** They go up the stair to the walkway, Keo on his hands for the ankle, and find nothing: bare concrete, rows of rusted stubs where something was mounted `[-]`, bird mess sharp in the throat `[-]`, and no eggs, because the birds roost here and do not nest `[-]`. Nothing to eat, nothing to make a fire with, nothing anyone wrote on. *(scene-list D15.2: they find almost nothing.)*
5. **[body]** The headache. She thinks of the reef in the dark and the gap she could not feel, and that the reason she could not feel it was a bag of mash she would not wait a night for.
6. **[world]** Past the house's end the ground drops into flood, and the flood runs all the way to the foot of the crown's slope. Teva says the founders will have come ashore on this side and gone up from here, across that ground `[-]`; today the way is water. They do not go down to it.
7. **[relation] [turn — TEVA]** Keo says they will find another way up. Teva says, flat, *we'll be fine*, the old line, and it breaks in the middle and she cannot finish it. Nobody says anything to her. *(`character-arcs.md` §3, D15.2.)* `[requires D11.5 — "We'll be—" / "Don't", the old line refused]`
8. **[world]** They wait out the afternoon on the bank for the flood to go off the low ground, and it does not `[-]`. Nothing bites; the wind keeps the fliers down `[-]`. The light is going. Benal says they need anywhere with a roof before dark, and somewhere off the ground, and looks back at the plant house: the walkway, under the part of the vault that still stands.
9. **[closes] [world]** The light going, and the wind cold on their wet skin, and nothing to make a fire with: the kindler went with the fieldpack *(D14.2)*. *Ending **THREAT***.

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** They have reached Genesis and found almost nothing: the one building they can reach is a ruin, open and empty. They can see nothing on the crown from the bank, and the way to it is flood. They have a roof for the night, on the walkway. Teva has said the old line and it broke. Night is coming.

**Worked on and did not change.** The headache. Her decision on Day 12, still hers.

**Left open.** Whether the rest of Genesis is anything more than this.

**Refused outright.** A puzzle: there is no lock to fail at. Anyone remarking that the founders left because this plant failed. Anyone blaming her. Comfort that works. Wading: the flooded ground is left, and nobody steps off the sill.

## State closure

**Against D15.1:** the morning after the storm, the three together, the water drunk.

**Against D15.3:** evening; they need shelter before dark, and the one roofed place off the water is the plant house walkway, up the stair from the sill, above the flooded floor.
