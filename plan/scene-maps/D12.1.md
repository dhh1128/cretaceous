## IN

**Place and time.** A warm spring in the limestone, a few hundred meters above and off the line of march, Day 12, the midday lay-up — roughly 1100 to 1500. Three or four of the day's six kilometers are behind them. Rain, continuous, warm and saturating; gray light that does not change all day. `journey-calendar.md` §2 calls the day *misery without incident*.

**World facts, so that nothing here has to be invented at the keyboard.**

- The water comes out of the rock at about forty degrees and smells of sulfur. Both are allocated to Day 12 and to no other day (`milieu-allocation.md` §5, touch and smell). It runs — it is a spring, not a pool — which is what makes it usable for leaching at all.
- Sustained rain suppresses the biting insects. This is the one mercy of Days 11–12 and nobody comments on it (`milieu-allocation.md` §3b; `journey-calendar.md` §2).
- **Day 12 has one sound and it is rain** (`milieu-allocation.md` §5). No insect wall, no calls, nothing.
- Ground: limestone broken country — scree, gulches, thin soil, exposed rock (`milieu-allocation.md` §2). Vegetation is thin scrub, gnetophyte berries on the scree, resurrection ferns on the rock.
- Rain here lands near 22 °C and is a warm shower. **Nobody may call the weather cold** (`journey-calendar.md` §2).
- Home processing, which all three have watched their whole lives: cores mashed to a gray-brown acrid pulp in a limestone basin, basketed, leached in running water for **one to three days**, dried to a pale neutral starch, baked into *pan*. Unprocessed pulp means scarcity or a broken food system (`milieu-brief.md` §6).
- **What the poison does, and the part that governs this scene is the last clause.** A glycoside the gut converts to the active agent, so a latency and then violent vomiting, cramp and watery diarrhea, then liver injury over days. The vomiting kills first, by dehydration, and faster in someone already short of water and calories. There is no field treatment. **Dose matters — a short leach is survivable and a raw dose is not, which is what makes leaching-time a decision rather than a rule** (`milieu-brief.md` §6). See **L1**: the six-to-twelve-hour figure in that section describes the raw dose, and a partial leach runs later and milder.

**Teva** — unshelled since Day 10 and carrying the suit rather than wearing it, so what she has lost is camouflage and sensors (`body-and-resources.md` §3b). Several kilos down. Feels cold from the inside, in warm rain, and the reader gets that in full while neither boy does (`voice-sheets.md`). Two nights of three-hour watches on top of everything before. **Wants: to put food into the two of them today.** Believes her anger is the only thing holding her up. Fears it is not. Hides the confession she is carrying, and hides that she has started to recognize the Council in herself.

**Keo** — febrile. Infection in the thigh laceration, and the healing upgrade closing it at the cost of strength, warmth and clarity out of reserves he does not have (`body-and-resources.md` §1). Cracked rib, wrenched ankle. **"Carried" is what the arc calls it and it is not yet literal** — he walks, slowly, carrying nothing, and Teva has his load as well as her own. That matters downstream: the escalation on Day 13 runs hand, then arm over a shoulder, then on a back, then the drag-frame, and **a litter on Day 12 spends the whole of it a day early.** See **L12**. **Wants: to be useful once, in the one subject he was actually trained in.** He is an Explorer and what is safe to eat is his field. Believes he is a liability. Hides how much pain he is in.

**Benal** — shoulder capacitor dead since Day 11 and nobody told; elbow seal half-repaired and the arm weak, hidden. Taking watches he cannot afford. **Wants: to be worth the food he is about to be given.** All he has is arithmetic. Fears being right too late.

## What the red team moved

Four models from four lineages attacked revision 1 against a self-contained packet (`.ignored/redteam/`). What survived verification and changed the map:

- **The leach was three hours, not four.** The prep moves stood between the opening and the bag going in the water, so the actual soak was shorter than the shortfall the scene is about. The bag now goes in at move 4, before the argument.
- **The decision was a gamble and is now a trade.** Revision 1 had her betting the leach had finished, which reads as desperate — the hazard the scene entry names. Move 15 now has her knowingly trading a partial dose against a day with no food in it, which is what `milieu-brief.md` §6 says leaching-time actually is.
- **Her venom resistance is not the best of the three.** Canon gives Keo's as excellent too. Move 16 now rests on the two comparisons canon supports.
- **Benal's objection had no content.** "Half-says the other thing" gave a drafter a shape and no claim, so the hedge had nothing to weaken. Move 10 names it.
- **The four-hour wait had nothing in it**, in the only lay-up these two scenes contain, while `body-and-resources.md` §2b assigns wound care, suit feeding and weapon drying to exactly that window. Move 12 now carries all three.
- **The taste is a dose.** Nobody had accounted for the exposure before the exposure.
- **The `[refused]` at old move 18 was describing ignorance, not refusal.** It has moved to OUT, where a state of knowledge belongs.
- **Rejected:** that the spring would run pH 2–3 and dissolve the bag. See L6.

A fifth seat returned late and returned only one of the six sections it was asked for, truncated mid-item — **and its `finish_reason` said `stop`, so the provider-metadata check that is supposed to catch that did not.** What caught it was grepping the answer for the six section headings it had been told to produce.
