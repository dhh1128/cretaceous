## IN

- **`PLACE`** — a warm spring in the limestone, a few hundred meters above and off the line of march; scree, gulches, thin scrub, wild stone-cycad, red gnetophyte berries on the scree. *(`milieu-allocation.md` §2, §3b, §4.)*
- **`TIME`** — Day 12, the midday lay-up, about 1100 to 1500; two or three of the day's four kilometers behind them. *(`journey-calendar.md` §1; `body-and-resources.md` §2b.)*
- **`WEATHER`** — continuous warm rain, near 22 °C; nobody calls it cold. It keeps the biting insects down. Teva's cold comes from inside her: hunger and no sleep. *(`journey-calendar.md` §2.)*
- **`LIGHT`** — flat gray, no shadows, all day.
- **`SOUND`** — rain, and nothing else. *(`milieu-allocation.md` §5.)*
- **`CLOCK`** — the four-hour lay-up, then they walk; and a spring is where predators drink, so they leave before the light goes. *(`body-and-resources.md` §2, §2b.)* The cores are pounded as they arrive and the bag goes in at about 1100 `[-]`.
- **`LOAD`** — Teva carries her pack and the fieldpack, which is Keo's, since the scree *(D11.4)*; Keo carries nothing and walks <!-- @WF-keo.carried: false -->; Benal has his own. - **`BODY`** — **Teva:** suit on since Night 11, its camouflage patchy; several kilos down; two nights of three-hour watches. **Keo:** the healing running hot in him, no infection — fever, shaking, fog; the thigh cut closing; cracked rib; wrenched ankle. **Benal:** shoulder capacitor dead since Day 11 and he has told nobody; arm weak; the worst sleep debt of the three. *(`body-and-resources.md` §1, §3, §3b.)*

## SPINE

**The mash**, from pounding to eating: pounding, bagging, sinking, waiting, hauling, tasting, eating. Hands are busy throughout *(`body-and-resources.md` §2b)*.

## MOVES

1. **[opens] [spine]** Teva is already pounding cores between a loose stone and the rock when the scene opens.
2. **[body]** She is cold from inside, and in her suit, and she does not trust its camouflage. `[requires D10.2 — her thermal failure and the labor of concealing it]`
3. **[image]** The spring: water out of the rock at blood heat, and the sulfur smell. *(`milieu-allocation.md` §5; used here only.)*
4. **[spine]** The bag goes into the flow, weighted and tied, before anyone argues.
5. Benal gives the numbers: hours of light, kilometers left, how long since anyone ate, and that what they cut all morning came out at about half what they hoped for. Nobody answers it.
6. Teva asks him how long the bag can stay in before they have to walk. He tells her.
7. **[spine]** Teva states the method: at home a leach takes one to three days in river water; fine pulp in forty-degree <!-- @WF-spring.temp: 40C --> water goes faster, maybe half a day or a day; they have four hours. She does not claim it will be done.
8. **[turn — KEO]** Keo says it needs another day, and says how: hang it in the runoff overnight and eat tomorrow. He says it once. `[plants → D12.2 — he does not say it again]` `[plants → D13.2 — the thing he conceals from here to Act 3]`
9. Teva does not dispute his method. She refuses the night: one more day with nothing in any of them, before the longest walk yet.

11. **[body]** Keo takes a turn at the pounding, cannot kneel to it on the thigh, and sits down before he is asked.

16. **[turn — TEVA]** The decision she is actually making: not whether the mash is clean, which she knows it is not, but whether what is left in it is less than a body can handle, against a day with nothing else in it. *(`milieu-brief.md` §4 and §6: dose matters.)*

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** Teva has eaten a full portion of four-hour mash at about 1500, plus the taste, and is well. The remainder is carried, uneaten. A waiting period has been said out loud. Keo has said the true thing once and let it go, and now has something to hide. Benal has been right and hedged it.

**Worked on and did not change.** Keo's usefulness; the suits; the spike shafts; the gap between Teva and the other two.

**Left open.** Whether the dose is under what a body can handle. Whether Teva heard Keo.

**Refused outright.** Any confirmation that Keo was right. Narration of what Teva feels about him. That venom resistance is the wrong category, which nobody present knows.
