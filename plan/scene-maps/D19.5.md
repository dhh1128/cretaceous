**Changed.** They are inside the perimeter, on the river reach between the two barriers, the hull grounded against the near bank, substantially intact. The rotors are spent and the charge is nearly nothing. Teva has watched Keo put the machine down on the water she teaches is death, on a machine's word, and has given him the decision to do it. Her hand has been to the flower and nobody saw. A watcher has seen them come over the wall, and the Enclave is coming.

**Left open.** Who is coming, and what they will do. Whether the machine can ever leave this water.
