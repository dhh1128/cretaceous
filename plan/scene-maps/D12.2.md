## IN

**Place and time.** Camp on the limestone broken country, chosen before the light went because it had to be, on ground nobody would pick. Day 12, night. Rain, continuous, warm, and it has not stopped since the front came through on Day 11.

**World facts, so that nothing here has to be invented at the keyboard.**

- **Day 12 has one sound and it is rain** (`milieu-allocation.md` §5). No insect wall — sustained rain suppresses it, and this is the mercy inside the misery. No calls. The absence is the texture of the night, and **rain is the one thing that defeats Benal's hearing** (`tech-rules.md`).
- Where they sleep is a decision that costs something every night: elevation is safer and exhausting to reach, the ground is easy and lethal, a defensible spot is usually a wet one (`body-and-resources.md` §2b). Tonight there is nothing to climb.
- **Do not move at night.** Roots, burrows and scree in the dark is how you break a leg, and a broken leg out here is a death (`body-and-resources.md` §2b).
- *Thescelosaurus* burrows are allocated to Day 12 and their holes are a hazard on bad ground (`milieu-allocation.md` §3).
- A *Didelphodon* is allocated to Day 12: badger-sized, semi-aquatic, a bone-crushing bite, entirely unimpressed (`milieu-allocation.md` §3, `geo-flora-fauna.md` §4.5).
- **Weapons are on a clock the weather winds.** Shafts, handles and bindings carry dormant archaea; dry is dormant and *sustained* wetting wakes them. Nothing has dried since Day 11. Bindings going soft, shafts spongy at the ferrule. Get it dry within a day or so and nothing has happened — and they cannot (`milieu-brief.md` §6, `body-and-resources.md` §3c).
- Three of them, so watches are three hours each and nobody has had an unbroken night since Day 3. Cumulative sleep debt is the quiet driver of everything on Days 11–13 and **is never named as a cause** (`body-and-resources.md`).
- The proximity sense reads presence and kind at short range; **a burrowing animal is a cold spark, thirty meters down** (`milieu-brief.md` §7). **Exhaustion breaks it** (`tech-rules.md`).

**Keo** — febrile: infection in the thigh laceration, and accelerated healing closing it at the price of strength, warmth and clarity out of reserves that are not there. Cracked rib, wrenched ankle, carried for two days. Has eaten nothing today and will eat nothing until morning. **Wants: to be worth a watch.** Believes he is a liability. Fears he has done to these two what he did to Yara. Hides how much pain he is in, and — new since this afternoon — **that he said the mash needed another day and let it go**.

**Teva** — nine to twelve hours out from a full portion of short-leached mash, and fine. Carrying two packs. **Wants: to be upright in the morning.** Says nothing about any of it.

**Benal** — capacitor dead and unmentioned since Day 11, arm weak and hidden, further into sleep debt than either of them because he keeps taking watches. **Wants: to be worth the food he was given today** — and he has not been given any yet either.

**Relationship temperatures** (`character-arcs.md` §3, Days 12–14): Keo↔Benal 8, the strongest dyad in the book and the one that moves without speech. Keo→Teva 5, changed and quieter.

**READER — computed, per `D12.1.md`'s IN, plus what D12.1 added:** that Teva short-leached the mash against Keo's one quiet objection, and that she has eaten a full portion of it. Nothing in `knowledge-ledger.md` is paid on Day 12 by either scene.

## SPINE

**The spear**, and the losing labor of trying to dry a thing in rain that does not stop. It is in Keo's hands when the scene opens and it is still wrong when it closes. The task carries the scene; the dialogue sits on top of it (`body-and-resources.md` §2b).

## MOVES

1. **[opens] [spine]** Keo working at a binding that is soft and will be softer by morning, under a covering that is not keeping anything dry. — *Opens on the labor, not on the camp. `body-and-resources.md` §3c wants the reader to see them fail at this, and to see it as routine.*

2. **[body]** The fever from inside: heat with no source in it, the wound closing faster than it should, hunger gone quiet, which is worse than hunger. — *The healing upgrade's cost, delivered as sensation. Only he can feel it, which is one of two reasons the scene is his.*

3. **[image]** The night has one sound in it. No insect wall. — *`milieu-allocation.md` §5: Day 12 has rain and nothing else. Two clauses.* **Must land in a single sentence.**

4. Benal says he will take first watch, and takes it, and neither of them stops him. — *`scene-list.md` marks this `[?]`. What makes it more than a kindness declined: **rain is the one thing that defeats his hearing**, so the person whose whole value is detection is taking a watch he cannot perform, and everyone lets him because the alternative is saying so out loud.* `[requires D11.6 — Benal sitting watch over a feverish Keo, and Keo's gratitude]`

5. Keo starts to say the thing about the mash. — *He has not stopped thinking it. He does not think* I should have insisted *— the scene entry's hazard is guilt narrated and that is the sentence it forbids.*

6. **[body] [turn — KEO]** His body takes the floor away from him mid-sentence and somebody else fills the gap. — *`voice-sheets.md`, under "Two rules taken from the exemplars", names this mechanism and assigns **Keo's fever on Day 12** to it outright: physiology costs a character the floor, the body business goes* after *the line so it reads as the price of having spoken, and they have to fight their way back in.*

7. He does not fight his way back in. — *The turn, and it is a non-event. `character-arcs.md` Days 12–13: what he hides from here to Act 3 is this, and the reason is in move 2 and not in his character.* `[requires D12.1.9 — Keo says the mash needs another day, once, quietly, and lets it go]` `[plants → D13.2 — he is dragging the consequence of a thing he was right about]`

8. **[spine]** Back to the binding. It is no better. — *The spine absorbs the moment, which is what a spine is for.*

9. The *Didelphodon* comes into the camp, and it is not interested in them. It is after the mash. — *Allocated here, and the allocation calls it nuisance and comedy. **The comedy is that it has no opinion of them at all**, and what turns it is that it wants the only food they have.*

10. Keo reaches for the old register at it, and the joke does not land. — *`humor-plan.md` mechanism 4 and the Keo entry: his bravado must die at the midpoint and stay dead, and* Nobody laughed *is used twice in the novel — once in Act 2 before the midpoint and once after, **when he tries the old register and it dies.** This is the second one. Two words, its own paragraph.*

11. He goes after the animal, in the dark, and goes into a burrow hole to the knee on the wrenched ankle. — *Two Day 12 allocations in one action. And the reason it happens: **his proximity sense would have shown him a cold spark thirty meters down a week ago**, and exhaustion has taken it. `tech-rules.md` rule 5 — capabilities degrade and do not recover — dramatized rather than asserted, at a cost.*

12. **[body]** What that nearly was, arriving after the fact and only in his body. Nobody in the camp says it. — *`body-and-resources.md` §2b: a broken leg out here is a death. **The hazard is writing this as an action sequence.** It is four seconds and then a long time sitting still.*

13. **[refused]** The spear held. It is not going to hold much longer and nobody remarks on that either. — *`body-and-resources.md` §3c: the weapons pay off **once, badly**, and that once is not here. What this scene spends is the labor, not the failure. Do not cash it.*

14. **[closes]** The rain, and Teva asleep, and him awake. — *Ending type **HELD** — fear held open, neither resolved nor advanced. The dread is the reader's arithmetic and not his; he is watching her sleep because he cannot sleep, and the reason he gives himself is the fever.*

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** Keo has now not said it twice, and the second time was his own choice rather than his body's. His proximity sense has failed him once at a cost, and he knows it. His ankle is worse. Benal has taken a watch he could not keep.

**Worked on and did not change.** The spear. The fever. The distance between what Keo knows and what he will say.

**Left open.** Whether the mash was leached long enough — and the night ends without answering it, which is the point of putting a scene here at all. Whether Benal slept.

**Refused outright.** Any narration of Keo's guilt as guilt. Any sign from Teva. Whether the spear will fail, and when.

**READER.** No `knowledge-ledger.md` row is paid. No `[turn — READER ONLY]`.
