## IN

**Place and time.** Camp on the limestone broken country, chosen before the light went because it had to be, on ground nobody would pick. Day 12, night. Rain, continuous, warm, and it has not stopped since the front came through on Day 11.

**World facts, so that nothing here has to be invented at the keyboard.**

- **Day 12 has one sound and it is rain** (`milieu-allocation.md` §5). No insect wall — sustained rain suppresses it, and this is the mercy inside the misery. No calls. The absence is the texture of the night, and **rain is the one thing that defeats Benal's hearing** (`tech-rules.md`) — twice over now, since the suit's dampening went with the capacitor on Day 11.
- **There is almost no light, and that governs the scene.** Overcast, raining, no moon to be had, nothing dry enough to burn. **Ambient light is one thing and on-demand light is another, and the gap between them is the instrument.** A repairing biofilm is faintly bioluminescent (`kb/worldbuilding/safety-suits.md`) and Benal's is repairing, which is enough to place a body and not enough to work by. **And a forearm display can be woken hard for a second or two** — much brighter, and clumsy in every direction: aimed with the whole arm, lighting the wearer more than the thing, costing charge that is not being replaced after dark, and leaving everyone who looked at it blind for a minute afterward. **Benal's is the only one**, because Teva's is off her and patchy. See **M6**.
- Where they sleep is a decision that costs something every night: elevation is safer and exhausting to reach, the ground is easy and lethal, a defensible spot is usually a wet one (`body-and-resources.md` §2b). Tonight there is nothing to climb.
- **The camp is built, not merely chosen.** Cut spined cycad fronds laid points outward, thorn scrub dragged into the gaps, green stakes sharpened and angled out on the open side — an hour of labor at the end of the day by three people who have eaten nothing, and standard practice every night since Day 3 (`body-and-resources.md` §2b). **It is sized for a predator, and a fence sized for a predator passes anything small**, which is the Enclave's river grating in miniature. See **M2**.
- **Do not move at night.** Roots, burrows and scree in the dark is how you break a leg, and a broken leg out here is a death (`body-and-resources.md` §2b).
- *Thescelosaurus* burrows are allocated to Day 12 and their holes are a hazard on bad ground (`milieu-allocation.md` §3).
- A *Didelphodon* is allocated to Day 12: badger-sized, semi-aquatic, a bone-crushing bite for shell and bone, entirely unimpressed (`milieu-allocation.md` §3, `geo-flora-fauna.md` §4.5). **It is a durophage and a generalist, not a starch forager** — see **M7** for what actually brings it in.
- **Weapons are on a clock the weather winds.** Shafts, handles and bindings carry dormant archaea; dry is dormant and *sustained* wetting wakes them. Nothing has dried since Day 11. Bindings going soft, shafts spongy at the ferrule. Get it dry within a day or so and nothing has happened — and they cannot (`milieu-brief.md` §6, `body-and-resources.md` §3c).
- Three of them, so watches are three hours each and nobody has had an unbroken night since Day 3. Cumulative sleep debt is the quiet driver of everything on Days 11–13 and **is never named as a cause** (`body-and-resources.md`).
- **The pheromone barrier is gone, and this is the first place it matters.** It reads to a predator as a wounded alpha and to anything smaller as *flee or die*, it is applied by hand, and it is harvested and reapplied on a schedule nobody out here can keep (`milieu-brief.md` §6, `tech-rules.md`). **Nine days, a river crossing and thirty-six hours of continuous rain have taken it off them.** An intact barrier and a badger-sized animal walking into camp cannot both be true, and sleeping on open ground has been assuming a barrier that stopped working days ago. See **M9**.
- The proximity sense reads **presence and kind** — living things, at short range (`milieu-brief.md` §7). **Exhaustion breaks it** (`tech-rules.md`). It does not read terrain, and it never did.

**Keo** — febrile: infection in the thigh laceration, and accelerated healing closing it at the price of strength, warmth and clarity out of reserves that are not there. Cracked rib, wrenched ankle, carried for two days. **Wants: to be worth a watch.** Believes he is a liability. Fears he has done to these two what he did to Yara. Hides how much pain he is in, and — new since this afternoon — **that he said the mash needed another day and let it go**.

**Teva** — five or six hours out from a full portion of the four-hour batch, and fine. Carrying two packs. **Wants: to feed the other two in the morning, and to feed them something better than what she fed herself.** She will not say that is what she is doing and nobody will make her. Says nothing about how she feels.

**Benal** — capacitor dead and unmentioned since Day 11, arm weak and hidden, further into sleep debt than either of them because he keeps taking watches. **Wants: to keep watch**, which is `character-arcs.md`'s own word for it across Days 12–15 and is what move 8 serves. *(An IN that declares a want the arc does not carry mis-makes the join at the one line the map exists to make it.)*

**Relationship temperatures** (`character-arcs.md` §3, Days 12–14): Keo↔Benal 8, the strongest dyad in the book and the one that moves without speech. Keo→Teva 5, changed and quieter.

**READER — computed, per `D12.1.md`'s IN, plus what D12.1 added:** that Teva short-leached the mash against Keo's one quiet objection, that Benal said the thing that would have stopped it and hedged it away, and and that she has eaten a full portion of it and nobody else has eaten anything. Nothing in `knowledge-ledger.md` is paid on Day 12 by either scene.

## SPINE

**The spear**, and the losing labor of trying to dry a thing in rain that does not stop. It is in Keo's hands when the scene opens and it is still wrong when it closes. The task carries the scene; everything else sits on top of it (`body-and-resources.md` §2b).

## MOVES

1. **[opens] [spine]** Keo working at a binding that is soft and will be softer by morning, by feel, because there is not enough light to do it by eye. — *Opens on the labor and establishes the dark in the same action, without a sentence about darkness.*

2. **[image]** The only light in the camp is coming off Benal's arm, and it is not enough to see by. — *`safety-suits.md`: a healthy biofilm is faintly bioluminescent, and his is repairing. **The one light they have comes off the boy who believes he is useless**, and nobody remarks on it.* **Must land in a single sentence.**

3. **[body]** The fever from inside: heat with no source in it, the wound closing faster than it should, hunger gone quiet, which is worse than hunger. — *The healing upgrade's cost, delivered as sensation. Only he can feel it, which is one of two reasons the scene is his.* **Three symptoms and no more. One past its share and the scene's named hazard — guilt narrated — arrives wearing sensory clothes.**

4. Keo begins to say it again — that they should have waited, that it is not the same thing as waiting a day. — *He has not stopped thinking it. **He does not think** I should have insisted *— the scene entry's hazard is guilt narrated and that is the sentence it forbids.*

Nobody argues, nobody concedes, and nobody names it. **It is also the mechanism of Day 13**: what the two boys eat at dawn has had the night in it and what she ate at the spring did not, which is why she is the only one who goes down. `D12.1` L10.* `[requires D12.1.8 — Keo's method, stated concretely and let go]` `[plants → D13.1 — Keo and Benal ate a batch that leached overnight and Teva did not]` **No dialogue. 9. **[spine]** Back to the binding. It is no better. — *The spine absorbs the moment, which is what a spine is for.*

10. The animal is at the runnel, working at the bag, before any of them knows it is there — **and it did not have to get through anything, because the bag was never inside.** **Moves 10 to 13 are the only event in thirteen hundred words and must not take the second half of them** — *bits, not quips* invites business, and business here would eat the scene. — ***This is the capability failing, and it is the right capability.*** *The proximity sense reads presence and kind at short range and that is exactly what a badger-sized mammal walking into a camp is. Exhaustion has taken it (`tech-rules.md`), and it has taken it silently, so what Keo notices is not a loss but an animal. `tech-rules.md` rule 5 — capabilities degrade and do not recover — dramatized at a cost. **The sense cannot be made to warn him about a hole in the ground; it has never read terrain.***

11. Then it goes and their eyes go with it.* **Two seconds, one sentence, and the dark afterward is worse than the dark before.**

12. Keo reaches for the old register at it, and the joke does not land. — *`humor-plan.md` mechanism 4 and the Keo entry: his bravado must die at the midpoint and stay dead, and* Nobody laughed *is used twice in the novel — once in Act 2 before the midpoint and once after, **when he tries the old register and it dies.** This is the second one. The chase and the fall are both consequences of the one useful thing anybody did.* **Four seconds. The hazard is writing this as an action sequence.**

14. He gets the leg out and says nothing about it. — *An action, not a realization. A feeling is not a move.*

15. **[refused]** The spear held. It is not going to hold much longer and nobody remarks on that either. — *`body-and-resources.md` §3c: the weapons pay off **once, badly**, and that once is not here. What this scene spends is the labor, not the failure. Do not cash it.*

16. **[closes]** The rain, and Teva asleep, and him awake — and what is left of the mash back in the water, where he said to put it. — *Ending type **HELD** — fear held open, neither resolved nor advanced. The dread is the reader's arithmetic and not his; the reason he gives himself for being awake is the fever.*

## OUT — deltas against IN

The bag is torn and lighter by whatever the animal got. Keo has now not said it twice, and the second time was his own choice rather than his body's. His proximity sense has failed him once at a cost. His ankle is worse. Benal has taken a watch he cannot keep.

**Worked on and did not change.** The spear. The fever. The distance between what Keo knows and what he will say.

**Left open.** Whether what Teva already ate was leached enough — and the night ends without answering it, which is the point of putting a scene here. Whether Benal slept. How much the animal got. **Whether anyone will ever say out loud whose idea the runnel was.**

**Refused outright.** Any narration of Keo's guilt as guilt. Any sign from Teva. Whether the spear will fail, and when.

## State closure

**Against D12.1**: entry state is exit state of D12.1 — afternoon march done, camp made before dark, Teva five or six hours past her portion and asymptomatic, the two boys unfed, the remainder carried and not yet in water.

**Against D13.1** (Day 13, morning, Benal, the coastal plain): the gap holds the rest of the night, the bag coming out of the runnel, the dawn meal, and the descent off the limestone. **D12.1's entry was right that they eat at dawn and D13.1's *she had one last night as well* is her portion at the spring — neither entry needs changing.**

**And the batch is the whole mechanism.** Teva ate four hours at forty degrees. The boys eat that same four hours *plus the night at ambient*, which once the temperature is accounted for is roughly three times the leaching. **She is not sicker because she ate more; she is sicker because she ate the one that had not finished** — and she ate it because she would not let anyone else go first. At the latency in **L1** she comes due mid-morning on Day 13, roughly eighteen hours out, and the two of them are queasy across the day and never down. That is exactly the picture D13.1 asks for, and until this correction nothing supplied it.
