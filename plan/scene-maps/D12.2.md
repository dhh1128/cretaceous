- **`WEATHER`** — rain, continuous and warm, unbroken since the front came through on Day 11. **To each body:** it defeats Benal's hearing, it keeps a wound wet that is trying to close, and it is warm rain that Teva feels as cold from the inside. *(`journey-calendar.md` §2.)*
- **`LIGHT`** — **almost none, and it governs the scene.** Overcast, raining, no moon, nothing dry enough to burn — **fire is ruled out.** Ambient is the faint bioluminescence of Benal's repairing biofilm: enough to place a body, not enough to work by. **On demand, a forearm display can be driven hard for a second or two** — much brighter, aimed with the whole arm, lighting the wearer more than the thing, costing stored charge that generation does not replace after dark, and leaving everyone who looked at it blind for a minute. Not a torch, never designed as one. Benal's is the only one; Teva's is off her and patchy. *(`kb/worldbuilding/safety-suits.md`.)*

## SPINE

**The spear**, and the losing labor of trying to dry a thing in rain that does not stop. It is in Keo's hands when the scene opens and it is still wrong when it closes. The task carries the scene; everything else sits on top of it (`body-and-resources.md` §2b).

## MOVES
