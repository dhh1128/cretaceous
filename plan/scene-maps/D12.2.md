## IN

**Place and time.** Camp on the limestone broken country, chosen before the light went because it had to be, on ground nobody would pick. Day 12, night. Rain, continuous, warm, and it has not stopped since the front came through on Day 11.

**World facts, so that nothing here has to be invented at the keyboard.**

- **Day 12 has one sound and it is rain** (`milieu-allocation.md` §5). No insect wall — sustained rain suppresses it, and this is the mercy inside the misery. No calls. The absence is the texture of the night, and **rain is the one thing that defeats Benal's hearing** (`tech-rules.md`) — twice over now, since the suit's dampening went with the capacitor on Day 11.
- **There is almost no light, and that governs the scene.** Overcast, raining, no moon to be had, nothing dry enough to burn. What there is comes off a living suit: a healthy biofilm is faintly bioluminescent (`kb/worldbuilding/safety-suits.md`), and Benal's is repairing, which means feeding, which means glowing. It is enough to place a body and not enough to work by. See **M6**.
- Where they sleep is a decision that costs something every night: elevation is safer and exhausting to reach, the ground is easy and lethal, a defensible spot is usually a wet one (`body-and-resources.md` §2b). Tonight there is nothing to climb.
- **Do not move at night.** Roots, burrows and scree in the dark is how you break a leg, and a broken leg out here is a death (`body-and-resources.md` §2b).
- *Thescelosaurus* burrows are allocated to Day 12 and their holes are a hazard on bad ground (`milieu-allocation.md` §3).
- A *Didelphodon* is allocated to Day 12: badger-sized, semi-aquatic, a bone-crushing bite for shell and bone, entirely unimpressed (`milieu-allocation.md` §3, `geo-flora-fauna.md` §4.5). **It is a durophage and a generalist, not a starch forager** — see **M7** for what actually brings it in.
- **Weapons are on a clock the weather winds.** Shafts, handles and bindings carry dormant archaea; dry is dormant and *sustained* wetting wakes them. Nothing has dried since Day 11. Bindings going soft, shafts spongy at the ferrule. Get it dry within a day or so and nothing has happened — and they cannot (`milieu-brief.md` §6, `body-and-resources.md` §3c).
- Three of them, so watches are three hours each and nobody has had an unbroken night since Day 3. Cumulative sleep debt is the quiet driver of everything on Days 11–13 and **is never named as a cause** (`body-and-resources.md`).
- The proximity sense reads **presence and kind** — living things, at short range (`milieu-brief.md` §7). **Exhaustion breaks it** (`tech-rules.md`). It does not read terrain, and it never did.

**Keo** — febrile: infection in the thigh laceration, and accelerated healing closing it at the price of strength, warmth and clarity out of reserves that are not there. Cracked rib, wrenched ankle, carried for two days. **Wants: to be worth a watch.** Believes he is a liability. Fears he has done to these two what he did to Yara. Hides how much pain he is in, and — new since this afternoon — **that he said the mash needed another day and let it go**.

**Teva** — about five hours out from a full portion of short-leached mash, and fine. Carrying two packs. **Wants: to feed the other two, which is the thing she has been waiting all evening to be allowed to do.** Her declared waiting period is what has kept the mash untouched since the spring, and it runs out inside this scene. Says nothing about how she feels.

**Benal** — capacitor dead and unmentioned since Day 11, arm weak and hidden, further into sleep debt than either of them because he keeps taking watches. **Wants: to be worth the food he is about to be given.**

**Relationship temperatures** (`character-arcs.md` §3, Days 12–14): Keo↔Benal 8, the strongest dyad in the book and the one that moves without speech. Keo→Teva 5, changed and quieter.

**READER — computed, per `D12.1.md`'s IN, plus what D12.1 added:** that Teva short-leached the mash against Keo's one quiet objection, that Benal said the thing that would have stopped it and hedged it away, and that she has eaten a full portion and declared a waiting period. Nothing in `knowledge-ledger.md` is paid on Day 12 by either scene.

## SPINE

**The spear**, and the losing labor of trying to dry a thing in rain that does not stop. It is in Keo's hands when the scene opens and it is still wrong when it closes. The task carries the scene; everything else sits on top of it (`body-and-resources.md` §2b).

## MOVES

1. **[opens] [spine]** Keo working at a binding that is soft and will be softer by morning, by feel, because there is not enough light to do it by eye. — *Opens on the labor and establishes the dark in the same action, without a sentence about darkness.*

2. **[image]** The only light in the camp is coming off Benal's arm, and it is not enough to see by. — *`safety-suits.md`: a healthy biofilm is faintly bioluminescent, and his is repairing. **The one light they have comes off the boy who believes he is useless**, and nobody remarks on it.* **Must land in a single sentence.**

3. **[body]** The fever from inside: heat with no source in it, the wound closing faster than it should, hunger gone quiet, which is worse than hunger. — *The healing upgrade's cost, delivered as sensation. Only he can feel it, which is one of two reasons the scene is his.*

4. **[turn — TEVA, offstage]** Teva says the waiting is done and divides the mash. — *Her want served, and the answer to the question revision 1 could not answer: why three starving people sit beside food they do not eat. Because she said so at the spring and nobody has argued with her about anything all day.* `[requires D12.1.18 — the waiting period declared out loud in front of both of them]`

5. **[spine]** They eat. All three, and she takes a second portion because there is one and because she is the one who has already carried it. — *`D12.1` L10: this is where the second meal goes, not at dawn. It is also what makes her dose twice theirs, which is what D13.1 needs.*

6. Keo begins to say it again — that they should have waited, that it is not the same thing as waiting a day. — *He has not stopped thinking it. **He does not think** I should have insisted *— the scene entry's hazard is guilt narrated and that is the sentence it forbids.*

7. **[body] [turn — KEO]** The rib takes the sentence off him mid-clause, and Teva fills the gap with something practical about the morning. — *`voice-sheets.md`, "Two rules taken from the exemplars", names this mechanism and assigns **Keo's fever on Day 12** to it outright: physiology costs a character the floor, the body business goes* after *the line so it reads as the price of having spoken, and they have to fight their way back in. **The interrupting event and the person who fills the gap are both named now**, which they were not in revision 1.*

9. Benal takes first watch, and neither of them stops him. — *`scene-list.md` marks this `[?]`. What makes it more than a kindness declined: **rain has taken his hearing twice**, by weather and by the dead capacitor he has told nobody about, so the person whose whole value is detection is taking a watch he cannot perform, and everyone lets him because the alternative is saying so out loud.* `[requires D11.6 — Benal sitting watch over a feverish Keo, and Keo's gratitude]`

10. **[spine]** Back to the binding. It is no better. — *The spine absorbs the moment, which is what a spine is for.*

11. The animal is inside the camp before any of them knows it is there. — ***This is the capability failing, and it is the right capability.*** *The proximity sense reads presence and kind at short range and that is exactly what a badger-sized mammal walking into a camp is. Exhaustion has taken it (`tech-rules.md`), and it has taken it silently, so what Keo notices is not a loss but an animal. `tech-rules.md` rule 5 — capabilities degrade and do not recover — dramatized at a cost. **Revision 1 had the sense failing to warn him about a hole in the ground, which it was never able to do.***

Two words, its own paragraph.*

It takes what it came for and goes, and Keo gets two steps after it before the ground gives under him to the knee. — *The animal's exit is on the page rather than assumed, and the hole is what `body-and-resources.md` §2b already says the dark is full of. **He does not chase it far, because there is no light to chase anything by** — the fall is two steps from the fire there isn't.* **Four seconds. The hazard is writing this as an action sequence.**

15. **[refused]** The spear held. It is not going to hold much longer and nobody remarks on that either. — *`body-and-resources.md` §3c: the weapons pay off **once, badly**, and that once is not here. What this scene spends is the labor, not the failure. Do not cash it.*

16. **[closes]** The rain, and Teva asleep, and him awake — and all three of them carrying it now, not one. — *Ending type **HELD** — fear held open, neither resolved nor advanced. The dread is the reader's arithmetic and not his; the reason he gives himself for being awake is the fever.*

## What the red team moved

- **The scene had no light at all**, and four moves depended on seeing. Three of four seats found it independently. It is now M6 and it reshapes moves 1, 11, 12 and 13 — the chase ends after two steps because there is nothing to chase by.
- **The proximity sense cannot detect a hole in the ground.** It reads presence and kind of living things at short range. Revision 1 built move 11 on it warning him about terrain, which it has never been able to do. It now fails on the animal, which is exactly what the sense is for — and that repairs a second objection, that the scene gave Keo no way to perceive the *Didelphodon* at all.
- **The animal had no exit and no plausible motive.** Both fixed, at moves 11 and 13 and in M7.
- **Teva was furniture** — a stated want and nothing to do but sleep and be watched, which is defect three on this project's own standard. She now brings the meal, which also answers why three starving people sit beside food they do not eat.
- **"Somebody else fills the gap"** named no party, and **"what that nearly was"** was a feeling rather than a move. Moves 7 and 14.
- **Benal's dampening is gone as well as defeated by rain**, which makes move 9 worse in the right way.
