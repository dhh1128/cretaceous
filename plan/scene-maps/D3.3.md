- **`WANTS`** *(`character-arcs.md` §2)* — **Teva:** wants *to be right*; believes *competence is a moral quality*; fears ***water***; hides *her thermal failure, the fear*. **Keo:** to lead. **Benal:** not to be a burden.

- **`READER`** — computed; no row paid.
