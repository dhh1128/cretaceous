## SPINE

**The drill, and Teva's hands in Omya's hair.** Omya, lucid, does what a Master Keeper does with a lucid hour: she drills her apprentice. **First the protocol, then the sky, then the fold**, and the order is the scene. It runs from the knowledge Teva half has, through the knowledge nobody values, to the knowledge keeping them alive, and the last is where it breaks. The drill is spoken: **the gel keys dissolve outside the Vitarium's humidity** *(`milieu-brief.md` §8)*, so there is nothing to handle. **Teva's hands are busy the whole time, redoing the Master Keeper's plaits** (L1). That is her warmth done rather than said *(`voice-sheets.md`)*, it gives the talk somewhere for the hands to be, and it is the one Keeper thing the girl with the failed senses can still do perfectly. Talk has carried scenes of this length before, and none of them had anything for the hands to do.
