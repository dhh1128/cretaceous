## MOVES

1. **[opens] [image]** **The decoy goes in.** — *All three at the first stone of the narrows. **Keo, beside her, very still, looking upstream at the pool** fifty yards off `[-]` *(he pushes from here, so nobody has to wait for anybody)*; **one of the hadrosaurs walking down into the slow water to drink and cool, as a hadrosaur would in this heat** — then **wading on, out past where it meant to stop, until it is swimming in the slow deep water and too far out to get back easily** *(the push)*. **Shapes and wakes turning toward the pool from up and down the river.** **The bellowing.** **Then the herd on the bank, panicking, crowding down into the shallows along the pool's edge** — water and mud churned over a wide front *(conditions, never named as help)*. **Keo, gray, says *now*.** **All three go in at once.*** **~12%.**

## Work queue, found by walking the invariants

- **Canon premise run.** *A boulder narrows at low water*: a riffle of boulders with shallow water between most and a few deep fast chutes is the ordinary shape of a rocky constriction at low flow. *Letting a chute carry you onto the next rock*: what non-swimmers can do if something holds them up. *Small ripples swept downstream*: D7.2. *Blood reaching the narrows*: slow water moving fifty yards in a minute or two is under a meter a second. *A hadrosaur wading in to drink and cool in heat*: ordinary; the nudge is toward what it wants anyway *(`tech-rules.md`: nudges inclination)*. *The push*: projection nudges inclination; a hadrosaur that wants to drink can be nudged deeper than it would go, and the crocs do the rest *(§3)*; the push costs him the animal's terror *(§9)*. *The croc's other senses*: they are not hidden, only quieter than the pool; the brush is a croc going past them to the louder thing. Passes.
- **What L1 commits the book to.** If the suits float a wearer, **Day 14–15's immersion at sea** *(`body-and-resources.md` §3b: immersion; the sea takes the fieldpack)* happens to people the suits hold up, and Keo, unsuited, is the one at risk. If they do not, this crossing needs a different means.
