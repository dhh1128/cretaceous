## Work queue, found by walking the invariants

- **Canon premise run.** *A suited body floating*: the suit's impact layer is 3–5 mm of aerogel foam over the whole body *(`safety-suits.md`)*; a full wetsuit of that thickness floats a swimmer, so the suit holding them up is physical, not magic. *Kicking under the surface*: the least surface disturbance a human can make. *Ripples swept downstream at the narrows*: D7.2's work queue. *A panicking hadrosaur held in slow water while crocs gather and kill it*: projection holds briefly; the crocs end it. *Keo feeling it*: `milieu-brief.md` §9, the one thing that comes back. Passes.
- **Keo unsuited in the water.** The gel on his skin is all he has; the river takes it off inside the hour *(§3)*. **That is why he goes last and fastest**, after letting go.
- **What Keo felt** comes back at D8.3, from Benal's POV, only as what Benal can see.
