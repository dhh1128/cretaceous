## SPINE

## MOVES

1. **[opens] [body]** Full dark on the gallery. The three of them against the end wall, Keo between Teva and Benal, shaking against her.
2. **[spine]** Before anyone sleeps it gets said, what they found, and each of them says it in their own register `[-]`. Keo asks whether this is it, after eighty kilometers <!-- @WF-route.overland: 80km -->, Noli and the boat, and does not finish. Benal gives the inventory, exact and hedged: two knives, two dying suits, no food, no fire, no boat, the rain on the bank gone by tomorrow, and nothing here that anybody wrote on. Teva, last, flat: she brought them here. Nobody answers her, and nobody says it will be all right. *(Q-PMHV, Daniel: the dark night that follows finding almost nothing useful; `plan/voice-sheets.md`: Keo asks and abandons the sentence, Benal explains and hedges, Teva keeps assertion to the bone.)*
3. **[body] [turn — TEVA]** Later, the other two asleep or near it. Her flash: light where there is none, and in it this same place, dry and lit, the vault whole overhead and the machines standing on a dry floor below the gallery, and people in suits like hers down among them, arguing. Some have loads on their backs and stand at the doorway to go; others face them. Everyone is talking and she cannot hear any of it. She takes them for the founders, from the suits and the place, and the loads tell her which are leaving: her own people, the ones who walked `[-]`. Nobody ever told her there was an argument; the story she knows begins on the turtle beach *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3)*. The faces that hold her are the ones with the loads, and they are not wicked: terror, grief, stubborn paralysis *(`plan/outline.md`: the ancestors' faces)*. She does not know whether any of it is real *(`plan/scene-list.md` D17.1: she did not know they were real)*. *(What it shows is a last flare of the argument at the leaving, not the vote: the brief runs the argument, then the preparation, then the walk (`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3); the drafter does not call it the vote.)* Then dark. She says nothing about it. *(`character-arcs.md` §1, Day 15: the turn; `plan/outline.md`, *Dark Night of the Soul*; `plan/knowledge-ledger.md` FH: the schism, partial; the jump is D17.2's.)*
4. **[body]** Benal jerks awake and says he dreamed his own equation, written in a hand that was not his, with one term in it he has never written, and it felt like being lied to. He calls it a nightmare and does not want to talk about it. *(`plan/outline.md`, *Dark Night of the Soul*; D17.2 recognizes the term.)*
5. Keo says it first, into the dark, with the day under it: he destroyed his family's lives for nothing. And then Yara. ## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** They have said aloud what Genesis has given them so far, which is almost nothing, each in their own way. Each has said the thing they hid. Keo↔Benal is at 9. Teva has seen the founders afraid, and has stopped hiding. Benal has had the fragment and called it a nightmare. Keo has asked, and she does not remember. They have agreed that this cannot be all of Genesis, and that in the morning they go and look.

**Worked on and did not change.** Benal's capacitor, still untold *(`character-arcs.md` §2, Benal 10–11: he tells nobody; D16.1 IN)*. The situation: no food, no fire, no way into the station. They can leave the gallery down the stair, and the water below is going down with the surge.

**Left open.** What the flashes are. What the rest of Genesis holds.

**Refused outright.** The therapy circle: the outline's lines are a source, not a script; someone resists, someone says the wrong thing first. Silence in place of the despair. Comfort that works: nobody says it will be all right. Teva knowing what Keo's question meant. Anyone explaining a flash. Anyone remarking that the founders left because this plant failed.
