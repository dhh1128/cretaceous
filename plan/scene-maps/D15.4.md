## MOVES

1. **[opens] [body]** Full dark in the upper store. Teva awake at one end of the three of them, Keo between her and Benal, shaking against her.
2. **[body] [turn — TEVA]** Her flash: light where there is none, and in it this same room, dry and lit, its shelves full, and people in suits like hers in it, arguing. Some have loads on their backs, taken off the shelves, and stand at the door to go; others face them. Everyone is talking and she cannot hear any of it. She takes them for the founders, from the suits and the place, and the loads tell her which are leaving: her own people, the ones who walked `[-]`. Nobody ever told her there was an argument; the story she knows begins on the turtle beach *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3)*. The faces that hold her are the ones with the loads, and they are not wicked: terror, grief, stubborn paralysis *(`plan/outline.md`: the ancestors' faces)*. She does not know whether any of it is real *(`plan/scene-list.md` D17.1: she did not know they were real)*. Then dark. She says nothing about it. *(`character-arcs.md` §1, Day 15: the turn; `plan/outline.md`, *Dark Night of the Soul*; `plan/knowledge-ledger.md` FH: the schism, partial; the jump is D17.2's.)*
3. **[body]** Benal jerks awake and says he dreamed his own equation, written in a hand that was not his, with one term in it he has never written, and it felt like being lied to. He calls it a nightmare and does not want to talk about it. *(`plan/outline.md`, *Dark Night of the Soul*; D17.2 recognizes the term.)*
4. Keo says it first, into the dark: he destroyed his family's lives for nothing. And then Yara. They know how she died, as everyone does: after heavy rain, in a flooded grove away from the river, gathering snails, to a croc that had grown up inside the perimeter *(`content/D4.1.md`: "Everybody knew how Yara had died"; `plan/knowledge-ledger.md` FM)*. What neither of them has heard is his part: he was beside her, and his projection did not register it. He left the third suit on the rack because of her: to prove he could live through what she had not. *(`plan/outline.md`, *Dark Night of the Soul*; `plan/milieu-brief.md` §4; `plan/foreshadow-and-motif.md` R2, R21, R25; `plan/knowledge-ledger.md` M: Teva and Benal learn it here.)* `[requires D2.11 — the third suit left on the rack]` `[requires D4.1 — Keo gathers the snails after rain, quickly and well, and says nothing about why he knows how]`
5. Benal answers it wrong: he says something exact and useless, an arithmetic of what Keo's parents still have. Teva tells him to stop. *(scene-list D15.4: someone says the wrong thing first.)*
6. Benal, after a while, and against his own wish to say nothing: his parents pity him; his work is useless; he came to find an answer and he has lost even what he had written down. *(`plan/outline.md`, *Dark Night of the Soul*; the board went with the fieldpack, D14.2.)*
7. Keo: his don't pity him, they don't trust him; they see a child to protect. Benal says his father told him to do something real. Keo says his told him to stay inside the perimeter `[-]`, which is the same thing said the other way round, and they both came anyway. Benal laughs once, in the dark, and says yes. *(`plan/outline.md`, *Dark Night of the Soul*: we're fighting the same battle.)*
8. **[relation] [turn — TEVA]** Teva's bridge, and it costs her more than it comforts them: they are lucky, they have parents to fight with; she left the only one she has, while she is disappearing; and her mother died of a bite gone bad and a Seeding that did not take, the same as Alira, and she sat and listened to the breathing. She says she is cold and frightened, which she has never said. She has not said any of it to herself before, out loud. *(`plan/voice-sheets.md`, Teva, does not say: until Day 15 night, where she stops hiding.)* *(`plan/foreshadow-and-motif.md` R10; `plan/backstory.md` T16.)* *(`plan/outline.md`, *Dark Night of the Soul*; `character-arcs.md` §2: this is where she stops hiding.)*
9. Nobody answers that, and Keo finds her hand in the dark.
10. **[relation]** Later, Teva tells Benal about the boy who used to shout to make him cover his ears, and that Keo made him stop. Benal did not know. He asks Keo why he never said; Keo says there was nothing to say. Benal leans his shoulder into Keo's and leaves it there. *(K26, T27; Keo↔Benal to 9.)*
11. **[relation]** Keo, lightly, asks Teva if she remembers chasing him round the nests when they were small, threatening to kiss him. She does not. He laughs it off, and after a while he lets go of her hand. *(K22, T30; strict third: she hears a joke and feels the hand go.)* What Keo has just watched, her turned toward Benal through move 10, is in it too `[-]` *(`character-arcs.md` §3 table, Night 15: Teva→Benal 7, and Keo sees it)*; she does not know that, and the reader can.
12. **[closes]** The three of them in the upper store, the wind gone quiet in the opening, and none of it having changed the situation at all. *Ending **SETTLED***.

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** Each has said the thing they hid. Keo↔Benal is at 9. Teva has seen the founders afraid, and has stopped hiding. Benal has had the fragment and called it a nightmare. Keo has asked, and she does not remember.

**Worked on and did not change.** Benal's capacitor, still untold *(`character-arcs.md` §2, Benal 10–11: he tells nobody; D16.1 IN)*. The situation: no food, no fire, no way into the station. They can leave the upper store down the rubble, and the water round it is going down with the surge.

**Left open.** What the flashes are.

**Refused outright.** The therapy circle: the outline's lines are a source, not a script; someone resists, someone says the wrong thing first. Teva knowing what Keo's question meant. Anyone explaining a flash.
