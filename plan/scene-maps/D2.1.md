## SPINE

**The drill: get Noli to the Barrier, and hold her there.** It is what he does at dawn, and it is the exact skill D3.1 needs to get her through the tangle and out *(`tech-rules.md`: D3.1, holding Noli against the Barrier)*. **He does not know that yet** — the decision to go is tonight's — so the drill is habit and pride, and the reader will see later what it was rehearsal for.
