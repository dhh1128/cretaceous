# D2.3 — the family nest — KEO (close third)  ·  LONG, ~2,000 words

Forward map per `process/methodology-theory.md` §4, with IN as the form §4a requires. **Phase 4 is a hard stop** — the invention ledger at the foot goes to Daniel before any prose exists.

Purpose **BOND**. Ladders EM5 P1 S4 SP3, and Day 2 morning is *rest — the deposit* on three of four (`pacing-and-stakes.md` §6). **This is the most-demanded unmapped scene in the book**: `D2.6.md`, `D19.7.md` and `D19.8.md` arrive with five payloads between them, and every one of them is a thing this morning has to leave lying on the floor for someone else to pick up.

**The scene's whole job is to make the reader want this family back**, and then take the register away in front of them. `humor-plan.md` gives it one assignment — *this family has jokes that predate the novel* — and the register **turns** when Alira's name is said: the same jokes keep coming, and after the name Keo hears them as being handled. So the scene has two halves and the hinge between them is a name.

## IN

- **`PLACE`** — the family nest in the village core: a roofed platform **twelve to fifteen meters up** in the monkey-puzzle conifers, reached by a notched rope ladder through a round opening in the floor, walled with woven vine and divided by fern screens *(`milieu-brief.md` §4, and `content/superseded/04.1.md` for the opening in the floor, the vine wall, the fern-screen divider, the mats, the larder basket and the gear pile — **D2.10 is set in this room tonight and every object here must still be where D2.10 finds it**)*. Thirty meters of canopy overhead. The platform rides on ropes over greased hardwood saddles and self-compensates, so what a body feels is creak and small adjustment and nothing more. **The smell of pan baking at the cook pits has followed him up the ladder** `[-]` — **the family nest is in the outer ring of nest trees, near the open ground where the pits are** — the morning, ordinary, the family at breakfast. *The Barrier's smell was here and is moved to D2.1: gear that reeked of it would keep Noli away from Keo for the whole journey, and the corpus already puts the Barrier on the night stakes, not on anyone's kit.*
- **`TIME`** — Day 2, morning. Keo has come up from the perimeter, where he spent the dawn working Noli after a bad night *(D2.1)*. Alira died last evening and was returned last night *(D1.1, D1.2)*. **The Council session was called at her returning and everybody has known about it since** *(`D2.6.md` A2)*. - **`WEATHER`** — clear dawn going to heat, and the heat is already building *(`journey-calendar.md` §2, Day 2)*. Nothing in the scene remarks on it: `journey-calendar.md` forbids describing weather that is doing nothing, and this is ordinary heat on an ordinary morning. It does nothing to any body here that matters.
- **`LIGHT`** — **hard morning light broken through the vine walls** *(`journey-calendar.md` §2, Day 2, verbatim)*. Daylight, plenty of it; every face in the room is legible, which matters because two of the moves are Keo reading his father's hands and one is the reader reading Riel's.
- **`SOUND`** — the village waking under the platform, which Keo's hearing gives him more of than he wants and which the scene uses once. Rope creak. **Nothing is loud and nothing is defeating anyone's hearing.** Keo's hearing is strong and carries no stated price *(`milieu-brief.md` §9)*.

  - **Keo, Days 1–2:** wants *to be treated as an adult*; believes *the adults are broken; he is strong enough*; fears *being a child*; hides *the crush; Yara; that he is frightened.* **He does not yet believe *someone has to act*** — that is the *2, evening* row, and `D2.6.md`'s OUT says he did not walk into the chamber holding it. **So nobody in this room proposes Genesis**, Keo least of all. ## SPINE

**Breakfast, and then the parents getting ready to go down.** A domestic task running under the talk — Joram working on gear across his knees, Riel feeding them and then filling the day's water — which gives every line somewhere for the hands to be and lets the scene end by people leaving rather than by a conversation reaching a conclusion. **The talk does not conclude.** The parents go down the ladder to do their duty and Keo is left with the last thing his father said.

## MOVES

**The comic register runs the whole scene and changes jobs halfway.** Before Alira's name the family bits are warmth. After it the parents keep reaching for the same bits, because that is how this family carries grief, and **Keo hears every one of them as being handled like a child** and will not play. The jokes become the misreading: their kindness is exactly what he takes for condescension, which is FN in comic form. `humor-plan.md` asks for an inside joke that stops being funny; this is where the family's does.

One of the scene's two sensory items. **The Barrier is not here**: it was, and it moved to D2.1, where Noli is afraid of it. `[requires D2.1 — Keo coming off a dawn at the perimeter with Noli, a bad night behind him and the projection ache still on him]`* **Two sentences.**
