# D2.3 — the family nest — KEO (close third)  ·  LONG, ~2,000 words

Forward map per `process/methodology-theory.md` §4, with IN as the form §4a requires. **Phase 4 is a hard stop** — the invention ledger at the foot goes to Daniel before any prose exists.

Purpose **BOND**. Ladders EM5 P1 S4 SP3, and Day 2 morning is *rest — the deposit* on three of four (`pacing-and-stakes.md` §6). **This is the most-demanded unmapped scene in the book**: `D2.6.md`, `D19.7.md` and `D19.8.md` arrive with five payloads between them, and every one of them is a thing this morning has to leave lying on the floor for someone else to pick up.

**The scene's whole job is to make the reader want this family back**, and then take the register away in front of them. `humor-plan.md` gives it one assignment — *this family has jokes that predate the novel* — and a hard edge: **the register dies the instant Alira's name is said and does not return.** So the scene has two halves and the hinge between them is a name.

## IN

- **`PLACE`** — the family nest in the village core: a roofed platform **twelve to fifteen meters up** in the monkey-puzzle conifers, reached by a notched rope ladder through a round opening in the floor, walled with woven vine and divided by fern screens *(`milieu-brief.md` §4, and `content/superseded/04.1.md` for the opening in the floor, the vine wall, the fern-screen divider, the mats, the larder basket and the gear pile — **D2.10 is set in this room tonight and every object here must still be where D2.10 finds it**)*. Thirty meters of canopy overhead. The platform rides on ropes and pulleys and self-compensates, so what a body feels is creak and small adjustment and nothing more. **The parents' Explorer gear is here, and it carries the Barrier** — the chemical ghost of it never washes out *(`milieu-allocation.md` §2 gives this scene exactly two sensory items and this is one)*.
- **`TIME`** — Day 2, morning. Keo has come up from the perimeter, where he spent the dawn working Noli after a bad night *(D2.1)*. Alira died last evening and was returned last night *(D1.1, D1.2)*. **The Council session was called at her returning and everybody has known about it since** *(`D2.6.md` A2)*. The day is young; the afternoon storm is hours off.
- **`WEATHER`** — clear dawn going to heat, and the heat is already building *(`journey-calendar.md` §2, Day 2)*. Nothing in the scene remarks on it: `journey-calendar.md` forbids describing weather that is doing nothing, and this is ordinary heat on an ordinary morning. It does nothing to any body here that matters.
- **`LIGHT`** — **hard morning slats through the vine walls** *(`journey-calendar.md` §2, Day 2, verbatim)*. Daylight, plenty of it; every face in the room is legible, which matters because two of the moves are Keo reading his father's hands and one is the reader reading Riel's.
- **`SOUND`** — the village waking under the platform, which Keo's hearing gives him more of than he wants and which the scene uses once. Rope creak. **Nothing is loud and nothing is defeating anyone's hearing.** Keo's hearing is strong and carries no stated price *(`milieu-brief.md` §9)*.
- **`CLOCK`** — `[-]` **it is the day of the weekly balm, and the custodians go down to the Repository this morning.** That is what ends the scene: the parents are getting ready to leave, and the last three moves are them leaving. It also gives Riel a reason to fill the day's water and gives Keo a target for his contempt at move 13. *(The weekly balm is canon — `milieu-brief.md` §6, `minor-characters.md` — and so is their custodianship; that today is the day is the choice.)*
- **`LOAD`** — nothing on anyone. They are at home.
- **`PRESENT`** — **Keo, Joram, Riel.** Nobody else. **Noli is off-page and loose** at the perimeter where Keo left her *(D2.1; `D2.6.md` places her the same way and gives the reason — nothing in canon gives her a pen)*. A sicklefoot does not climb a rope ladder, and the reason to say so is that she is the one living thing that keeps going unaccounted for.
- **`BODY`** — **nobody is hurt, hungry or short of water**, and this is one of the last scenes where that is true. Keo: sixteen, unhurt, short of sleep, and still carrying the pressure behind the eyes from holding Noli at dawn *(D2.1; `milieu-brief.md` §7 — releasing a projection leaves a pounding ache)*. Joram and Riel: whole, fed, tired the way everyone in the Enclave is tired this morning. **Joram carries a healed injury** — see move 5 — and it is old and costs him nothing now.
- **`WANTS`** — **quoted from `character-arcs.md` §2 for Keo; the parents have no row there and take their stance from `voice-sheets.md` and `minor-characters.md`.**
  - **Keo, Days 1–2:** wants *to be treated as an adult*; believes *the adults are broken; he is strong enough*; fears *being a child*; hides *the crush; Yara; that he is frightened.* **He does not yet believe *someone has to act*** — that is the *2, evening* row, and `D2.6.md`'s OUT says he did not walk into the chamber holding it. **So nobody in this room proposes Genesis**, Keo least of all. What he wants from his parents this morning is smaller and worse: for them to *say something* tonight.
  - **Joram** says less than he means and expects to be understood, and never explains twice; what he will not say is what he is afraid of *(`voice-sheets.md`)*. **He wants Keo where he can see him** — the same want `D2.6.md` gives him in the chamber, and here it is served by keeping the boy talking at breakfast.
  - **Riel** gives a number where anyone else gives an impression *(`voice-sheets.md`)*. `[-]` **She wants the ordinary morning to last**, and the jokes are how she keeps it going; when it breaks she does a chore instead of answering, which is her way of ending an argument without losing it.
- **`CAPABILITIES`** — **mental projection** is in play only as its cost, the headache Keo brought up the ladder; it is taught at D2.1 and there is no animal here. **The proximity sense is neither named nor used** — Keo's and Riel's both — because `tech-rules.md` teaches it at D2.10, *in this room, on these two sleeping people*, and spending it here would take that scene's teaching. ## SPINE

**Breakfast, and then the parents getting ready to go down.** A domestic task running under the talk — Joram working on gear across his knees, Riel feeding them and then filling the day's water — which gives every line somewhere for the hands to be and lets the scene end by people leaving rather than by a conversation reaching a conclusion. **The talk does not conclude.** The parents go down the ladder to do their duty and Keo is left with the last thing his father said.

## MOVES

1. **[opens]** Keo comes up through the floor into the nest and the smell of his parents' gear is the first thing there. — *The Barrier, faint and chemical, on everything they own. `milieu-allocation.md` allots it here and it is one of two sensory items the scene gets. **He has known it since before he had words, and does not find it strange.*** `[requires D2.1 — Keo coming off a dawn at the perimeter with Noli, a bad night behind him and the projection ache still on him]` **Two sentences.**

2. **[image]** The room in the morning: hard slats of light, the platform giving and taking under three people who lean with it without noticing. — *The second and last sensory item. **One sentence, and do not write the sway as texture** — `milieu-brief.md` §4 says it is not what living there feels like. Nothing else about the room is described; D2.10 is the scene that gets its objects.*

3. **[spine]** Riel answers a question nobody asked with a number, and the other two take it off her. — *The first comic move. **She gives a figure where an impression would do** — how long he was gone, how much of something is left — and Keo and Joram run the family's old bit on it, the tease exaggerating her the way family teases do. `voice-sheets.md`: **the caricature belongs to the family, not to the narration.** The prose never plays her as an abacus; the two of them do, and she lets them.* `[-]` *What the number is about.*

4. Joram's contribution to the bit is one word, and the other two supply the rest of his sentence for him. — *The second comic move and the second of `humor-plan.md`'s two named bits: **an old running joke about Joram's silence that all three can run without explaining.** He lets them. **This is the move that has to make the reader like him**, because every later scene he is in — D2.6, D2.10, D19.7 — shows him saying nothing, and silence the reader has already laughed at reads as a person rather than as a wall.* `[-]` *The content of the exchange.*

5. **[ledger]** Keo pokes at something about his father's body — a scar, a stiffness — and it turns into the story of an injury that closed in days and then left Joram hollow for a fortnight. **Riel supplies the numbers.** — ***The healing rule, and its price, taught cost-free.*** *`tech-rules.md` names this scene and this device: an old injury of Joram's that healed in days and left him hollow for a fortnight, mentioned in passing while he works, four lines. **The limit is the half that matters** — Days 11 to 13 spend it on Keo — so the fortnight gets the weight and the healing gets a clause. **It is still the comic register**: the family is ribbing Joram about the fortnight he spent asleep on his feet, and Riel's figures are the punchline.* `[plants → D11.4 — accelerated healing costs strength, warmth and clarity, taught before it is spent]` `[-]` *What the injury was, and where; it may not be a bite, a croc or water, because those are D2.10's.*

7. Do not let it run long enough to become a comic scene.**

## OUT — deltas against IN
