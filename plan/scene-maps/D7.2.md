## SPINE

## MOVES

1. **[opens] [body]** **Teva at the water's edge, and the fear she hides.** — *The lay-up spent on the bank in the heat, **nobody sleeping** `[-]`. **Her body will not let her within ten paces of it** `[-]`; she stands where she can see it and hates that she cannot stop looking. **Keo walks down to the edge and crouches there**, easy, unsuited — **she reads it as a liability**; the reader, who has Yara, reads it as something else *(READER)*.* **~10%.**

2. **[spine] [turn — BENAL]** **The raft, and what it would cost him.** — *Benal proposes it, because it is the engineer's answer `[-]`. **It dies on the surface**: a raft is a paddled, splashing, slow thing on mirror-flat water, the loudest thing on the river for the whole width *(§2)*, and building it takes days they do not have *(the food count)*. **It costs him**: he worked it out before he finished saying it, and said it anyway.* **~10%.**

3. **[spine] [turn — KEO] [ledger]** **The saropo, and the wall.** — *`journey-calendar.md` §1: dies on **control, not size**. **Who raises it is the cost**: Benal, half joking `[-]` — and **Keo has to say why not**, which means telling them about the wall *(D6.3)*: **nobody can steer one; he tried, and it did not know he was there**; being shaken off or left standing mid-channel is worse than swimming *(§1)*. **The cost is Keo's**: he admits a limit aloud. Teva sees him go quiet and **misreads it** `[-]` as sulking.* **~12%.**

4. **[spine] [turn — KEO]** **Upstream and around.** — *Keo wants to walk: **follow the bank and hope** it narrows, or **go around** *(`scene-list.md`)*. Teva kills it with what she knows: **rivers on this plain gather toward the sea and do not narrow upstream into nothing for days** `[-]` *(`journey-calendar.md` §1: the channel is more plausible at forty kilometers than at eight)*; and **the food count** — what they have counted *(pinyons, honey)* is not enough for days of going around. **It costs Keo his instinct**: walking is the only thing he has been sure of.* **~10%.**

5. **[spine] [turn — BENAL] [ledger]** **Go home.** — *`scene-list.md`: go home. **Who says it is L1.** Proposed: **Benal**, quietly — the boy who said yesterday was the best day of his life. **Teva kills it**, and this is where her **shared guilt** lives *(`scene-list.md`)*: the Council sat and argued while **Alira died** *(D1.1)*, and going home is sitting with them. **She is certain**, and her certainty is louder than her fear for the length of a speech — **the marshaling register, rationed** *(`voice-sheets.md`: evidence in order, delivered flat; three times in the book)* `[-]`. **It costs Benal** the day before.* **~14%.**

6. **[spine] [turn — TEVA]** **The narrows, and her authority.** — *What is left is the one fixed point every child knows: **the First Walk crossed at the narrows** *(`milieu-brief.md`)*. **Teva takes it openly, for the first time** *(`scene-list.md`)*: her magnetic sense and the suit's distance tell her **how far off the First Walk's line they came out of the woodland**, and which way — **her leftover error from Day 3** *(`tech-rules.md`)*. She leads them along the bank. **Three kilometers** *(`journey-calendar.md` §1)*, in the late heat, and **there it is: the channel tightening between boulders**, quicker water, a shorter gap *(§2)*.* **~14%.**

7. **[spine] [turn — ALL]** **The plan, and each of them earns a part.** — *`scene-list.md`: **theirs, and earned.** **Teva: the narrows**, which she found. **Keo: the decoy** — **a herd of wild hadrosaurs** `[-]` **grazing the bank upstream of the narrows**, which they pass on the walk, the wild kin of the Enclave's labor animals *(no species showcase; `milieu-allocation.md` holds the Day 10 herd)*: he can hold one, briefly *(`milieu-brief.md` §9)*, so **drive one into the slow wide water fifty yards upstream** *(§2)*, where it will stay where it is put. **Benal: the hour and the current** — **go in when the light reaches into the water and the bank is lying up**, and **the current carries their own ripples away from the decoy** *(§2: the part that makes the plan good rather than lucky)*. **Teva** supplies the fact it all rests on, the one she drilled into the yazhi: **they hear the surface** *(D2.5)*. **None of them is confident** *(D8.1 hazard)*. **The herd's own churning across a wide front is not in the plan** *(§2: not theirs, and must not read as theirs)*.* **~14%.**
