## Work queue, found by walking the invariants

- **Canon premise run.** *A raft as the loudest thing on still water*, and **in full view of the bank** — the crocs see as well as feel. It would take cutting trunks with knives `[-]`. *Ripples and the current*: surface waves go every way on still water, but **short ripples cannot travel upstream against water moving faster than they do** — the slowest ripples move at roughly a fifth of a meter a second, and quick water at a narrows outruns them — so the swimmers' small ripples are swept downstream, away from the decoy above; **a big splash makes longer, faster waves that could go up**, which is why they must swim quietly *(this supports `journey-calendar.md` §2's mechanism and names its limit)*. *The decoy staying*: projection holds a wild hadrosaur briefly *(`milieu-brief.md` §9)*, and the crossing of the gap is minutes; slow water keeps it from drifting while he holds it. The herd churning is the help that is not theirs. *A wild hadrosaur herd on a river bank*: ordinary; the Enclave keeps hadrosaurs as labor animals *(`milieu-allocation.md`: hadrosaur pens)*, so Keo knows how they move. *Rivers on a low-relief plain do not narrow upstream into a ford within days*: `journey-calendar.md` §1–2. *Teva's error recovered by heading and distance*: `tech-rules.md`. Passes.
