## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** They are in the air, on a charge that the voice says will not reach, by a choice all three made. The lift has cost what Benal said, and the lift units are now hoarded for the tangle, which all three have heard named. Keo has flown the wig off the hard and over the sea, and he and Benal have sung Liaso's song together. Teva has held a heading over water. The coast is in front of them.

**Worked on and did not change.** The charge, which only goes down from here. The cylinder, unexplained.

**Left open.** Whether it reaches. What the flood will be from the air. What Benal dug up.

**Refused outright.** The triumphant takeoff *(`plan/scene-list.md` D19.3 hazard)*: they are frightened, the machine is old, and the first minute feels like a mistake. The flybeak, or its shadow, which is D19.4's *(`plan/milieu-allocation.md` §3)*. The flood seen whole, which is D19.4's *(`plan/scene-list.md` D19.4)*. The voice flying the craft *(`kb/worldbuilding/the-wig.md` §5)*. A planing run off the ramp into the lagoon *(`kb/worldbuilding/the-wig.md` §2)*. Nothing refuses feeling: the fear is in their bodies and their voices, and the song is how two of them carry it *(Daniel, D15.1: deadpan is worse)*.
