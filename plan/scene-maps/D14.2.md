# D14.2 — the storm — BENAL (close third)  ·  LONG, ~1,800 words

Forward map per `process/methodology-theory.md` §4. Moves carry events and changes, not wording; how a line is said is the drafter's. The band is a floor for this map's material, never for the prose. **Phase 4 is a hard stop** — the ledger at the foot goes to Daniel before any prose exists.

Purpose **TRAVERSE**, ending **THREAT**. Ladders EM9 P9 S7 SP6. Drafted script-first: moves 2–4 are carried by talk; from move 5 the storm takes the talk away, and what is said is shouted and short, so the rest is bodies, the sea and Benal's fear.

## IN

- **`PLACE`** — the raft, on open water between the coast and Genesis, which lies 5–8 km offshore *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3)*; at dusk they are perhaps two thirds of the way `[-]`. **The raft**: founder polymer floats on an alloy frame, a flat deck with a low rail of struts round it, room for three lying flat with the packs between them `[-]`. Nothing in sight on any side by the end.
- **`TIME`** — Day 14, from dusk into the night `[-]`. The afternoon of paddling is in the gap from D14.1.
- **`WEATHER`** — the black wall arrives: first a wind out of the calm, then rain, then the full storm, and waves like black moving mountains *(`journey-calendar.md` §2; scene-list D14.2)*. The first winds come off the land behind them and drive the raft seaward, the way they were going, though nobody aboard can know it `[-]` — which is how the sea puts them on the bank in the eye at dawn *(D15.1)*. The sea is warm, 28–32 °C *(`body-and-resources.md` §3, Day 15)*.
- **`LIGHT`** — the last of the day under the black, then none.
- **`SOUND`** — wind, then rain, then the sea; **rain defeats Benal's hearing** *(`plan/tech-rules.md`)*, and there is nothing to hear through it and nobody who could be told anything.
- **`CLOCK`** — none they can act on. The night does not end inside the scene.
- **`LOAD`** — the raft; Benal's pack and the fieldpack, on him; Teva's pack; the two paddle blades broken from the rail, Keo's spike and Teva's bare shaft, lying along the deck `[-]`; the razor-club; the three skins. No cord left to tie anything down *(D13.2: the coil and the binding went into the frame)*.
- **`PRESENT`** — the three of them.
- **`BODY`** — as D14.1's exit, plus an afternoon of paddling in glassy heat: **Teva** hot in a suit that cools patchily, the headache splitting; **Keo** spent; **Benal** at the end of what his chest and arm can do. Salt is in every cut *(`body-and-resources.md` §3, Day 14)*.
- **`WANTS`** — `character-arcs.md` §2. **Benal:** to keep watch, and there is nothing to watch with. His fears arrive together: chaos, drowning and physical helplessness *(scene-list D14.2)*. **Teva:** to get them there, and she is about to lose the way. **Keo:** to keep them on the raft.
- **`RELATIONS`** — `character-arcs.md` §3, D14.2: **nobody manages anything; they hold on, and Keo holds Benal on the raft, in the body, where Keo and Benal have always worked.** Dyads: Keo↔Benal 8; Teva→Benal 7.
- **`REGARD`** — `character-arcs.md` §4, Days 12–15: the reader fears for all three.
- **`TEXTURE`** — none spent.
- **`CAPABILITIES`** — **Teva's magnetic sense**, the only thing to steer by offshore, and the headache takes it *(`character-arcs.md` §1)*. **Benal's hearing**, defeated by the rain. **The suits**, past helping: the film starving, unfed since Day 6, and Benal's capacitor dead since Day 11 *(`body-and-resources.md` §3b)*. **Keo's projection** has nothing here to push.
- **`READER`** — computed; no row is paid. The raft is founder-era and has not dissolved, and nobody says so *(`plan/tech-rules.md`, archaea failsafe; R7 pays at D17.2)*.
- **`PREMISE`** — the mash is settled; Teva's headache is the one residue assumption 6 allows. This scene breaks none.

## SPINE

**Holding on to the raft**, from the last of the light to the middle of the night.

## MOVES

1. **[opens] [world]** Dusk on the raft. The black is half the sky. The water is still glassy, and they are paddling, slowly, with the two blades.
2. **[body] [turn — TEVA]** Teva gives the heading, and corrects it, and corrects it again, and says she cannot hold it: the headache has taken it. Nobody can see Genesis. *(`character-arcs.md` §1: her own Day 12 decision takes the navigation from them.)*
3. Benal asks her which way it was last; she points, and Keo turns them that way. It is good for as long as nothing moves them, and then the wind comes.
4. A wind comes out of the calm, off the land behind them. Keo tells them to get down flat and get their hands through the rail.
5. **[world]** The rain comes, and Benal's hearing goes with it; there is nothing left to listen for.
6. **[world]** The sea builds. The raft climbs and drops. A wave over the deck takes the paddle blades and Teva's pack, and nobody can reach for them `[-]`. There is nothing to steer with and no heading to steer to.
7. **[body]** A wave puts the sea inside Benal's collar and he comes up choking; his chest will not take a full breath after it. *(Benal's fears — chaos, drowning, helplessness — arrive as things happening to him, in proportion.)*
8. **[relation] [spine]** A strut of the rail breaks under Benal's hands and he slides. Keo, with his good foot braced against the next strut and one arm through the rail, gets his other hand in the back of Benal's suit and holds him on, wave after wave, with his rib taking it every time and no words for any of it. *(`character-arcs.md` §3, D14.2.)*
9. **[body]** Benal starts counting the waves, to have something to count, and loses the count, and does not start again.
10. **[world]** The raft holds, an old thing that has no business holding. Nobody remarks on it.
11. Teva sees a bigger wave coming and shouts, and Benal gets his head down before it comes over.
12. **[closes]** Hours of it, and the night not over. *Ending **THREAT***.

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** They have lost the heading, both paddles, Teva's pack and any idea where Genesis is; the wind is driving them, and they do not know which way. Keo has held Benal on the raft through the worst of the night. Nothing has been managed.

**Worked on and did not change.** The raft, still holding. The fieldpack, still on Benal's back.

**Left open.** Where they are. Whether the raft lasts the night.

**Refused outright.** The storm as spectacle. Competence: nobody manages anything; they hold on. Anyone remarking that the raft has not dissolved. Benal brave.

## State closure

**Against D14.1:** the raft launched about midday, on Teva's heading; the afternoon's paddling in the gap.

**Against D14.3:** still night, still storm, the raft still in one piece, the fieldpack on Benal.
