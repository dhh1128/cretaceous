# D14.2 — the storm and the mosasaur — BENAL (close third)  ·  LONG, ~1,800 words

Forward map per `process/methodology-theory.md` §4. Moves carry events and changes, not wording; how a line is said is the drafter's. The band is a floor for this map's material, never for the prose. **Phase 4 is a hard stop** — the ledger at the foot goes to Daniel before any prose exists.

Purpose **CLASH**, ending **BLACKOUT**. Ladders EM9 P10 S7 SP6. One scene for the storm and the strike (Q-BCKT): the storm's events come fast, each a change, and the mosasaur ends it. Drafted script-first: moves 2–4 are carried by talk; after that what is said is shouted and short, and the rest is the sea, the animal and Benal's body.

## IN

- **`PLACE`** — the raft, on open water between the coast and Genesis, which lies 5–8 km offshore *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3)*; at dusk they are perhaps two thirds of the way `[-]`. **The raft**: founder polymer floats on an alloy frame, a flat deck with a low rail of struts round it, room for three lying flat with the packs between them `[-]`. Nothing in sight on any side by the end.
- **`TIME`** — Day 14, from dusk into the night `[-]`. The afternoon of paddling is in the gap from D14.1.
- **`WEATHER`** — the black wall arrives: first a wind out of the calm, then rain, then the full storm, and waves like black moving mountains *(`journey-calendar.md` §2; scene-list D14.2)*. The first winds come off the land behind them and drive the raft seaward, the way they were going, though nobody aboard can know it `[-]` — which is how the sea puts them on the bank in the eye at dawn *(D15.1)*. The sea is warm, 28–32 °C *(`body-and-resources.md` §3, Day 15)*.
- **`LIGHT`** — the last of the day under the black, then none. Later, only the faint glow left on Benal's forearm, and his display when he drives it: it runs off its own store, which the glassy afternoon filled, not the dead shoulder capacitor `[-]` *(`kb/worldbuilding/safety-suits.md`; D12.2: driven hard for a second or two)*.
- **`SOUND`** — wind, then rain, then the sea; **rain defeats Benal's hearing** *(`plan/tech-rules.md`)*, and there is nothing to hear through it and nobody who could be told anything.
- **`CLOCK`** — none they can act on.
- **`SMELL`** — brine and dead fish, the mosasaur's breath as it surfaces beside the raft *(`milieu-allocation.md` §5, Day 14)*.
- **`LOAD`** — the raft; Benal's pack and the fieldpack, on him; Teva's pack; the two paddle blades broken from the rail, Keo's spike and Teva's bare shaft, lying along the deck `[-]`; the razor-club; the three skins. No cord left to tie anything down *(D13.2: the coil and the binding went into the frame)*. In the fieldpack: Benal's board, wrapped in greased hide so the chalk work has lasted the wet days `[-]`, the croc tooth, the kindler, the last of the eggs *(`kb/worldbuilding/benals-equation.md`)*. The knives stay on their belts `[-]`.
- **`PRESENT`** — the three of them, then each alone; one *mosasaurus*, for one strike *(`milieu-allocation.md` §3)*.
- **`BODY`** — as D14.1's exit, plus an afternoon of paddling in glassy heat: **Teva** hot in a suit that cools patchily, the headache splitting; **Keo** spent; **Benal** at the end of what his chest and arm can do. Salt is in every cut *(`body-and-resources.md` §3, Day 14)*. Nobody can swim; Teva and Benal float in their suits, and Keo, unsuited, has only what he can hold *(`kb/worldbuilding/safety-suits.md`)*.
- **`WANTS`** — `character-arcs.md` §2. **Benal:** to keep watch, and there is nothing to watch with. His fears arrive together: chaos, drowning and physical helplessness *(scene-list D14.2)*. **Teva:** to get them there, and she is about to lose the way. **Keo:** to keep them on the raft.
- **`RELATIONS`** — `character-arcs.md` §3, D14.2: **nobody manages anything; they hold on, and Keo holds Benal on the raft, in the body, where Keo and Benal have always worked.** Dyads: Keo↔Benal 8; Teva→Benal 7. And **as the fieldpack and his mathematics go, Benal hears Marek's *do something real*, his one tie home at the moment of loss** *(`character-arcs.md` §3, D14.2)*.
- **`REGARD`** — `character-arcs.md` §4, Days 12–15: the reader fears for all three.
- **`TEXTURE`** — Marek's *do something real* *(D2.2)*.
- **`CAPABILITIES`** — **Teva's magnetic sense**, the only thing to steer by offshore, and the headache takes it *(`character-arcs.md` §1)*. **Benal's hearing**, defeated by the rain. **The suits**, past helping: the film starving, unfed since Day 6, and Benal's capacitor dead since Day 11 *(`body-and-resources.md` §3b)*. **Keo's projection** has nothing here to push.
- **`READER`** — computed; no row is paid. The raft is founder-era and has not dissolved, and nobody says so *(`plan/tech-rules.md`, archaea failsafe; R7 pays at D17.2)*. The fieldpack is the fourth of the five proofs of *water is death*, never enumerated *(`foreshadow-and-motif.md`)*, and the second of Benal's two losses *(`character-arcs.md`)*; the reader sees it as the setup for D17.2, and he sees only the end of his usefulness.
- **`PREMISE`** — the mash is settled; Teva's headache is the one residue assumption 6 allows. This scene breaks none.

## SPINE

**The raft**, from the last of the light to the strike that ends it.

## MOVES

1. **[opens] [world]** Dusk on the raft. The black is half the sky. The water is still glassy, and they paddle slowly with the two blades.
2. **[body] [turn — TEVA]** Teva gives the heading, corrects it, corrects it again, and says she cannot hold it: the headache has taken it. Nobody can see Genesis. *(`character-arcs.md` §1.)*
3. Benal asks which way it was last; she points, and Keo turns them that way.
4. A wind comes out of the calm, off the land behind them. Keo tells them to lie flat and get their hands through the rail.
5. **[world]** Rain, and Benal's hearing goes with it. The sea builds; the raft climbs and drops. A wave over the deck takes the paddles and Teva's pack `[-]`.
6. **[body]** A wave puts the sea inside Benal's collar and he comes up choking; his chest will not take a full breath.
7. **[relation]** A strut of the rail breaks under Benal's hands and he slides. Keo, good foot braced on the next strut and an arm through the rail, gets his other hand in the back of Benal's suit and holds him on, wave after wave, his rib taking it every time. *(`character-arcs.md` §3.)*
8. **[world]** In a trough, something breathes out beside the raft, close, and the smell comes over them: brine and dead fish. Then it is gone under.
9. **[world]** One strike from below. The raft comes apart, and he is in the water.
10. **[spine]** The fieldpack's strap is caught on a broken strut going down, and it takes him under with it. He holds on, and lets go. The fieldpack goes down, his board in it `[-]`.
11. **[relation]** As it goes he hears his father: *do something real*. He reads it as his failure. `[requires D2.2 — Marek's "do something real"]`
12. **[world] [image]** He comes up and drives his display, and for a second there is an eye beside him in the water, as large as his head; and past it, Keo, with his arms round a piece of the deck. Then dark, and the animal is gone before he has understood it. *(`milieu-allocation.md` §5: the eye, once.)*
13. He shouts their names, and nothing comes back.
14. **[closes]** The suit holds him up, and that is all it does. A wave puts him under, and another before he is up, and he swallows the sea and goes out. The suit keeps him up. *Ending **BLACKOUT***.

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** The heading, the paddles and Teva's pack went in the storm; the raft is in pieces. The fieldpack is gone, and with it his written work, the croc tooth, the kindler and the last of the food; the spike and the razor-club went with the raft. He has the equation only in his head. They are separated. He has seen Keo on a piece of the deck; Teva he has not seen. The wind has been driving them seaward, toward Genesis, and nobody knows it.

**Left open.** Whether the others are alive. Where the sea will put them.

**Refused outright.** The storm as spectacle. Anyone managing anything. A fight: one strike, in the dark, and the animal gone before it is understood. Benal brave. Anyone remarking on the raft's age, or enumerating *water is death*.

## State closure

**Against D14.1:** the raft launched about midday, on Teva's heading; the afternoon's paddling in the gap.

**Against D15.1** (Day 15, dawn, Benal, the coral bank): the sea puts all three on the coral bank in the eye at dawn, separately; Benal comes to and finds Keo wrapped around the piece of deck. How each lands is D15.1's.
