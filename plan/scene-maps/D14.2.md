Surf breaks white along the whole ring, and from outside it looks like one line with no gap `[-]`. **The boat**: founder polymer floats on an alloy frame, a flat deck with a low rail of struts round it, room for three lying flat with the packs between them `[-]`.

## SPINE

**The boat**, from the last of the light to the strike that ends it.

## MOVES

1. **[opens] [world]** The middle of the afternoon at the reef: surf breaking white along the coral ring, and Genesis beyond it, close enough to see the station's walls. Salt has dried white on his face and lips through the afternoon, and his skin is tight with it `[-]`.
2. **[spine]** They work along outside the break, looking for a way in. Benal listens for a place where the surf sounds different and cannot find one through the roar of it.
3. **[world]** The black comes over and the light goes. A wind out of the calm, off the land behind them, pushing them toward the break; it smells of the plain `[-]`. Keo tells them to lie flat and get their hands through the rail.
4. **[body] [turn — TEVA]** In the dark and the rain Teva tries to find the gap by her sense, and the headache takes it; she says she cannot. *(`character-arcs.md` §1.)*
5. **[world]** The rain takes Benal's hearing. The sea builds; the boat climbs and drops. A wave over the deck takes the paddles and Teva's pack `[-]`.
6. **[body]** A wave puts the sea inside Benal's collar and he comes up choking; his chest will not take a full breath.
7. **[relation]** A strut of the rail breaks under Benal's hands and he slides. Keo, good foot braced on the next strut and an arm through the rail, gets his other hand in the back of Benal's suit and holds him on, wave after wave, his rib taking it every time. *(`character-arcs.md` §3.)*
8. **[world]** In a trough the boat is lifted from below, not by a wave, and something long scrapes along the floats under them and goes on past. Then nothing.
9. **[world]** One strike from below, at the reef edge, which is where it hunts *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3: marine predator territory)*. The boat comes apart, and he is in the water.
10. **[spine]** The fieldpack's strap is caught on a broken strut going down, and it takes him under with it. He holds on, and lets go. The fieldpack goes down, his board in it `[-]`.
11. **[relation]** As it goes he hears his father: *do something real*. He reads it as his failure. `[requires D2.2 — Marek's "do something real"]`
12. **[world] [image]** He comes up and drives his display, and for a second there is an eye beside him in the water, as large as his head; and past it, Keo, with his arms round a piece of the deck. Then dark, and the animal is gone before he has understood it. *(`milieu-allocation.md` §5: the eye, once.)*
13. He shouts their names, and nothing comes back.
14. **[closes]** The suit holds him up, and that is all it does. The surf takes him toward the reef; a wave puts him under, and another before he is up, and he swallows the sea and goes out. The suit keeps him up. *Ending **BLACKOUT***.

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** They reached the reef and could not get through it. The paddles and Teva's pack went in the storm; the boat is in pieces at the reef edge. The fieldpack is gone, and with it his written work, the croc tooth, the kindler and the last of the food; the spike and the razor-club went with the boat. He has the equation only in his head. They are separated. He has seen Keo on a piece of the deck; Teva he has not seen. The wind and the surf are carrying them onto the reef.

**Left open.** Whether the others are alive. Where the sea will put them.

**Refused outright.** The storm as spectacle. Anyone managing anything. A fight: one strike, in the dark, and the animal gone before it is understood. Benal brave. Anyone remarking on the boat's age, or enumerating *water is death*.

## State closure

**Against D14.1:** the boat launched about midday, on Teva's heading; the afternoon's paddling in the gap.

**Against D15.1** (Day 15, dawn, Benal, the coral bank): the surf carries all three over the reef, and they wake on the bank at first light, the storm blown out, separately; Benal comes to and finds Keo wrapped around the piece of deck. How each lands is D15.1's.
