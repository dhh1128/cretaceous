## IN

The stair goes down into a hall full of black water and up two flights to a top landing under the roof, closed at the top by a door that does not open `[-]`. The roof over the stairwell is whole. **The break faces the land, so in the back half of the storm, whose wind comes from the sea, it is in the lee** `[-]` *(D14.2: the front half's wind came off the land; the back half's comes from the other side)*. They climb in over the slope; nobody goes into the water.
- **`TIME`** — Day 15, from the eyewall's return, early in the morning, through a dark gray day to dusk `[-]`. ## SPINE

**The hours**, from climbing in to full dark.

## MOVES

1. **[opens] [world]** The eyewall comes back over them from the sea, worse than the night: rain sideways. They go for the break.
2. **[spine]** They climb the slope of fallen wall, Benal first, Keo last on the ankle with Teva behind him, and go in over the edge into the stairwell. The wind drops the moment they are through: the break is in the lee.
3. **[world]** Below them the stair goes down into black water; above, two flights to a closed door under the roof. They go up and sit on the top landing with their backs to the wall.
4. **[body]** Keo has no suit and is wet through, and the gusts that eddy in find his skin. He shakes and cannot stop, and the other two put him between them.
5. **[relation]** To get through it they talk. Keo, to stop his teeth, starts on Topo, his ankle and the way he teases back. Benal asks Teva about her two friends, and she tells him they are closer to each other than to her, which she has not said before. Teva tells them walking Omya down to the Vitarium in the evenings. Small home things, told badly, and it warms them. *(`character-arcs.md` §3, D15.3; `plan/backstory.md` K18, T18, T3.)*
6. **[world]** Through the afternoon the water in the hall comes up the stair, one flight, and stops below them `[-]`. They watch it and do not move.
7. **[body] [ledger]** Keo is standing at the top of the lower flight, looking down at the water, and does not know how he got there. Benal has him by the arm. The landing tilts when nothing moves, and comes back. He says it is the cold. *(`plan/foreshadow-and-motif.md` R26: Keo worst in the flooded section.)*
8. **[closes]** The gray in the break goes to black, and the wind does not drop, and there are hours of it left. *Ending **THREAT***.

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** They are inside Genesis, on a top landing above the risen water, roofed, out of the wind, and wet. They have drunk rain. They have told home stories, and it has warmed them. Keo's shaking is the cost of having no suit.

**Left open.** The night.

**Refused outright.** Describing the storm: the subject is three people in a wet dark room. Wading: nobody goes into the water. Keo explaining the tilt. The rising water as a set piece: it comes up and stops.

## State closure

**Against D15.2:** the wind back and the light going; the first sight of the break.

**Against D15.4:** the same landing, full dark, the storm still on them, the same night.
