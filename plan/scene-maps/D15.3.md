## IN

- **`PLACE`** — **the upper store**: the last top module of the store row on the low ground, shoved askew by storms on the one below it, its end door gone *(D15.2)*. A bank of storm rubble heaped against the stack on the bank side, above the water, goes up to the opening. Inside, a long metal room, roofed, its floor a module's height above the ground and sagging a little in the middle; the far end door is shut and does not open `[-]`. Below it the lower module stands full of black water, the surge still in it, and the flood lies round the stack on the low-ground side; where the floor seam has split near the opening they can see the water down through it `[-]`. The roof is whole but for a split seam over the opening `[-]`. Off the ground and above the water is the point, the culture's first law kept for the night *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3: water is death)*. They climb in up the rubble; nobody goes into the water.
- **`TIME`** — Day 15, evening into the first of the dark `[-]`; D15.4 has the night.
- **`WEATHER`** — the storm is over *(`journey-calendar.md` §2, Day 15)*; the wind, dropping, still comes in at the opening off the sea, and that is enough on wet skin.
- **`LIGHT`** — gray through the opening, then none. The faint glow of what is left of the two suits' film.
- **`SOUND`** — the wind in the opening; the water in the module below, slapping; Genesis creaking; the surf, far off now.
- **`CLOCK`** — the night ahead, and nothing to do but get through it.
- **`LOAD`** — knives and suits; nothing else.
- **`PRESENT`** — the three of them.
- **`BODY`** — **All three** wind-chilled alike off wet skin, evaporative and not the sea *(`body-and-resources.md` §3, Day 15)*: cold that is a place, not a sky, and the suits are coolers that do nothing for it *(`journey-calendar.md` §2, Rules)*. No fire, because the kindler is gone *(D14.2)*. **Keo:** no suit, and never has had one: wet cloth and bare skin in the wind, everything the suits were for happening to him directly *(scene-list D15.3)*; cut all over from the shell; the rib; the ankle, so the rubble is a climb on his hands; the thigh closed and tender. **Teva:** her broken regulation makes the cold worst in her, and she feels it first *(`journey-calendar.md` §2, Rules; `plan/outline.md`, *Dark Night of the Soul*: Teva worst)*; she does not say so until the night *(`plan/voice-sheets.md`, Teva)*. **Benal:** chest raw from the sea, the arm weak *(D15.1)*. All three hungry, unfed since the eggs on Day 14. Rain from the night has run in at the split seam and stands where the floor sags; they drink it `[-]`.
- **`WANTS`** — `character-arcs.md` §2. **Keo, Days 14–17:** wants *to be useful again*; believes *Benal is the one who matters now*; fears *irrelevance*; hides *nothing — he has stopped*. Here: to get them through the hours.
- **`RELATIONS`** — `character-arcs.md` §3, D15.3: **the hours of wet dark are got through on home stories: Topo, Teva's two friends, Omya. It warms them toward the night.** Dyads: Keo→Teva 5; Keo↔Benal 8; Teva→Benal 7.
- **`REGARD`** — the reader fears for all three.

- **`CAPABILITIES`** — **the temporal disorientation, stronger inside Genesis than anywhere** *(scene-list D15.3; `kb/worldbuilding/the-second-jump.md`: the jump site is a scar on the island)*, and worst for Keo, who has had the flashes since Day 11 *(R26)*. The suits cover the other two's skin but not their faces or hands *(`kb/worldbuilding/safety-suits.md`; `content/D10.2.md`: the suit pulses at the wrists)*.
- **`READER`** — computed; **FK** grows: **Keo worst in the flooded section** *(`plan/foreshadow-and-motif.md` R26)*, the reader still not certain.

## SPINE

**The hours**, from climbing in to full dark.

## MOVES

1. **[opens] [world]** Evening; the light going. They go up the rubble to the opening, Benal first, then Keo on the ankle, slowly, using his hands and not the rib, Teva last behind him, and in over the lip into the upper store.
2. **[world]** Through the split seam by the opening, black water in the module below, the surge standing in it; outside, a line of weed and debris on the lower module's wall, below their floor, shows how high it came in the night. They go along the room to the far end, away from the opening and the wind, and sit with their backs to the shut door.
3. **[body]** Rain stands where the floor sags. They drink it, lying down to it, one at a time.
4. **[body]** Keo has no suit and is wet through, and the wind through the opening finds his skin. He shakes and cannot stop, and there is nothing to burn and nothing to light it with. The other two put him between them. Teva is shaking too, against his side, and says nothing about it.
5. **[relation]** To get through it they talk. Keo, to stop his teeth, starts on Topo: a story they have not heard, of Topo teasing him back `[-]` (D9.1 has already told the ankle and the slow walk home). Benal asks Teva about her two friends, and she tells him they are closer to each other than to her, which she has not said before. Teva tells them walking Omya down to the Vitarium in the evenings. Small home things, told badly, and it warms them. *(`character-arcs.md` §3, D15.3; `plan/backstory.md` K18, T18, T3.)*
6. **[world]** In the last light the water round the stack is lower than it was: more of the lower module's wall showing below the weed line. It is going out. *(The sea is dropping on the reef, and the water it held over the low ground drains with it, D15.2 `[-]`; D16.1 walks the low ground.)*
7. **[body] [ledger]** Keo is standing at the opening, on the lip above the rubble and the water, looking down, and does not know how he got there. Benal has him by the arm. The floor tilts when nothing moves, and comes back. He says it is the cold. *(`plan/foreshadow-and-motif.md` R26: Keo worst in the flooded section.)*
8. **[closes]** The gray in the opening goes to black, and the night is all ahead of them. *Ending **THREAT***.

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** They are inside Genesis, in the upper store above the flooded module and the flooded ground, roofed, off the ground, and wet. They have drunk rain. They have told home stories, and it has warmed them. Keo's shaking is the cost of having no suit; Teva's, which she does not mention, is her own regulation.

**Left open.** The night.

**Refused outright.** Wading: nobody goes into the water. Keo explaining the tilt. The water as a set piece: it is going out, and nobody remarks much on it.

## State closure

**Against D15.2:** the light going, the wind cold on wet skin; the first sight of the opening.

**Against D15.4:** the same room, full dark, the same night.
