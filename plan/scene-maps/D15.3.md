## IN

- **`PLACE`** — **the gallery of the plant house** *(Q-PMHV; D15.2)*: the raised concrete walkway along the wall where the membranes sat, above the floor, which is under the surge; roofed by the part of the vault that still stands; open at the far end, where the vault fell, and the wind comes in there and at the doorway off the sea `[-]`. They reach it up the concrete stair from the sill inside the doorway, above the water *(D15.2)*. Its edge has no rail, the steel one rusted away, and it stands well above the floor, where the surge lies shallow round the rust of the machinery `[-]`. **Seabirds roost on the gallery toward the fallen end**, where they come and go, and they rode out the storm there: that end is white with their mess and smells of it and of fish `[-]`; the inner end, against the house's end wall, is clean `[-]`. The rain that came in where the vault fell stands in the sags of the gallery floor there, foul with the birds `[-]`. Off the ground and above the water is the point, the culture's first law kept for the night *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3: water is death)*. Nobody goes into the water.
- **`TIME`** — Day 15, evening into the first of the dark `[-]`; D15.4 has the night.
- **`WEATHER`** — the storm is over *(`journey-calendar.md` §2, Day 15)*; the wind, dropping, still comes in off the sea at the fallen end, and that is enough on wet skin.

- **`SOUND`** — the wind at the fallen end; the water below the gallery, slapping and echoing under the vault; the birds settling; Genesis creaking; the surf, far off now.
- **`CLOCK`** — the night ahead, and nothing to do but get through it.
- **`LOAD`** — knives and suits; nothing else.
- **`PRESENT`** — the three of them; the seabirds, come back to roost at the fallen end after dark `[-]`.
- **`BODY`** — **All three** wind-chilled alike off wet skin, evaporative and not the sea *(`body-and-resources.md` §3, Day 15)*: cold that is a place, not a sky, and the suits are coolers that do nothing for it *(`journey-calendar.md` §2, Rules)*. No fire, because the kindler is gone *(D14.2)*. **Keo:** no suit, and never has had one: wet cloth and bare skin in the wind, everything the suits were for happening to him directly *(scene-list D15.3)*; cut all over from the shell; the rib; the ankle, so the stair is a climb on his hands; the thigh closed and tender. **Teva:** her broken regulation makes the cold worst in her, and she feels it first *(`journey-calendar.md` §2, Rules; `plan/outline.md`, *Dark Night of the Soul*: Teva worst)*; she does not say so until the night *(`plan/voice-sheets.md`, Teva)*. **Benal:** chest raw from the sea, the arm weak *(D15.1)*. All three hungry, unfed since the eggs on Day 14. ## SPINE

**The hours**, from climbing in to full dark.

## MOVES

1. **[opens] [world]** Evening; the light going. On the bank, before they go in, they kneel at the hollows and drink again from the rain still standing in them `[-]`. The small crabs that work the bank go into the cracks ahead of their hands faster than three spent people in the failing light can take them `[-]`. Then they go in at the doorway and up the stair to the gallery for the night, Benal first, Keo slowly on the ankle, Teva behind him `[-]`. *(The stair and the birds were D15.2's.)*
2. **[world]** Below the gallery's edge, well down, the water lies shallow on the floor round the rust; on the wall below them a line of weed and debris shows how high it came in the night. Before anyone settles, Keo looks down into the water and along it, end to end, as anyone raised on *water is death* would; nothing is there `[-]`. They go along the gallery to the inner end, away from the fallen end and the wind, and sit with their backs to the end wall.
3. **[world]** The fallen end is fouled where the birds roost, and the rain standing in the sags there is foul with them. Nobody drinks it; they drank at the bank's hollows before coming in `[-]` *(D15.1 move 9)*. They keep to the inner end, which the birds did not use.
4. **[body]** Keo has no suit and is wet through, and the wind along the gallery finds his skin. He shakes and cannot stop, and there is no fire and nothing to light one with. The other two put him between them. Teva is shaking too, against his side, and says nothing about it.
5. **[world]** In the last light the water below is lower than it was: more of the wall showing under the weed line. It is going out. *(The sea is dropping on the reef, and the water it held over the low ground drains with it, D15.2 `[-]`; D16.1 walks the low ground.)*
6. **[relation]** The gray at the fallen end goes to black. In the dark, to get through it, they talk. Nobody has said what they found today, and nobody says it yet `[-]` *(D15.4 move 2 says it)*. Keo, to stop his teeth, starts on Topo: a story they have not heard, of Topo teasing him back `[-]` (D9.1 has already told the ankle and the slow walk home). Benal asks Teva about her two friends, and she tells him they are closer to each other than to her, which she has not said before. Teva tells them walking Omya down to the Vitarium in the evenings. Small home things, told badly, and it warms them. *(`character-arcs.md` §3, D15.3; `plan/backstory.md` K18, T18, T3.)*
7. **[body] [ledger]** Keo is standing at the gallery's open edge in the dark, above the water he can hear below, with no rail, and does not know how he got there. Benal has him by the arm. The floor tilts when nothing moves, and comes back. He says it is the cold. *(`plan/foreshadow-and-motif.md` R26: Keo worst in the flooded section.)*
8. **[closes]** Benal brings him back to the inner end `[-]`, and the night is all ahead of them. *Ending **THREAT***.

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** They are inside Genesis, on the plant house gallery above the flooded floor and the flooded ground, roofed, off the ground, and wet. They last drank at the bank's hollows, at dusk. They have told home stories, and it has warmed them; what they found today is still unsaid. Keo's shaking is wet cloth in the wind, hunger, and what his healing costs him in warmth *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §4, §9; `plan/journey-calendar.md` §2: never solved by a suit, because a suit is a cooler)*; Teva's, which she does not mention, is her own regulation.

**Left open.** The night.

**Refused outright.** Wading: nobody goes into the water. Keo explaining the tilt. The water as a set piece: it is going out, and nobody remarks much on it. Anyone remarking what the plant house was for, or why the founders left.

## State closure

**Against D15.2:** the light going, the wind cold on wet skin; the gallery seen from below.

**Against D15.4:** the same gallery, full dark, the same night.
