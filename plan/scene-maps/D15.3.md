## IN

- **`PLACE`** — the seaward block's landward end *(D15.2)*: a break high in the wall above a slope of fallen wall, and inside, a stairwell. The stair goes down into a hall full of black water, the surge still standing in it, and up two flights to a top landing under the roof, closed at the top by a door that does not open `[-]`. The roof over the stairwell is whole, and off the ground is the point: nothing that hunts the shore at night comes up two flights `[-]`. They climb in over the slope; nobody goes into the water.
- **`TIME`** — Day 15, evening into the first of the dark `[-]`; D15.4 has the night.
- **`WEATHER`** — the storm is over *(`journey-calendar.md` §2, Day 15)*; the wind, dropping, still comes in through the break off the sea, and that is enough on wet skin.
- **`LIGHT`** — gray through the break, then none. The faint glow of what is left of the two suits' film.
- **`SOUND`** — the wind in the break; the water in the hall below, slapping; Genesis creaking; the surf, far off now.
- **`CLOCK`** — the night ahead, and nothing to do but get through it.
- **`LOAD`** — knives and suits; nothing else.
- **`PRESENT`** — the three of them.
- **`BODY`** — **Keo:** no suit, and never has had one: the wind on wet skin is the one genuine chill in the book, evaporative and not the sea *(`body-and-resources.md` §3, Day 15)*; no fire, because the kindler is gone *(D14.2)*; cut all over from the shell; the rib; the ankle; the thigh closed and tender. **Teva and Benal** in starving suits that do little, cold too but less. All three hungry. Rain from the night has run in through the broken roof-edge and stands in a hollow of the landing floor; they drink it `[-]`.
- **`WANTS`** — `character-arcs.md` §2. **Keo, Days 14–17:** wants *to be useful again*; believes *Benal is the one who matters now*; fears *irrelevance*; hides *nothing — he has stopped*. Here: to get them through the hours.

## SPINE

**The hours**, from climbing in to full dark.

## MOVES

1. **[opens] [world]** Evening; the light going. They go up the slope of fallen wall to the break, Benal first, Keo last on the ankle with Teva behind him, and in over the edge into the stairwell.
2. **[world]** Below them the stair goes down into black water, the surge standing in the hall; a line of weed and debris on the wall a flight up shows how high it came in the night. Above, two flights to a closed door under the roof. They go up and sit on the top landing with their backs to the wall.
3. **[body]** Rain stands in a hollow of the landing floor where the roof-edge is broken. They drink it, lying down to it, one at a time.
4. **[body]** Keo has no suit and is wet through, and the wind through the break finds his skin. He shakes and cannot stop, and there is nothing to burn and nothing to light it with. The other two put him between them.
5. **[relation]** To get through it they talk. Keo, to stop his teeth, starts on Topo, his ankle and the way he teases back. Benal asks Teva about her two friends, and she tells him they are closer to each other than to her, which she has not said before. Teva tells them walking Omya down to the Vitarium in the evenings. Small home things, told badly, and it warms them. *(`character-arcs.md` §3, D15.3; `plan/backstory.md` K18, T18, T3.)*
6. **[world]** In the last light the water in the hall is lower than it was: a step showing that was under. It is going out. *(The surge drains with the tide; D16.1 walks the low ground.)*
7. **[body] [ledger]** Keo is standing at the top of the lower flight, looking down at the water, and does not know how he got there. Benal has him by the arm. The landing tilts when nothing moves, and comes back. He says it is the cold. *(`plan/foreshadow-and-motif.md` R26: Keo worst in the flooded section.)*
8. **[closes]** The gray in the break goes to black, and the night is all ahead of them. *Ending **THREAT***.

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** They are inside Genesis, on a top landing above the flooded hall, roofed, off the ground, and wet. They have drunk rain. They have told home stories, and it has warmed them. Keo's shaking is the cost of having no suit.

**Left open.** The night.

**Refused outright.** Wading: nobody goes into the water. Keo explaining the tilt. The water as a set piece: it is going out, and nobody remarks much on it.

## State closure

**Against D15.2:** the light going, the wind cold on wet skin; the first sight of the break.

**Against D15.4:** the same landing, full dark, the same night.
