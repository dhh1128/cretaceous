## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** **Teva has crossed from rage to nuance** — the arc the whole book is named for, completed in a recognition she did not choose and cannot un-see. The thesis has been spoken once. She has given up a turn she fought nineteen days for.

**Worked on and did not change.** The speech, still unsaid and now unsayable. The accusation, abandoned mid-sentence. Keo's parents, still under guard. The three dyads, all where `character-arcs.md` leaves them: two unresolved and one settled.

**Left open.** Everything the hazard note requires: the cure, the schism, the asteroid, the wig. **And whether anyone in the room will ever know what she almost said.**

**Refused outright.** Her explaining herself. Any character other than Teva understanding what just happened to her. Whether she forgives them — the book takes no position on whether the founders were right *(`voice-sheets.md`, standing refusals)*. Whether Keo knows what she feels, in either direction.

**READER.** No `knowledge-ledger.md` row is paid — **FP is deliberately withheld and corrected by EP13 after the book ends**, which is the point of the false solution. **R15's second half lands at move 11.** No `[turn — READER ONLY]`; every turn is hers.
