- **`WEATHER`** — the eye: high bright cloud straight overhead, no wind, the sea still heaving on the reef, and all round the horizon the wall of the storm standing up like a cliff *(`journey-calendar.md` §2, Day 15; `milieu-allocation.md` §5: the eye, high and bright and wrong)*. ## SPINE

**Finding them**, from Benal waking alone to all three together, looking at the sky.

## MOVES

The eye is about an hour; this scene takes the first half of it `[-]`.

1. **[opens] [body]** Benal wakes on the shell bank above the wrack line, in bright light, cut, choking up seawater, with no wind at all. He does not know where he is.
2. **[world]** He sits up. The reef with the surf breaking on it; wrack and pieces of the raft along the bank; small iridescent crabs going away from him. Up the spit, the station's walls. He is on Genesis.
3. **[body]** He reaches for the fieldpack and it is not there, and he remembers letting it go. The board is in it. He has the equation only in his head. *(scene-list D15.1; `kb/worldbuilding/benals-equation.md`.)*
4. **[spine]** He walks the bank calling, and finds Keo a little way along, on his side above the wrack, one arm still through a piece of the deck; he dragged himself and it out of the surf before he went out `[-]`. Cut everywhere the suit would have covered. Breathing. Benal gets him sitting; Keo asks for Teva.
5. **[spine]** They go along the bank, Keo leaning on him, and hear her before they see her: she is calling their names, hoarse, from beyond a spur of rock.
6. **[spine]** Teva, in the wrack, a cut on her forehead below the helmet's rim. When she sees them she stops calling and says nothing for a while. Then she says they are here. Nobody is glad out loud. *(Hazard: the reunion as relief.)*
7. **[world]** High bright cloud, no wind, the sun through it. Keo says it is over, and for a moment it looks like it is: the reader is allowed to feel rescued *(scene-list D15.1)*.
8. **[body] [ledger]** For a moment the sound of the surf comes to Benal twice, the same wave breaking again exactly as it broke. Keo stops and stares at nothing; Teva puts a hand to her eyes. Nobody says anything about it. *(`plan/foreshadow-and-motif.md` R26: all three on the bank, each differently; Benal can know only his own and what he sees of theirs.)* `[requires D14.1 — Benal's first flash, the sound that repeats exactly]`
9. **[body]** A hollow in the limestone still holds some of the night's rain, fresh enough to drink. A few mouthfuls each, kneeling, and it is gone `[-]`. *(Rain drains through coral within the hour, `plan/milieu-brief.md` §3, so it will not last.)*
10. **[closes] [world]** Benal turns all the way round. Past the reef on every side the storm stands up off the sea like a wall, and the seabirds are going round and round inside it because they cannot get out. He understands what the calm is, and that the back of it is coming. *Ending **REVEAL***.

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** All three are on Genesis, alive and together, with nothing but their knives, their suits and Keo's clothes. Benal knows the fieldpack is gone. They have drunk a few mouthfuls; nothing is left. Benal knows they are in the eye.

**Worked on and did not change.** Benal's capacitor, still untold. The headache.

**Left open.** How long they have before the back wall. How to get inside.

**Refused outright.** The reunion as relief. Anyone saying they made it. Benal brave.
