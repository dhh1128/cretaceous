## SPINE

**Finding them**, from Benal waking alone to all three together, looking at the sky.

## MOVES

1. **[opens] [body]** Benal wakes on the shell bank above the line of washed-up weed, shadowless light, wind still coming off the sea, cut, choking up seawater. He does not know where he is, and the wind carries nothing he can read `[-]`.
2. **[world]** He sits up. The reef with the surf breaking on it; weed, driftwood and pieces of the boat along the bank; small iridescent crabs going away from him sideways into the cracks, like the river's crayfish and not `[-]`; seabirds quarreling over the weed `[-]`. Beyond the bank, across the flooded low ground, the crown stands up pale; along the bank, the station's walls `[-]`. He is on Genesis.
3. **[body]** He reaches for the fieldpack and it is not there, and he remembers letting it go. The board is in it. He has the equation only in his head. *(scene-list D15.1; `kb/worldbuilding/benals-equation.md`.)*
4. **[spine]** He walks the bank calling, and finds Keo a little way along, on his side above the weed line, one arm still through a piece of the deck; he dragged himself and it out of the surf before he went out `[-]`. Cut everywhere the suit would have covered. Breathing. Benal gets him sitting; Keo asks for Teva.
5. **[spine]** They go along the bank, Keo leaning on him, and hear her before they see her: she is calling their names, hoarse, from beyond a spur of rock.
6. **[spine]** Teva, among the washed-up weed, a cut on her forehead below the helmet's rim. When she sees them she stops calling and says nothing for a while. Then she says they are here. Nobody is glad out loud. *(Hazard: the reunion as relief.)*
7. Keo starts to say they made it, and stops, because nobody can see what they have made. *(Refused: anyone saying they made it.)*
8. **[body] [ledger]** For a moment the sound of the surf comes to Benal twice, the same wave breaking again exactly as it broke. Keo stops and stares at nothing; Teva puts a hand to her eyes. Nobody says anything about it. *(`plan/foreshadow-and-motif.md` R26: all three on the bank, each differently; Benal can know only his own and what he sees of theirs.)* `[requires D14.1 — Benal's first flash, the sound that repeats exactly]`
9. **[body]** The night's rain stands in hollows all over the limestone, fresh enough to drink. They kneel and drink their fill, the first since the skins went `[-]`. Benal says, to nobody, that it will not stand long on this ground. *(`plan/milieu-brief.md` §3: coral and shell are porous, and what falls drains.)*
10. **[closes] [world]** He looks along the bank at the station: walls standing, salt-white, and no light, no sound, no one. They have walked eighty kilometers <!-- @WF-route.overland: 80km --> to it, and it does not know they are here. *Ending **REVEAL***.

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** All three are on Genesis, alive and together, with nothing but two knives, the suits and Keo's clothes. Benal knows the fieldpack is gone. They have drunk their fill from the storm's standing rain. They have seen the station close, and it is silent.

**Worked on and did not change.** Benal's capacitor, still untold. The headache.

**Left open.** How to get inside. How long the standing rain will last.

**Refused outright.** The reunion as relief. Anyone saying they made it. Benal brave.
