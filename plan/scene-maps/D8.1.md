- **Canon premise run.** *Nudging a herd along a bank over a morning*: projection nudges inclination *(`tech-rules.md`)*, and herd animals follow each other, so moving the lead animals moves the rest; it costs him. *Noli's leaps*: `scene-list.md` and `journey-calendar.md` §2 specify them. *The gel on skin*: §0's one exception. *The scene-list's "ends the morning"*: the approved calendar has Noli go an hour before the afternoon crossing, so her call ends the waiting in the lay-up, not the morning. Passes.
- **The gel and the herd.** The gel reads as an apex predator to anything smaller *(§0)*, which includes a herd of hadrosaurs at grazing distance; **so no one is gelled until the decoy is in the water**, and Keo gels last. **Noli's going is her own**, as the calendar has it.
- **Noli's unprotected crossing survives because of the hour**, the same reason they chose it: everything that hunts the bank is lying up in the noon heat.
