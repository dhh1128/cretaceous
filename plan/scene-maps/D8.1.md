Passes.
- **The gel and the herd.** The gel reads as an apex predator to anything smaller *(§0)*, which includes a herd of hadrosaurs at grazing distance; **so no one is gelled until the decoy is in the water**, and Keo gels last. **Noli's going is her own**, as the calendar has it.
