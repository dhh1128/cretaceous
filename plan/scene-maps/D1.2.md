# D1.2 — the returning — TEVA (close third)  ·  MEDIUM, ~1,300 words

Forward map per `process/methodology-theory.md` §4, with IN as the form §4a requires. **Phase 4 is a hard stop** — the invention ledger at the foot goes to Daniel before any prose exists.

Purpose **DISCOVER**, ending **RESOLVE**. Ladders EM6 P1 S4 SP3. **Demanded by `D2.6.md`**: *Alira's returning, and nothing under the mat within the hour*; and `D2.6.md` A2 — a ruling in an approved file — has the Council session **called at the returning**, which this scene has to contain.

**The scene's job is to teach the strangest fact about this culture at the moment it hurts most** (`scene-list.md`): the dead are covered, the balm goes on, and within an hour there is nothing under the mat. It gives the reader the Enclave as a place rather than a chamber, puts the first rung on the social ladder — a community that failed in public performing its most solemn rite over the consequence — and establishes the returning before the mudwalk, so that **Keo's unsuited body is legible as a risk** at D2.11 and after.

Nothing in this scene pays a ledger row. **The rite is new to the reader**, and the scene's whole method is that it is not new to Teva: **she does not explain it, because she knows it**, and the reader learns it by watching her know it.

## SPINE

**The rite, start to finish** — laying out, the names, the covering, the balm, the waiting, the nothing. **It is a procedure, and the procedure carries the scene**; Teva has nothing to do in it but watch and say one word.

## Work queue, found by walking the invariants

- **`milieu-brief.md` §4's *nothing under the covering within about an hour*** is beyond what any process does to a body with bone and teeth in it, engineered or not; all four seats said so. **This scene shows only the result — the mats lying flat — and never the process**, which is the least the claim can be asked to carry. Whether canon wants a mechanism or a longer clock is a question for `milieu-brief.md`, not for this scene.
- **`lingo.md`'s closed list does not hold *the returning*, *the balm* or *True Name*,** which `milieu-brief.md` uses throughout. They belong in its proper-nouns line, or a drafter told the list is closed will hesitate over the scene's own subject.
- **Why an unsuited death outside is worse than a suited one**: every colonist carries the flora, so the difference is not the returning itself but **a body taken before anyone can cover it**. The plant at move 10 now says so; D2.11 and D11.4 should not claim more.

- **What light the village has at night** is unstated anywhere. This map uses a fire for the rite and nothing else; lightmold is the kiva's.
