## IN

- **`PLACE`** — **the Vitarium**: the deep recessed chamber off the kiva, reached through a natural cleft *(`milieu-brief.md` §8)*. **Constant temperature, high humidity, darkness, and still air, because it is off the chimney.** Human cell lines in **lopsided clay vessels**, each shaped to look like a random clump *(`milieu-brief.md` §6, §8)*, set in clefts in the rock. **The protocol station and the sequencing tray of protein-gel keys** — the keys dissolve outside the chamber's humidity *(`milieu-brief.md` §8; the tray and station are `content/superseded/01.1.md`'s and are kept, see L3)*. Behind Teva, through the cleft, the kiva proper and its steady yellow-green lightmold.
- **`TIME`** — Day 1, evening. **Two to three weeks into the crisis** *(`milieu-brief.md` §8)*: the flood that killed two of the three Masters was that long ago, not *last season* as the draft has it. Alira is two and a half *(`minor-characters.md`)*.
- **`WEATHER`** — overcast and dead still outside *(`journey-calendar.md` §2, Day 1)*, and **none of it reaches the Vitarium.** The prose does not mention outside weather at all: *nothing outside acknowledges what happened inside* is the calendar's own dramatic note for the day.
- **`LIGHT`** — **the cultures' own glow and nothing else** *(`milieu-brief.md` §8: the Vitarium is lit by nothing but its own cultures, in two colors, going out)*. **Rust-red and blue, dimming toward gray; no third color, and a failing line loses its color rather than changing it.** Faces are legible only where a living culture is near. **The yellow-green of the kiva behind the cleft is the only other light, and it must never be confusable with the cultures** *(§8)*.
- **`SOUND`** — Rano's voice, the tray, the vessels, the still air. **Then Alira's breathing** — the wet rattle — and that is the sound the scene is built on. Teva's hearing is ordinary *(`milieu-brief.md` §9)*.
- **`SMELL`** — **autolysis**: the sweet denatured-protein decay of dying cultures *(`milieu-allocation.md` §5 allots it to Day 1, here)*. Teva's smell did not take *(`milieu-brief.md` §9)*, so she gets it as anybody would, and it is still unmistakable.
- **`TOUCH`** — **limestone underfoot, cold** *(`milieu-allocation.md` §5: Day 1, established once and never again)*. **Teva is cold**, the way she is in the kiva and the Vitarium: she cannot hold heat and is wearing no suit *(`milieu-brief.md` §9)*. Miserable, not dangerous.
- **`CLOCK`** — **Alira's breathing.** The test has no clock of its own — it can go on as long as Rano can stand it — and the scene's real duration is how long a two-year-old with a septic stingburn has. **What ends it is the breathing stopping.**
- **`LOAD`** — nothing.
- **`PRESENT`** — **Teva**, at the cleft. **Omya** at the protocol station. **Rano**, conducting the test. **The witnesses** — see **L1**. **Elana**, who is not in the room when it opens, and **Alira**, whom she carries in. Nobody else is named.
- **`BODY`** — **Teva:** sixteen, sturdy, unhurt, cold. **Omya:** old, lost tonight; plaited in a Master Keeper's loops `[-]`; her hands hesitate over the tray rather than trembling *(`D2.4.md` IN)*. **Rano:** worn through *(`minor-characters.md`)*. **Alira:** a stingburn at the wrist with a bruised line running up the arm *(`content/superseded/01.1.md`, kept — L3)*, septic, breathing wet. **Elana:** a mother who has run with a child.
- **`WANTS`** — **quoted from `character-arcs.md` §2 for Teva; the adults from `voice-sheets.md` and `minor-characters.md`.**
  - **Teva, Days 1–2:** wants *someone to blame*; believes *the adults chose this*; fears *that nothing could have been done*; hides *that she is not sure she can do it*. **She arrives wanting her grandmother to pass**, and leaves wanting somebody to blame, and the scene is the conversion.
  - **Omya** — lost tonight. **She wants to do the task in front of her**, and cannot tell which task it is. Food words when lost *(`voice-sheets.md`)*.
  - **Rano** is pleading with a person who cannot answer him, in front of people who are watching, and keeps going long after it is pointless, because stopping would mean saying it is over *(`voice-sheets.md`, verbatim)*.
  - **Elana** wants to be told it can still be done, and goes on asking after everyone in the room knows it cannot. She will not accuse anyone *(`voice-sheets.md`)*.
  - **The witnesses:** L1.
- **`CAPABILITIES`** — **none.** Teva's magnetic sense is D2.8's to teach; her failed senses are what make the smell ordinary and the dark dark. **The upgrades are in play only as the thing that is failing** — rust-red is T-cell activation, the immunity baseline; blue is lysine and digestion *(`milieu-brief.md` §8)*.
- **`KNOWS`** — Teva knows the protocol in fragments, knows Omya taught her the stars, knows the three-Master rule, and **watched her mother die of the same infection after a Seeding that did not take, two years ago** *(`milieu-brief.md` §9; `minor-characters.md`)*.
- **`READER`** — computed. **EP1 sits immediately before this scene**: a pathogen designated L-9, mortality 99.97%, no vaccine, no treatment. **That is FA and it is all the reader has.** **FB is not paid until EP5**: nothing here may let the reader suspect the upgrades are the cure. The bulletin and the dying child are about different diseases and nothing connects them.
