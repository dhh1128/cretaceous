## IN

- **`PLACE`** — the Council chamber, in the kiva: a large natural dissolution cave with a sloping cleft for a door and a ceratops hide hanging in it. **Bare walls, and they are "a monument to erasure"** — `foreshadow-and-motif.md` §3 makes that the strongest motif in the book, and this is the room where the motif is densest and nobody may say so. Sila sits at the center of the dais. *(`milieu-brief.md` §4.)*
- **`TIME`** — Day 19, evening. They landed in the middle of the afternoon; the handover at the Vitarium took four minutes *(D19.6)*; they were sent for. **Nineteen days <!-- @WF-story.span: 19d --> and eighty kilometers <!-- @WF-route.overland: 80km --> end in this room.**
- **`WEATHER`** — clear and hot outside, high hard light all the way home and no weather to hide behind, which was the price of the heresy *(`journey-calendar.md` §2)*. Inside, none of that reaches: limestone, constant temperature, high humidity. **The only body the weather is still working on is Keo's** — burned, unsuited, nineteen days <!-- @WF-story.span: 19d --> of it, now standing still in a cool room for the first time since Day 2.
- **`LIGHT`** — **lightmold only, and that is a change the scene can use.** The kiva's collapse hole drops one shaft of daylight that crosses the floor through the day **and goes out in the evening** *(`milieu-brief.md` §4)*. It is evening. So the chamber is lit by cultivated mycelial lattice, yellow-green, calibrated — *light as information, not beauty*, `foreshadow-and-motif.md` §3 — and every face in it is lit by something the colony grew on purpose.
- **`SOUND`** — a crowded chamber under stone. No insect wall, no rain, no river: **the first enclosed human noise in seventeen days**, and after Genesis and the flight it should be the loudest thing in the book without a single loud sound in it.
- **`CLOCK`** — a Council session that the Council controls. **What ends the scene is Sila finishing**, not the trio. They have no power to leave and no power to stop it, which is the inverse of every scene since Day 2, where the clock was weather or tide or light or charge. **Here the clock is other people.**
- **`LOAD`** — they carry almost nothing and that is the state they arrive in. The protocol tablets are gone, handed over four minutes after landing and already being worked *(D19.6)*. **One tablet of the founders' own dating fix is in the room**, carried in deliberately because it is the thing Benal has to put in front of them; the rest of what they chose at D19.1 is still with the wig. **Benal has the capsule, unopened, under his arm** *(D19.2)*, and it stays unopened <!-- @WF-capsule.opened.book1: false --> — `sequels.md` requires it. The wig is in the river between the two barriers, substantially intact and visible from the bank.
- **`PRESENT`** — the three of them. The Council, with **Sila** at the center of the dais and **Hesh** on it. **Joram and Riel under guard**, implicated as suit custodians since the night of the theft and confined ever since *(`minor-characters.md`)*. **Marek and Lunet.** A crowd — this is public, as the Day 2 debate was public. `[?]` Whether **Elana** and **Rano** are in the room is **A1**. **Omya is not here**; she is at the Vitarium with the protocols and cannot follow them, and her one lucid moment is outdoors at D19.9.
- **`BODY`** — nineteen days <!-- @WF-story.span: 19d -->, and nothing reset. **Keo:** burned, thin, the Day 11 thigh laceration closed badly and fast at the cost of everything else, cracked rib, ankle. **Teva:** six days past the poisoning, still hollowed out, and `body-and-resources.md` has her only keeping food down from Day 17. **Benal:** the lightest of the three and the one with the least left; his suit is a dead gray skin he is still wearing because taking it off is worse.
- **`WANTS`** — quoted from `character-arcs.md` §2. **Keo, Day 19 evening:** wants *his parents to look at him*; believes *they were afraid for him*; fears *that they were right*; hides *nothing*. **The believes column is the post-realization state and the scene is where he crosses into it** — he does not arrive holding it. **Teva, Day 19:** wants *to accuse*; fears *that she will see herself in them*. **Benal, Day 19:** wants *to be heard*; fears **being agreed with by the wrong person** — which is the thing that happens to him in this room, in his own arc's own words.
- **`CAPABILITIES`** — **the proximity sense fails here, and the failure is the scene's best gift.** `tech-rules.md` gives its limits as crowded minds and exhaustion, and this is the most crowded room in the novel. **Keo is sensorily blind at the exact moment he finally has to read his parents' faces with his eyes**, and nobody points at it. Mental projection is not used; there is no animal. Nothing else is in play — this is the one scene in the book where no upgrade does any work.
- **`RELATIONSHIPS`** — `character-arcs.md` §3, Day 19: Keo→Teva 7 unresolved, Teva→Benal 7 unresolved, Keo↔Benal 9 settled.
- **`READER`** — computed. The reader holds everything: **FB** since EP5 at the end of Act 1, **FM** and **FM2** since Act 1, **FN** accumulating since Act 1 and completed at D2.10, **FO** since D2.6, **FR** and **FS** since D2.6, **FE FF FG** delivered to the trio at D17.2 and to the reader in the epigraph channel. **FP is the one thing the reader has wrong**, deliberately — they have concluded the founders lied, and EP13 corrects it after this scene is over. **The reader is ahead of the room on every count and behind it on none**, for the only time in the book, and the whole design of `knowledge-ledger.md` has been building to that.

## SPINE

**The session itself** — a sequence of people being given the floor and taking it or failing to. It does not change hands; it is the room's, and that is the point. The trio's only agency is what they do with a turn when they get one.

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** The Enclave holds FE and FF. Hesh has accepted the revelation completely and is the first to do so. **Keo has FN** — he understands what was in his father's face — and has not said a word about it. Benal has been heard twice and absorbed once. Teva has accused and has not yet given the speech she wrote.

**Worked on and did not change.** FB, which the room now holds as a devotional claim rather than a medical one and is therefore worse off than before he said it. Keo's parents, still under guard. The schism, which is wider than it was this morning.

**Left open.** What the colony does with twenty years <!-- @WF-impact.deadline: 20y -->. Whether Keo says anything to his parents. Whether Teva takes her second turn.

**Refused outright.** Any character articulating the thesis in this scene — see **A2**. Any villain. Any interruption of Benal, because the interrupted revelation is a recognizable cheat and this failure is epistemic.

**READER.** **FN completes at move 15** — accumulated since Act 1 through D2.3, D2.6 and D2.10, and this is the named completion scene. **R5, R6 and R15 all pay.** FE and FF reach the Enclave, which the reader already had. **No `[turn — READER ONLY]`**: every turn here happens to somebody in the room.
