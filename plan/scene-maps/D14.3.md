## SPINE

**The strike and its aftermath**, from the smell to his going under.

## MOVES

1. **[opens] [world]** The raft rides a trough. Something breathes out beside it, close, and the smell comes over them: brine and dead fish. Then it is gone under.
2. **[world]** One strike from below. The raft comes apart, and he is in the water.
3. **[spine]** The fieldpack's strap is caught on a broken strut of the deck, and the strut is going down and taking him with it. He holds on to the pack, and goes under with it, and lets go. The fieldpack goes down, and his board in it, and everything on it `[-]`.
4. **[relation]** As it goes he hears his father: *do something real*. He reads it as his failure. `[requires D2.2 — Marek's "do something real"]`
5. **[world] [image]** He comes up and drives his display, and for a second there is an eye beside him in the water, as large as his head, ancient and cold; and past it, Keo, with his arms round a piece of the deck. Then dark. The animal is gone before he has understood it. *(`milieu-allocation.md` §5, Day 14: the eye is used once.)*
6. He shouts their names, and nothing comes back through the storm.
7. **[body]** The suit holds him up, and that is all it does. He cannot tell which way anything is.
8. **[closes]** A wave puts him under, and another before he is up, and he swallows the sea, and with nothing in his hands he goes out. The suit keeps him up. *Ending **BLACKOUT***.

## OUT — deltas against IN

**Changed.** The raft is in pieces. The fieldpack is gone, and with it his written work, the croc tooth, the kindler and the last of the food; the spike and the razor-club are gone with the raft. He has the equation only in his head. They are separated. He has seen Keo on a piece of the deck, for a second; Teva he has not seen *(scene-list D14.3; D15.1 finds Keo wrapped around it)*.

**Left open.** Whether the others are alive. Where the sea will put them.

**Refused outright.** A fight: one strike, in the dark, and the animal is gone before it is understood. Benal brave. Anyone enumerating *water is death*.
