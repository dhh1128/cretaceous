## MOVES

1. **[opens] [body]** **The silence.** — *It rings, in his ears and in the place in his head where she was. **He reaches for the bond out of habit**, the way he has every morning for years, **and there is nothing at the end of it.** He is sitting where he was when it happened.* **~14%.**

2. **[spine] [image]** **The feather.** — *In the trodden fern where she slept, **one feather**, spattered, **purple-black in the low sun and mud-brown when he turns it** `[-]` *(`milieu-brief.md` §9: both canon; the morning is clear, so the angle changes it, not a cloud)*. **The spatter is from the beak's grip.** **He picks it up.** He does not know what to do with it, and holds it.* **~14%.**

3. **[spine] [relation]** **Teva and the water.** — *She has kicked the ashes apart and gathered the spears and her carried suit while he sat *(the ashes: `milieu-brief.md` §6)*. **She shares out the last of the water, a mouthful each, and gives him hers as well** `[-]`, and does not look at him while she does it. **She talks**, interrogating and deciding: what is left, how far, **the dark line low in the west** `[-]` that may be rain by mid-morning, and that they go north into the broken ground where there will be shade and rock *(the talk is about the next mile)*. He answers in single words or not at all.* **~18%.**

4. **[spine] [relation]** **Benal and the fence.** — *Benal pulls the fence apart near him, not beside, **frond by frond, methodically**, laying the stakes in a row because they are still good stakes and he will not waste them *(they go with him)* `[-]`. **He talks**, in his register, the next mile in figures and hedges, how far the broken country is and how long before the heat; **he asks Keo one question whose answer he already knows**, to give him something to say. **He does not talk about her.*** **~16%.**

5. **[spine] [turn — KEO, interior]** **No rite.** — *Keo looks at the sky where she went. **Nothing came back.** There is no body; **it is the same as Yara**, who was taken in the grove and never came back to be returned, and the family never said so *(`plan/minor-characters.md`; K20)*. **His own comparison, and he knows the rite was never Noli's**: it is the empty hands that are the same, and that his push did nothing either time. **The thought lives in his head only, and goes no further; nobody says it, and nobody says her name.*** **~18%.**

6. **[closes] [body] [relation]** **The pack, and walking.** — ***Ending SETTLED*** *(`scene-list.md`)*. *He stands because they are standing. **Teva has the fieldpack up, held open for his arms**, the way you hold a tunic for a child, and **he puts his arms through it** because he cannot think of a reason not to. The feather goes inside his tunic `[-]`. **The three of them walk**, toward the broken country to the north *(D11.3)*, with the sun climbing.* **~20%.**
