## MOVES

1. **[opens] [body]** **The silence.** — *It rings, in his ears and in the place in his head where she was. **He reaches for the bond out of habit**, the way he has every morning for years, **and there is nothing at the end of it.** He is sitting where he was when it happened.* **~14%.**

2. **[spine] [image]** **The feather.** — *In the trodden fern where she slept, **one feather**, spattered, **purple-black in the low sun and mud-brown when a cloud's shadow crosses it** `[-]` *(`milieu-brief.md` §9: both canon)*. **He picks it up.** He does not know what to do with it, and holds it.* **~14%.**

3. **[spine] [relation]** **Teva moves his pack.** — *She has broken the camp around him while he sat. **She picks up his pack, the fieldpack with the wood still bound to it, and moves it beside him** so it is there when he stands. **She does not look at him while she does it.** She says something about the water: what is left, and that the front may come *(the talk is about the next mile)*.* **~16%.**

4. **[spine] [relation]** **Benal and the fern.** — *Benal sits down near him, not beside, and **picks a fern frond apart, leaflet by leaflet, methodically**, and lays the pieces in a row. **He talks**, in his register, about the heat and how far, the next mile in figures. **He does not talk about her.*** **~16%.**

5. **[spine] [turn — KEO, interior]** **No rite.** — *Keo looks at the sky where she went. **There is nothing to return.** No body, no balm, no mats, no night by a fire at a distance; **it is the same as Yara**, who was taken in the grove and never came back to be returned, and the family never said so *(`plan/minor-characters.md`; K20)*. **The thought lives in his head only, and goes no further; nobody says it, and nobody says her name.*** **~18%.**

6. **[closes] [body]** **The pack, and walking.** — ***Ending SETTLED*** *(`scene-list.md`)*. *He stands because they are standing. **He puts the pack on.** The feather goes inside his tunic `[-]`. **The three of them walk**, toward the broken country to the north *(D11.3)*, with the sun climbing.* **~22%.**
