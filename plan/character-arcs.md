# Character arcs and intimacy

What each of the three wants, believes, fears and hides across the nineteen days <!-- @WF-story.span: 19d -->; where each arc turns; and the temperature of the three dyads.

Kept in one file because they are one system: the intimacy is not a subplot running beside the arcs, it is the arcs colliding.

**Everything here is indexed by day**, because the day is the one address in this book that does not move. Scene numbers are being reassigned; `journey-calendar.md` is the authority and its nineteen days <!-- @WF-story.span: 19d --> are stable.

---

## 1. The de-escalation requirement

**Models escalate. This book must not, because Daniel's own novel runs the other way.** In *cordimancy*, the god grants Toril no new power; he then loses the magic he had and spends the last stretch of the book powerless, and that is what makes the ending mean anything.

The shape being aimed at is that each of the three **loses a capability they were defined by**, and does not get it back. A target, not a quota — if one of them keeps something and the book is better for it, the book wins.

| | loses | when | what replaces it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Keo** | mental projection's *meaning*. The gift is intact and it could not save the one thing it was for. Then he is injured, feverish and slowing them, with nothing on him and his load on Teva's back — the protector becomes the burden <!-- @WF-keo.carried: false --> | **Day 11**, both | judgment, and eventually a machine |
| **Teva** | her suit to the heat; then **her own judgment — she short-leaches the mash and it poisons her** | **Day 10**, then the decision on **Day 12** and the collapse on **Day 13** | the willingness to be wrong |
| **Benal** | his suit's defenses, concealed; then his written work to the sea | **Day 11**, then **Day 14** | what is already in his head, which turns out to be everything |

**Her magnetic sense is not a loss and never becomes one.** Offshore it is worth more than it was on land, because there is nothing else to steer by. What limits her is a splitting headache from the short-leached mash: she gives them a heading and cannot hold it. **Her own Day 12 decision is what takes the navigation away from them**, which makes the poisoning teach her twice. `[?]` Whether she needs a third loss at all is open.

**The poisoning is how she earns the third column, and it has to be a decision or it earns nothing.** Noli dies on Day 11 and the grounders stop with her, so by Day 12 there is no protein and no time. Leaching takes one to three days in running water; they have hours. **Teva decides to leach it short.** Keo — the Explorer, whose training includes what is safe to eat — says it needs another day and does not press it, because he is injured and the burden and has stopped trusting himself. She is the one who decides, as she has decided everything since the Vitarium, and the rage that makes her decisive is what makes her wrong. **Her venom resistance is excellent and does not cover it** (`milieu-brief.md` §9) — she is the one who should have been safe.

**And a fourth loss that is Teva's alone, running underneath the book.** She was being trained for the Watch — Omya taught her the stars because Keepers hold a memorized sky and check it nightly (`milieu-brief.md` §5). Her sensory upgrades failed, so she has a Keeper's memory and not a Keeper's senses: **she was supposed to inherit the sky and got part of the way, and the woman who could finish teaching her is disappearing while she walks away from her.** It gives her grief a specific object instead of a general one, and a role in book 2 beyond navigator — she holds the only fragment of the Watch anyone has left, and it is incomplete.

**Benal's is the cruelest and the best:** he is stripped of the two things that made him survivable and left holding only the thing everyone called useless.

## 3. The three dyads

### How the triangle actually works

**Keo → Teva** is unrequited and unspoken, and he misreads her often. She recruits him and he hears a choice.

**Teva → Benal** is the one to get right, and the answer is not a rival suitor. She is drawn to his mind and **does not know what that feeling is.** Keo reads it correctly as attraction and incorrectly as settled, and watching her discover someone she has walked past for sixteen years is what unsettles him. **It ends in trust**: Benal's arc is about being heard, and she is the first person who ever asked, so she ends as the one he trusts to understand him. The attraction stays open; the friendship under it settles.

**Benal → Teva** is admiration for the person who listened. He is oblivious to the rest throughout, because he cannot conceive of being wanted.

So all three misread each other, which is the novel's whole subject arriving in the romance instead of being stated. `[?]` **Nothing resolves in book 1.** The engine is proximity, talk that circles the one thing each of them cannot say, and a POV character noticing a physical detail or a turn of phrase and drawing the wrong conclusion from it. Do not answer the question the scene raises; `voice-sheets.md` carries the standing list of what each scene refuses to answer.

**Keo ↔ Benal is the strongest dyad.** They have known each other since infancy, in a cohort of perhaps thirty, and are not close. **Keo pities him kindly, as everyone does**, and is protective of him the way he is of everyone, because he thinks he is the best of them at what lies past the tangle; Benal is the one it applies to most. Then debt, then the silent watch on Day 11, where the protection reverses, then Genesis proving Benal right, and it **ends in trust and admiration running both ways**: Keo admires the mind and the nerve, and Benal admires the competence he lacks and no longer resents it, because it has stopped being pointed at him as pity. Its texture is built through the body — carrying, watching, being carried — where Teva's is built through asking. It ends at 9 while both romantic lines end unresolved, which is the honest shape for a book about three sixteen-year-olds.

**Nobody in the cohort is a stranger to anyone.** A low number means *not close*, never *barely knows*. Everyone his age calls him **Ben**.

### Rules

1. **Declarations are the wrong instrument here.** The closest anyone comes is on the good day, and he doesn't. A strong default rather than a ban; a scene that earns one has earned it.
2. **Proximity and talk are the two instruments, and they work together.** Proximity makes the time: the lay-up, shared watches, treating wounds, sharing food, sleeping within arm's reach for sixteen nights. Talk is what they do with it: teasing, asking, arguing, stories from home, getting each other wrong out loud. Keo and Benal lean on the body and Teva's lines lean on asking, but none of the three relationships is built on silence. `body-and-resources.md` §2b provides four hours a day of enforced stillness; use it for talk.
3. **Jealousy is usually a misreading**, and where it is, the reader should see the misreading before Keo does.
4. **Touch is rationed, and functional when it comes** — a hand on a shoulder, a wound being cleaned, being hauled up a bank. Roughly one an act is the rate that feels right; it is a sense of scarcity, not a budget with a balance to spend down. The functional touch that lasts a moment too long does more than any embrace.
5. **What each of them withholds is one specific thing** (`voice-sheets.md`), **never talk itself.** Silence is rationed like touch: rare, and used where it is the point.

### Relationships by day

The dyad table gives temperatures; this gives the scenes that carry them. Each scene's map cites its line here in `RELATIONS`, and spends the backstory by id from `plan/backstory.md`. Each placement costs a sentence or an exchange, not a scene.

**Days 1–2: before the Council, the three are already in each other's lives.** Each opening scene touches the other two at least once and shows one peer.

- **D1.2.** The returning is the one place all three are before the Council. Teva sees Keo standing with his parents, and Benal a head above the crowd at the back. Riel touches her arm as she passes (T20). Unseen by her: Keo's family never had a rite for Yara.
- **D2.1.** Topo turns up at dawn, and Keo's big-brother kindness is on the page in his own scene (K18).
- **D2.2.** Benal's botanist friend appears briefly (B24).
- **D2.3.** Riel asks after Teva and Omya, and the family teases Keo about the flowers he has brought Omya (K19, T20).
- **D2.4.** Teva tucks a flower into Omya's plait (T6), and Omya, lucid and dry, remembers the Explorers' boy who brought her flowers.
- **D2.5.** Benal's math children pass the pool on the teaching rota (X10). The yazhi know him, and a yazhi's joke about him is the funny line Teva does not laugh at.

**Days 3–5: what happens between them gets answered.** A quarrel, a save, a joke or a wound is met by a reply rather than closed by silence or summary. Each of the three has one moment tied to someone absent.

- **D3.1.** Keo says aloud to Benal something about what the suit's quiet means to him: the kind face of his pity. When Teva says "Possibly", Keo takes it up, so the joke belongs to all three.
- **D3.2.** Keo answers "reckless, unsuited idiot" in his own register, protesting and then conceding. Someone acknowledges that Teva saved him and Noli. The debt starts here.
- **D3.3.** Later that morning Keo makes "idiot" a joke against himself, and the quarrel is mended. Teva's fear of water stays hers alone, and she feeds her suit before she drinks, without comment from anyone (`body-and-resources.md` §3b). Navigating, Teva thinks of her mother (T43).
- **D3.4.** The day told back is dramatized in their voices: teasing, correcting, the croc-length game. The talk goes to shared childhood rather than family, the fog walk (X1) and math class (X4). Teva has a line. Keo's frogs are hunger's verdict (K14).
- **D3.5.** Benal wakes frightened on Teva's watch, and her guard comes down a little because he was vulnerable first. The sky stays her secret.
- **D4.1.** Keo is overconfident about the mushroom and Benal makes him do the smell test (K17, B35), and the running argument about what is edible begins (`humor-plan.md`, inside jokes). Benal's snail talk is in his voice. Teva says something about the wet ground that Keo takes the wrong way, so his misreading happens between them. The snails after rain are Keo's tie to Yara (K8).
- **D4.2.** At the moths, the day's one shared pleasure, Teva asks Benal what he hears and he tells them. Keo shows off about his bites.
- **D5.1.** At the lay-up, Teva teases Keo about Noli for the first time (T28). Keo answers Benal's false alarm with a tease rather than a turned back.
- **D5.2.** The after-talk is in voice: the count, the shoulder, the hands, and a joke that comes out wrong. Benal registers that Teva read the snake the way he reads things, the first step of his admiration. Marek's *you'll be ill* surfaces once (`minor-characters.md`).

**Day 6: the good day, and Teva inside the joke.** The three dyads peak together here and only here, so Teva has to be inside the comedy on the day it becomes theirs.

- **D6.1.** Teva asks Benal how he tells honey from resin at that distance: her first real question to him, the asking where her warmth is. At the young croc, Benal sees Keo's face and knows why, because small crocs inside are culled for Yara (K47); nobody says her name. Benal has one thought of home, the boy who had never been outside. The closing laugh is dramatized rather than reported.
- **D6.2.** Benal clowns and Teva lands the last line (`humor-plan.md`, mechanism 3), within her laughter budget. Teaching her the suit override, Keo's hands stay on her forearm a moment too long, and she notices and does not understand it. Keo answers "This is the best day I've ever had" with fondness, and someone asks what Benal's ordinary days were like (B2).
- **D6.3.** The beetle argument is dramatized, with Teva's dry cut-off. On the ridge they talk about standing where nobody they know has stood. Keo's almost-declaration becomes the flower (T6): he offers it instead of saying it, she takes it flatly and says something he misreads, and he never learns she kept it.

**Day 7: the river, and the absent kept present by observation.** Yara is never named; the others see what the crocs are to Keo, and Teva sees it wrong.

- **D7.1.** Benal sees Keo looking at sixteen crocs and knows what he is seeing. Teva's "I know" acknowledges what Benal offered instead of closing him down. Someone registers that they all trust his ears now, reversing Day 5. Benal tries to answer Keo's failed joke and cannot, so the register dies in both of them.
- **D7.2.** Teva reads Keo on the rock as bravado, and through her POV the reader can see him standing over croc water, which took his sister. When Benal says "We could go home", Keo answers protectively and Benal hears pity. After she takes the decision from Keo, Teva has a moment that shows what it costs her. When Keo admits he cannot do something, she asks him about it.
- **D7.3.** The night-before talk is dramatized: logistics on the surface and the three holding on to each other under it, drawing on Day 6's honey and the *delicious* joke. Keo thinks of Yara. Keo answers Benal's "Statistically".

**Day 8: the crossing, and what each of them pays for the others.** Talk fills the waiting and the night; silence is spent only in the water, where the surface is the danger.

- **D8.1.** The waiting is filled with talk. Noli crosses an hour early on her own, and Teva teases Keo that his animal is braver than he is; three days later the joke has no object. Keo sings under his breath, Liaso's song from D3.1 (K10), and Benal knows it. Driving the herd, Keo does what he does at home with the hadrosaurs (K1), and home is in his head while he does it. Keo watches Benal stack stones in the waiting and knock them over when they go down (B13), and does not know why.
- **D8.2.** Talk is spent only where the surface allows. Nobody swims; they are tied on Keo's cord, and Teva, first because her balance holds on wet rock, reels Benal onto each stone and hauls on the cord while he climbs the bank before she lets herself stop. That is her care. Benal's part is keeping still, which is the hardest thing he has done.
- **D8.3.** Benal sees that what Keo paid is more than a headache: the first time Benal looks after Keo, which the Day 11 reversal builds on.
- **D8.4.** The whispered argument at the bare fence, as mapped. Benal's joke fails, as planned, and Teva lands the last line, so the night ends with the three of them together rather than each alone with the fear.

**Day 9: the ordinary day after the enormous one, when they start telling each other their lives.**

- **D9.1.** On the long walk they trade home lives: Benal's shells (B12) and the mathematician everyone thinks is scatterbrained (B6); Keo's hadrosaurs and Topo (K1, K18). At a rest stop Benal balances stones without thinking (B13), the habit Keo watched at D8.1, now from inside, and this time he leaves the stack standing. Benal keeps watching Keo's head, carrying on the care that began at D8.3.
- **D9.2.** Teva shares out the last handful kept back from Day 6, the pinyons and honey (`body-and-resources.md` §3, Day 9), and offers the others a taste of the honey the way she offers Omya sips (T5), so what she brought from home is in what she does. Nothing is cooked. Keo takes the taste as meant for him: the misreading is how the scene moves the social ladder without an argument. Teva names the river behind them, flatly, as a fact, and nobody draws the conclusion aloud.

**Day 10: his best day and her worst, the same hours.** Water is the crisis, not food; Noli's grounder at D10.3 is the only fresh food.

- **D10.1.** Keo sings on the march (K10): his best day, breath to spare, the reader enjoying him as he enjoys himself. It is the last time he sings. The *delicious* joke is his here, as planned.
- **D10.2.** Teva, concealing sunfever, hears him singing ahead of her, and the irony is never stated. She keeps going by reciting the fields under her breath, and it breaks at the same place. Benal estimates their water aloud (B30), and one of his figures nearly gives her away; she heads it off. She is hiding from the one who notices.
- **D10.3.** Noli drags in her grounder, and Teva teases Keo about her for the last time (T28); nobody knows it is the last. Benal works out aloud what the recited sky is, the founders' own table and a way to date them, and Teva listens, while Keo, not following, performs for her.

**Day 11: the one day silence is the point.** Keo is numb, and the care is in what the others do; the scene list already carries the talk that is there. What this adds is the absent.

- **D11.2.** Noli is taken, with no body to return and no rite, which is exactly Yara. The parallel lives only in Keo's head (K20); nobody says it, and her name is not spoken.
- **D11.4.** The fall is driven by that grief. Leaving the third suit was Keo proving he could survive what Yara could not, and this is what it buys.
- **D11.5.** Teva's hands know wound care because she sat with her mother (T16), who died of infection after a bite, so Keo's wound frightens her in a specific way. That fear is under the hostile joke.
- **D11.6.** Benal, sitting watch, hums Liaso's song, the one Keo sang on Days 8 and 10, and Keo hears it through the fever. The song passes from Keo to Benal as the protection reverses, and neither says so.

**Days 12–13: the threads they built pay, and one pays cruelly.**

- **D12.1.** The edibility argument from D4.1 inverts: Keo is the cautious one now, and Benal, who always supplied the toxicology, hedges, the one rehearsal of *possibly* doing harm (`voice-sheets.md`, Benal). The joke built on Days 4–6 is what fails them. Teva serves the mash the way she serves Omya (T5), and eats first, to protect them.
- **D12.2.** Keo, febrile, tries to send Benal to sleep in his older-brother register, and it no longer works, because that register died at Day 11. Benal refuses with a death joke: Keo's humor falls as Benal's rises (`humor-plan.md`). Keo does try to stop him taking watches he cannot afford, and fails.
- **D13.1.** Benal's estimates (B30) are worth something at last. Watching Teva vomit, he remembers her sharing her lunch with him when they were small (B23), and that is where his tenderness comes from.
- **D13.2.** The distant *T. rex* pays Liaso's story from D2.9. Benal says his cousin's name, the only thing any of them has the energy to say, and it brings home and that night back to all three.

**Days 14–15: what was planted on Days 1–9 pays.**

- **D14.1.** They follow Benal's corridor through the turtle beach without asking, completing the turn from Day 5. Teva asks him how he does it.
- **D14.2.** Nobody manages anything; they hold on, and Keo holds Benal on the raft, in the body, where Keo and Benal have always worked.
- **D14.3.** As the fieldpack and his mathematics go, Benal hears Marek's *do something real*, his one tie home at the moment of loss.
- **D15.2.** At Teva's lowest, *we'll be fine*, the trio's line since Day 2, is said by Teva, flat, and it breaks as she says it.
- **D15.3.** Eighteen hours <!-- @WF-cyclone.tail: 18h --> of wet dark are got through on home stories: Topo, Teva's two friends, Omya. It warms them toward the night.
- **D15.4.** Teva tells Benal that, years ago, Keo made the boy who startled him stop (K26, T27): Benal learns he has been protected longer than either knew, and that is what takes Keo↔Benal to 9. Keo, edging toward the crush without saying it, asks Teva whether she remembers chasing him to kiss him when they were small, and she does not (K22, T30); he laughs it off, and the reader knows what it cost. It is the scene list's *someone says the wrong thing first*, and the romance stays unresolved (Rule 1).

**Days 16–18: Genesis, and the register pays.**

- **D16.2.** While the other two work the archive, Keo is useless, and his dyslexia (K15) is the specific reason: a room of text is his particular failure, which makes his wandering on Day 18 inevitable rather than sulky. Benal sees it, having suspected since math class (B22), and says nothing.
- **D16.3.** The protocols pass through Teva's hands. She cannot read all of it, but she recognizes the first twenty positions Omya drilled into her (T8), etched in metal: her grandmother's words outlasting her grandmother's mind.
- **D17.1.** *A name she knows, spelled the long way* is Cecilia Arroyo, ancestor of Sila, the woman Teva idolized and is disillusioned with, and mother of one of her two friends (T18, T19). The reader knows Cecilia from her warm, doubting letters, so the face Teva finds is the real one behind the voice Sila claims.
- **D17.2.** Keo asks the frustrated question, the rare question he cannot answer himself, and in the answer Benal hears something Marek once said (F22).
- **D17.3.** Teva gets as far as Omya guarding a protocol for a journey home she could never have made, and stops. Keo sides with her that it was a lie, and Benal is alone with the impossible mission: two against one, the reverse of D3.2.
- **D18.2.** The training system talks rather than needing to be read, which is why it is Keo's: the boy who could not use the archive finds the way home.

**Day 19: three threads close, and the D19.7–D19.8 maps carry the revisions below.** Act 3 has almost no humor until one small callback (`humor-plan.md`), and this is it.

- **D19.3.** At the launch Benal hums Liaso's song and Keo joins in. Keo knew the song at D3.1 and sang it on Days 8 and 10; it passed to Benal at his bedside on Day 11, and comes back to Keo here, the two of them singing it in the first frightening minute of flight.
- **D19.5.** As Keo aims for the river she has taught four-year-olds is death, Teva's hand goes to where she has kept the flower he gave her on Day 6 (T6). The reader sees it; Keo does not, and never learns she kept it (Rule 1).
- **D19.6.** Omya is present and cannot follow it. Teva sees that her plait has been done by another hand while she was gone: her grandmother was cared for, and by someone else.
- **D16.2, planted for Day 19.** In the dry room they say what they will do first at home, lightly: Benal that Marek will look at the board, Keo that he will tell his father something, Teva that she will do Omya's hair. Day 19 answers each.
- **D19.7.** Sila complains that two suits went out against every rule; Riel, under guard, answers *Three children went out. Three came home. That is what the suits are for.*, purpose over rule, the children and not the suits being what frightened them, and *children* dropped from the second half on purpose: adulthood planted by omission, reversing her own D2.3 refusal, and Keo half-hears it. When Sila absorbs Benal, Marek rises, says he called the work useless and was wrong, and crosses to stand beside him; Keo, already moving, takes Benal's other side. The room treats them as children who stole and does not notice what Keo's parents paid: the anger is the reader's.
- **D19.8.** Joram says two words, Keo steps into the hug, and Riel says low, *Two words. Now you know why I married him. So efficient.* Only Teva hears it, and she cries, joy for her friend and loss for herself. Benal turns and meets Marek's look, hands back his father's words, and Marek, for once, listens; then Benal looks across the room for Teva.
- **D19.9.** Teva re-plaits Omya's hair, and Omya's hand goes to hers. The three end side by side, and the D16.2 hopes come back as the final callback.


## 4. Reader regard

What the reader is meant to feel toward each of the three, and the cardboard version of each that the prose has to keep them out of. Maps answer `REGARD` from this section (`process/methodology-theory.md` §4a).

**The failure mode all three share is the strong, silent, moody, depressed loner.** It comes from how they communicate rather than from who they are: when talk is only logistics or concealment, three different people collapse into one sullen type. The guard is that each of them talks constantly in their own register about everything except the specific thing they hide (`voice-sheets.md`), and that the reader sees them want things from each other, tease, ask, and get things wrong out loud.

|---|---|
| **Keo** | the competent hothead |
| **Teva** | the angry one |
| **Benal** | the head-in-the-clouds loner. His dark humor is real and is not the danger |

### Regard by stretch

What the reader should feel toward each of the three, the details that earn it, and which caricature they hold off. The relationship score above places the details scene by scene; this says which of them do the earning.

**Days 1–2, before the walk.**
- **Teva**: protective sympathy and respect, for a grieving, capable girl alone with Omya. Earned by finishing Omya's plait, the flower tucked into it (T6), and her memory of Alira on Elana's hip (T23). Holds off *the angry one*: the reader sees her tenderness before her rage, so the rage reads as grief.
- **Keo**: liking, with worry. Earned by his big-brother kindness to Topo (K18), his family teasing him about the flowers he brought Omya (K19), and his care not to frighten Noli (K31). Holds off *the competent hothead*: his mother refuses him a suit in the same words as always (K32), and his hurt is a child's.
- **Benal**: affection and amusement. Earned by *Your grandmother, possibly* to the flybeak (B14), his botanist friend (B24), and the yazhi knowing him (D2.5). Holds off *the loner*: he has a friend and pupils, and he is funny without knowing it.

**Days 3–5, the first walking days.**
- **Keo**: admiration with exasperation. Earned by answering *reckless, unsuited idiot* and then making it a joke against himself (D3.3), and by yielding on the mushroom's smell test (K17). Holds off the hothead: he concedes aloud.
- **Teva**: respect turning to warmth. Earned by feeding her suit before she drinks (D3.3), asking Benal what he hears at the moths (D4.2), and her first tease about Noli (D5.1). Holds off the angry one: she asks, and she teases.
- **Benal**: tenderness and admiration. Earned by catching frogs with a kind of horrified care (B33), his curiosity about snails (B15), and joking about his own returning (B31). Holds off the loner: he talks to them, and *possibly* becomes theirs.

**Day 6, the good day.**
- **Keo**: delight and fondness. Earned by the flower offered instead of a declaration (T6), his hands on her forearm in the override lesson, and his fondness at Benal's *best day*. Holds off the hothead: he holds back.
- **Teva**: plain liking, for the first time. Earned by her honey question to Benal and by landing the last line. Holds off the angry one: she laughs, within budget.
- **Benal**: love. Earned by *This is the best day I've ever had* and the beetle argument. Holds off the loner: he is the center of the joke.

**Days 7–10, the river to the savanna.**
- **Keo**: fear for him, then enjoyment, then dread. Earned by facing sixteen crocs while the others see Yara in it, admitting he cannot, and singing on the savanna (K10). Day 10 is his hothead peak, and the guard is the scene list's own: the reader enjoys him as much as he enjoys himself, and he looks after Benal.
- **Teva**: admiration and anxiety. Earned by taking the decision, the cost seen from inside at D7.2, reeling Benal in on the cord, and concealing sunfever by reciting the fields. D7.2 is her angry-one peak, and the cost seen from inside is the guard.
- **Benal**: respect. Earned by seeing what Keo paid (D8.3), the stone stacks (B13), and estimating the water. Holds off the loner: he looks after Keo.

**Day 11, the midpoint.**
- **Keo**: grief with him. Earned by the Yara parallel in his head and his numbness. The hothead is gone; the fall is grief, not bravado.
- **Teva**: tenderness under hostility. Earned by *Be quiet or be bait* and the reader knowing it is her mother's infection she fears; the anger reads as care.
- **Benal**: admiration becoming love. Earned by sitting watch and humming Keo's song.

**Days 12–15, the poisoning to the confessions.**
- **Keo**: pity and admiration. Earned by being right and not pressing, carrying her, and asking whether she remembers the kiss chase.
- **Teva**: the reader fears for her and forgives her before she forgives herself. Earned by testing the mash on herself, and *we'll be fine* breaking in her mouth.
- **Benal**: tenderness. Earned by remembering her lunch, Marek's voice as the mathematics goes, and learning that Keo protected him.

**Days 16–19, Genesis and the return.**
- **Keo**: vindication. Earned by the archive he cannot use (K15) and the machine that talks, which makes him the one who flies them home, singing again.
- **Teva**: the reader wants her to be wrong about the lie and loves her for stopping at Omya. Earned by the flower at the landing and the plait done by another hand.
- **Benal**: triumph, and wanting him heard. Earned by being right in a room that will not take it, his canon fear: being agreed with by the wrong person.
- **Day 19 is the emotional climax, and it is not vindication.** The earlier events of Day 19 are the plot climax. At the confrontation and after it, the reader is angry with them when they are not received well, weeps with them for their disappointments, and is happy with them at their solidarity and friendship at the end *(`plan/scene-maps/D19.7.md`, `plan/scene-maps/D19.8.md`)*.
