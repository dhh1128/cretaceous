# The Attic — material cut from canon, preserved

Nothing here is canon. Nothing here may be used in prose without being promoted back into a canon document first. This file exists so that the corpus can be aggressively simplified without losing anything that a later book might want.

Each entry says what it was, why it was cut, and whether it might come back.

---

## From the old `lingo.md` (108 invented drift terms, cut to 13)

The old glossary was generated wholesale and never curated. It contradicted the milieu brief roughly forty times and described a different society. See `.ignored/materials-audit.md` §2. Cut in four groups.

### Group 1 — Competing names for things already named. Do not revive.

These offered a second word for a thing the milieu brief had already named, which is the single most corrosive kind of inconsistency, because the drafting model picks whichever it saw last.

*snapjaw*, *gater*, *snapper* (all for **croc**) · *longneck* (for **saropo**) · *skywing*, *skytooth* (for **flybeak**) · *dustwalk* (for **mudwalk**) · *rootmilk* (for **leche**) · *graincake* (for **pan**) · *saurin*, *hornback*, *thornback* (generic dinosaur names the book doesn't need) · *biter*, *runner* (generic predator/prey)

### Group 2 — Wrong society. Do not revive in this trilogy.

These presuppose a scavenger culture with working electronics, an industrial base and casual hard-tech use — the opposite of an enclave under a paradox taboo whose engineered archaea dissolve any old-tech left unmaintained.

*solarim* · *purif* · *sparkbat* · *flamekit* · *foilwrap* · *healfoam* · *dataslate* · *slater* · *linkhub* · *patchline* · *sparkline* · *fermi-pot* · *sparkfarm* · *breath-tank* · *screener* · *needler* · *chargeron* · *cellbank* · *sparkcell* · *brightmetal* · *steelvine* · *torchcap* · *sunpot* · *heat-glass* · *fireglass* · *stormbell* · *dripcatch* · *growhouse* · *domehouse* · *campa* · *chatter*

Two of these did direct damage: ***masker*** ("breathing or filtration mask") and ***darkglass*** ("…or visor") were part of the evidence that produced helmets and faceplates in four drafts. See repair register R1.

### Group 3 — Contradicts established biology or society. Do not revive without a canon change.

*clawhound* ("domesticated predator species") · *warmback* ("kept as pack animal") · *scalehen* ("feathered theropod kept for eggs") — the domestication set is hadrosaurs, plus possibly ground-dwelling birds for ovos. Adding domesticated predators and pack animals guts the difficulty of their lives, which is rule 5.

*crocoline*, *scuttlefish*, *wingcat*, *muddrake*, *razorfish*, *shellbird*, *flatfish* — invented fauna competing with the researched fauna in `geo-flora-fauna.md`.

*dustlung* ("from volcanic ash"), *critterpox*, *bluelips*, *bloodfade* — an invented disease landscape that sits badly beside an engineered immunity system, and dilutes the one disease that matters.

### Group 4 — Plausible but unused. May be revived if a scene needs them.

These broke no rules; they were cut for economy, because a dozen taught words is the budget.

| term | meaning | when it might earn its place |
|---|---|---|
| **skyflash** | lightning | a storm scene, as a one-off in dialogue rather than a glossary entry |
| **windwall** | barrier built to break storms | Enclave perimeter description, if the cyclone season is dramatized |
| **stormskin** | heavy waterproof outerwear | if unsuited characters need rain gear on the mudwalk |
| **suncloak** | light heat-reflective garment | Teva's thermal problem, if she ever loses the suit |
| **leafsalt** | mineral seasoning from halophytic plants | a camp scene; good texture, costs one clause |
| **firevine** | red-stemmed plant used for cordage | already plausible against the flora list |
| **glowmoss**, **night-light** | bioluminescent moss / insect | redundant with **lightmold**, which is canon and better because it's engineered rather than found |
| **daycount** | calendar record | interesting against the biodegradable-record problem |
| **histbook** | preserved settler record | ditto — and it rhymes with the archival-fragment device |
| **meatbug** | large edible insect | redundant with **crawler** |
| **shadecloth**, **bandmesh**, **seedkit**, **cookpit**, **grubpot**, **carryplate**, **mudskin** | domestic objects | free if ever needed; none is worth teaching in advance |

The old file dated the colony at "~250 years" in its closing note. Canon is **206**.

## From `safety-suits.md` (research options never chosen)

The original file was a chat transcript that presented options without marking which were adopted. These were **not** adopted, and appear here so nobody mistakes them for spec:

- **Portable force fields / energy barriers** — outside the tech level and violates the hard-tech taboo.
- **Drone decoys** — ditto, and it would defuse most set-pieces.
- **Holographic projections** — ditto; also contradicts the no-holograms rule in `world-bible.md`.
- **Breath recycling / breathing regulators / atmospheric filtering** — cut under repair register R1; these implied sealed headgear.
- **White-noise generators to mask sound** — would neutralize the stealth problem that drives several scenes (rule 5).
- **Rigid graphene plate inserts at neck, inner thigh, wrist** — plausible, unused, and would need to be reconciled with "impossibly light." Revivable.
- **Cells engineered to enter a spore state under stress** — a good idea for suit dormancy or long storage; unused. Revivable.
- **Suit "dumping" heat rapidly through vents in emergencies** — revivable; would make a good beat if it also makes the wearer briefly visible to a thermally-sensitive predator.
