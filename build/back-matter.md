## About the Author

Daniel Hardman was born in Oshkosh, Wisconsin, and grew up along the shores of Lake Winnebago. As a teenager, he lived in Indiana and Kentucky, where he consumed a steady diet of Jules Verne, H. G. Wells, Edgar Rice Burroughs, Ray Bradbury, Arthur C. Clarke, Isaac Asimov, Lloyd Alexander, J. R. R. Tolkien, and C. S. Lewis.

He works as a software architect, designing digital identity solutions that improve privacy and security. He blogs about professional topics at [https://codecraft.co](https://codecraft.co). His scholarly and technical writings are published in various places, and archived at https://dhh1128.github.io/papers.

You may also enjoy Daniel’s other books, which are available on Kindle, Apple Books, Kobo, Barnes & Noble, Google Play, Audible, and so forth. Daniel also publishes albums of music (primarily as a lyricist, using AI to arrange and perform) on Spotify, iTunes, Youtube Music, and other music platforms using the performer names Harrow and Tolemis.

Learn more about Daniel's creative projects at [https://sivanea.com](https://sivanea.com).
